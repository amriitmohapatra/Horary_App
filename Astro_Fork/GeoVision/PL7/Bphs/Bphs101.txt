Chapter 11:

mother. 
 
32. Doubtlessly, the mother will give up the child if it  
has malefics in Konas counted from the decreasing Chandra.  
No benefic shall be yuti with the said malefics. 
 
43-45. Parents: Surya is the indicator of father for all  
beings, while the mother is indicated by Chandra. Should  
Surya receive a drishti from one or more malefics or be  
hemmed between them, this will cause misfortune to father. 
Similarly, Chandra is considered in respect of the mother. 
 
Malefics in the 6th, the 8th, or the 4th from Surya will  
bring inauspicious results about the father. 
 
Malefics in similar places from Chandra will be adverse  
for the mother. The strength, or otherwise, of the occupants  
concerned will be suitably estimated.

2. If any one of Buddha, Guru, and Shukra are in an angle  
from Lagna, all evils are destroyed as Surya eliminates  
darkness.  

Chapter 14:
 
1-2. Physical comforts: Should Lagna lord be yuti with a  
malefic or be in Randhra, 6th, or 12th, physical happiness  
will diminish. 
 
With a benefic in an angle or trine, all diseases will  
disappear. 
 
Lagna's angles (i.e. Bandhu, Yuvati, or the lOth) or its  
trine (Putra, Dharma) containing a benefic is a powerful  
remedy for all problems related to health. 
 
3. Physical health suffers if Lagna or Chandra are drishtied  
by or yuti with a malefic, and are devoid of a benefic's  
drishti. 
 
If Buddha, Guru, or Shukra are in 4th, 7th, or 10th from  
Lagna, or are in the company of Chandra in Lagna, the person  
will enjoy  a royal fortune.

Chapter 16:
 
1. O Brahmin, I shall now tell you about Sahaj Bhava. Should 
Sahaj Bhava be yuti with or drishtied by a benefic, the  
person will have a sibling and will be courageous. 
 
Happiness in this respect will come to pass if Mangal or  
Sahaj's lord is in an angle or in a trine or in exaltation,  
or in friendly divisions. 
 
In the same situation, Mangal will destroy both the sibling  
born before and born later. 
 
15. After estimating the strength and weakness of such yogas,  
the effects related to brothers and sisters should be  
announced. 
 
  

Chapter 17:
 
Should a benefic be in Sukha, drishti Sukha, or in yuti  
with the lord of Sukha , or a drishti on the lord of Sukha  
Bhava, then the person will be happy with conveyances and be  
free from accidents and dangers. 
 
A malefic replacing the said benefic will cause losses  
concerning vehicles and the person is involved in severe  
accidents. 
 
  

Chapter 18:
 
Should Putra's lord be in Ari, Randhra, or in Vyaya Bhava,  
there will be no offspring. 
 
4. If Putra's lord is in Ari Bhava as Lagna's lord is yuti  
with Mangal, the person will lose his very first child.  
Thereafter his spouse will not be fertile enough to yield  
another offspring. 
 
8. If Putra's lord is in Ari, Randhra, or Vyaya Bhava, or  
in an inimical rashi, or is debilitated, or in Putra itself,  
the person will have children with difficulty. 
 
If Putra Bhava has a malefic in it or if Shani is in the 5th  
from Guru the person will beget offspring only through his  
second or third wife. 

Chapter 19:
 
3-5. Relatives Affected: The Karaka of a relative or the  
lord of such a bhava joining Ari's lord, or being in Ari,  
or Randhra Bhava, indicates ulcers/bruises to a relative  
such as a person's father. Surya with such lordship and in  
such a bhava denotes such affecting of head; Chandra denotes  
such affecting of the face; Mangal denotes such affecting of  
the neck; Buddha denotes such affecting of the navel; Guru  
denotes such affecting of the nose; Shukra denotes such  
affecting of the eyes; Shani denotes such affecting of the  
feet; and Rahu and Ketu denotes such affecting 
abdomen. 

Chapter 20:
 
If Shukra is yuti with a malefic in any bhava, this will  
cause loss of wife. 
 
If Yuvati Bhava is occupied or owned by Shani/Mangal, the  
person will beget a harlot as his spouse or he will have  
other illegal liasons. 
 
Should Shukra be in a Navamsha of Mangal, or in a Rashi of  
Mangal, or receive a drishti from or be yuti with Mangal,  
will have extremely intimate relations with a female. 
 
If Shukra is so related to Shani, the person will have  
extremely intimate relations with a male. 

Chapter 21:
 
If the lords of Putra, Randhra, and Tanu Bhava are in their  
own Navamshas, or their own Rashis or in friendly Rashis,  
the person will enjoy a long span of life. 

Chapter 23:
 
3. If Karma's lord is devoid of strength, the person will  
face obstructions in his work. 

Chapter 25:
 
9. If Rahu is in Vyaya along with Mangal, Shani, and Surya,  
the person will go to hell. Similar effects will occur if  
Vyaya's lord is with Surya. 

Chapter 26:
 
3. If Lagna's lord is in Sahaj Bhava, the person will equal  
a lion in valor and  will be endowed with all kinds of  
wealth. He will be honorable and will have two wives. He  
will be intelligent and happy. 
 
15. If Dhana's lord is in Sahaj Bhava, the person will be  
valorous, wise, virtuous, lustful, and miserly; all these  
are true when related to a benefic. If related to a malefic,  
the person will be a heterodox. 
 
28. If Sahaj's lord is in Bandhu Bhava, the person will be  
happy, wealthy and intelligent, but will acquire a wicked  
spouse. 
 
42. If Bandhu's lord is in Ari Bhava, the person will be  
devoid of maternal happiness and be given to anger. He will  
be a thief and a conjurer (or magician). He will be  
independent in action and be indisposed. 
 
54. If Putra's lord is in Ari Bhava, the person will obtain  
such sons who will be equal to his enemies,or will lose  
them, or will acquire an adopted or purchased son. 
 
64. If Ari's lord is in Bandhu Bhava, the person will be  
devoid of maternal happiness and will be intelligent,  
jealous, evil-minded, and very rich. He will be a tale  
bearer. 
 
75. If Yuvati's lord is in Sahaj Bhava, the person will  
suffer loss of children. Sometimes, with great difficulty,  
a son may survive. There is also the possibility of a  
daughter surviving. 
 
87. If Randhra's lord is in Sahaj Bhava, the person will be  
devoid of fraternal happiness. He will be indolent and  
devoid of servants and strength.  
 
98. If Dharma's lord is in Dhana Bhava, the person will be a  
scholar and will be dear to all. He will be wealthy,  
sensuous and endowed with happiness from wife, sons, etc. 
 
117. If Karma's lord is in Dharma Bhava, the person born of  
royal scion will become a king, whereas an ordinary person  
will be equal to a king. This placement will confer wealth  
and the happiness of progeny. 
 
122. If Labha's lord is in Dhana Bhava, the person will be  
endowed with all kinds of wealth and all kinds of accomplish- 
ments. He will be charitable, religious, and always happy. 
 
134. If Vyaya's lord is in Dhana Bhava, the person will  
always spend on inauspicious deeds. He will be religious,  
will speak sweetly, and will be endowed with virtue and  
happiness. 

Chapter 27:
 
7. If Dhum is in Ari Bhava, the person will be strong and  
will conquer his enemies. He will be very brilliant, famous  
and free from diseases. 
 
20. If Vyatipat is in Yuvati Bhava, the person will be  
bereft of wealth, wife and sons. He will be subdued to  
females. He will be miserable, sensuous, shameless, and  
friendly to others. 
 
26. Effects of Paridhi (or Parivesh) in various Bhavas:  
(up to sloka 37): If Paridhi is in Tanu Bhava, the person  
will be learned, truthful, peaceful and rich. He will be  
endowed with sons. He will be pure, charitable and dear to  
elders. 
 
49. If Chaap is in Vyaya Bhava, the person will be wicked,  
very honorable but, evil in disposition. He will be  
shameless, and will visit other's females, and always be  
poor. 
 
50. Effects of Dhwaja (or Sikhi, or Upaketu) in various  
Bhavas (up to sloka 61): If Dhwaja is in Tanu Bhava, the  
person will be skilful in all branches of learning and will  
be happy. He will be efficient in speech, agreeable, and be  
very affectionate. 
 
69. If Gulik is in Randhra Bhava, the person will be  
troubled by hunger and be miserable. He will be cruel and  
very short-tempered. He will be very unkind, poor, and  
bereft of good qualities. 
 
77. If Pranapad is in Bandhu Bhava, the person will be  
happy, friendly and attached to females and elders. He  
will be soft and truthful. 

Chapter 31:
 
8-11. Pada and Finance: (up to sloka 15) O Brahmin, I shall  
now tell you of some effects of grahas based on Pada. If  
the 11th from Lagna Pada is occupied or receives a drishti  
from a graha, the person will be happy and rich; 
 
wealth will come through various means, if a benefic is  
related as above. 
 
A malefic will confer wealth through questionable means. 
 
If there is both a benefic and a malefic, it will be  
through both means. 
 
16-17. Pada and Financial Losses: (up to sloka 21): If the  
12th from Lagna Pada receives a drishti from or is yuti with  
both benefics and malefics, there will be abundant earnings, 
 but plenty of expenses. The benefic will cause earning  
through fair means; malefic will cause through unfair means  
and mixed grahas through both fair and unfair means. 
 
23. The 7th Bhava from Pada (up to sloka 27): If Rahu or  
Ketu is placed in the 7th from Lagna Pada, the person will  
be troubled by disorders of the stomach, or by fire. 

Chapter 32:
 
O excellent of the Brahmins, if Upa Pada is yuti with or  
receives a drishti from a benefic graha, a person will  
obtain full happiness from progeny and spouse. 
 
If the said occupant is in its exaltation rashi, or  
Navamsha or receives a drishti from another graha, there  
will be many charming and virtuous wives. 
 
Chandra so placed will give a son, 
 
32. If Shukra is in the 3rd or the 11th from Lagna Pada,  
there would have been an abortion to the mother earlier.  
Same is the effect if Shukra is in the 8th from natal Lagna,  
or from Lagna Pada. 
 
38. If Rahu is in the 7th or the 12th from Lagna Pada or  
gives a drishti to one of the said bhavas, the person will  
be endowed with spiritual knowledge and be very fortunate. 

Chapter 35:
 
Should Atma Karaka be in Mithun Navamsha, the person will be  
afflicted by itching etc.  
 
while that of a malefic will cause no good. 
 
Otherwise, the contrary will prevail (i.e. the person will  
not visit others' wives). 
 
32. Effects of the 3rd from Karakamsha: A malefic in the 3rd  
from Karakamsha will make a person valorous, 
 
Rahu in the 5th from Karakamsha denotes a machinist. 
 
Chandra in Karakamsha or in the 5th from there denotes a  
follower of Sankhya philosophy (of Maharishi Kapila who  
enumerated 25 true principles with emphasis on final bliss),  
and indicates that a person is versed in rhetorics and  
singing, 
 
and Ketu or Rahu in Karakamsha or in the 5th from there  
denotes that  a person is a Jyotishi. 
 
46. Effects of the 6th from Karakamsha: If the 6th from  
Karakamsha is occupied by a malefic, the person will be an  
agriculturist 
 
49. Effects of the 8th from Karakamsha: If a benefic or the  
graha owning the 8th from Karakamsha happens to be in the  
8th from Karakamsha, the person will be long-lived, 
 
while a malefic placed in the 8th from Karakamsha will  
reduce the life span. 
 
If a malefic is in the 11th from Karakamsha, the person  
will gain by questionable means, be famous and valorous. 
 
If the 12th from Karakamsha is vacant, then also good effects  
(in respect of expenses) will follow.  
 
Rahu, Surya, and Mangal respectively in these places denote  
a machinist, a knife user, and a spear or arrow user. 
 
while Chandra in Karakamsha or the 5th from there denotes a  
Sankhya Yogi, a rhetoric, or a singer. 

Chapter 36:
 
11-12. Lordships of Kendras and Konaas: If there is an  
exchange between a lord of a Kendra and a lord of a Kona, or  
if a lord of a Kendra is yuti with a lord of a Kona in a  
Kendra or in a Kona, or if a lord of a Kona is in a Kendra  
or vice versa, or if there happens to be a full drishti  
between a lord of a Kendra and a lord of a Kona, they cause  
a Yoga.  A person born in such a Yoga will become a king and  
be famous. 
 
13. If one and the same graha gets the lordships of a Kona  
as well as a Kendra, or if a graha is in a Kendra or in a  
Kona, it will specially prove a Yoga Karaka. 
 
14. Lordship of Kendra: It has been said that a malefic  
owning a Kendra will become auspicious, which is true only  
when it simultaneously lords over a Kona and not by merely  
owning a Kendra. 
 
15. If the lords of a Kendra or a Kona simultaneously own  
an evil bhava, he (i.e. the lord of the Kendra or the lord  
of the Kona) does not cause a Raj Yoga by mere stipulation  
of relations (as per ch. 34, slokas 11 and 12). 
 
16. Rahu and Ketu: Rahu and Ketu predominantly give the  
effects as due to their yuti with a bhava lord or as due  
to the bhava they occupy. 
 
33-34. Grahas and Tula Lagna: Guru, Surya, and Mangal are  
malefics. Shani and Buddha are auspicious. Chandra and Buddha  
will cause Raj Yoga. Mangal is a killer. Guru and other  
malefics will also acquire a disposition to inflict death.  
Shukra is neutral. 

Chapter 37:
 
if all grahas are in 5 rashis, Paasha Yoga is formed, 
 
45. Paash Yoga: A person born in Paash Yoga will be liable  
to be imprisoned. He will be skilful in work and will be  
deceiving in disposition. He will talk much, be bereft of  
good qualities and will have many servants. 

Chapter 38:
 
7-8. Parvata Yoga: Benefics in Kendras will produce Parvata  
Yoga, as Yuvati and Randhra Bhava are vacant or are occupied  
by benefics only. A person born in Parvata Yoga will be  
wealthy, eloquent and charitable. He will be learned in the   
Shastras. He will be fond of mirth and will be famous. He  
will be splendorous, and be the leader of a city.  

Chapter 40:
 
1. Vesi, Vosi, and Abhayachari Yogas: Barring Chandra, if a  
graha among Mangal etc. is in the 2nd from Surya, Vesi Yoga  
is formed; 
 
2-3. Effects of Vesi, Vosi, and Ubhayachari Yogas: A person  
born in Vesi Yoga will be even sighted, truthful and long  
bodied. He will be indolent, happy, and endowed with negligible  
wealth.  
 
4. Benefics causing Vesi, Vosi, or Ubhayachari Yogas will  
give the above mentioned effects, while malefics will  
produce contrary effects.

Chapter 45:

64. If Shani or Lagna's lord is yuti with any exalted graha,

65. Long life will be enjoyed if malefics are in Sahaj, Ari,

Chapter 47:
 
If Surya is in Upavesana Avastha, the person will suffer  
poverty, will carry loads, will indulge in litigation, be  
hard-hearted, wicked, and will lose in his undertakings. 
 
 
Surya in Upavesana Avastha will make a person an  
artisan, black in complexion, devoid of learning and  
miserable. The person will serve others. 
 
 
Should Chandra be in Prakasha Avastha, the person will be  
famous in the world and he will have his virtues exposed  
through royal patronage; he will be surrounded by horses,  
elephants, females, and ornaments; he will visit shrines. 
 
 
If Mangal is in Kautuka Avastha the person will be curious  
in disposition and will be endowed with friends and sons; 
 
If Shukra is in Kautuka Avastha, the person will be equal to  
Lord Indra. He will attain greatness in the assembly; he  
will be learned and will always have Lakshmi dwelling in his  
abode. 
 
111-122. Effects of Shani's Avasthas at Birth: If Shani is  
in Sayana Avastha, the person will be troubled by hunger and 
thirst; he will incur diseases in boyhood; later on, he will 
become wealthy. 
 
If Rahu is in Upavesana Avastha the person will be distressed  
due to ulcers; he will be endowed with royal association; he  
will be highly honorable but devoid of financial happiness. 
 
while if Ketu is in Sayana Avastha in other rashis (i.e.  
other rashis than Mesha, Vrishabha, Mithun, or Kanya),  
increased illness will follow. 
 
150. Undoubtedlt, auspicious effects follow the location of  
a malefic in Putra Bhava, but only with Nidra Avastha or in  
Sayana Avastha.
