dD Thithi (New Moon and Full Moon Days)

In Kerala New Moon day is held precious and

(III) Kaalasarpa Dosha

Even this is not properly understood by many. If all
planets are hemmed in between Rahu and Ketiu in any
order they seem to opine the existence of Kaalasarpa
Dogha. This is not always correct. You know that Rahu is
a Cobra with poison in the mouth while Ketu a Scorpion
has it in his tail. The movements of these being anti-clock-
wise, planets in-between the mouth of Rahu and the tail
of Ketu only get hurt. It is here that Kaalasarpa Dosha
exists and not when they are between the head of Ketu
and trunk of Rahu for the elementary reason that

' scorpion cannot hurt by its mouth and cobra by its tail.
To be more clear if all the planets are from Ketu to Rahu
in clockwise order the Dosha is formed and not when in
the space of anti-clockwise order. If a planet be with
Rahu or Ketu in the same sign then unless by degree
position he falls inside the above clockwise direction this
Dosha will not be formed.
