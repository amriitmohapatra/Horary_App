## Transit Results (Lecture at Calcutta)


Today the subject chosen isTransit and its Results. This is one of the methods to find out what a person can expect due to the movement of the planets in various signs as days pass on. General predictions offered in dailies, weeklies and monthlies are based on either of the two systems Hindu orWestern.

Hindus take the position of Moon in Nirayana chart at the time of the birth of a person whereas Westerners take the position of Sun at one's birth time. Hindus find out in which sign a planet transits and how many signs away it is, when counted from the

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=206|p. 206]]

Sign occupied by Moon. They do not take into consideration whether Moon is in the 1st degree or in the middle or in the end of the sign; so also a planet transiting in a sign may be in the beginning or in the middle or in the end. The actual longitudinal distance is never calculated and included for prediction. Suppose Moon is in the first degree of the sign Mesha and if a planet is in the beginning or in the middle or in the end in Rishaba it is always said to be in the 2nd sign to Moon sign and hence in 2 to Moon.

The longitudinal distance will range between 30° and 60°. If Moon were to be over 29° in Aries and a planet just leaves Aries and enters into Taurus it is said to be in the second sign till it leaves Taurus. Therefore the longitudinal distance between the two will be 1° to 30°. Does it sound alright to call a planet in 2 to Moon when the longitudinal distance varies between 1° and 60°. When the distance is only 1° are they not in conjunction? Only in the map we draw for a horoscope, we draw lines to separate each sign. In the heavens is there any partition wall? Will not both the planets appear very close to each other? If the declination is the same then Moon alone can be seen and the planet will be hidden. When actually a planet is in rapt conjunction to say that it is in the second house to Moon and also to call a planet in 29° Aries, when Moon is in the first degree of Aries, as conjoined with Moon in Janma Rasi, does not appeal alright.

Secondly, if a person has a horoscope let him take it. Note the lagna. On that day there were some births. Suppose Moon on that day was in Cancer. Then, think awhile. Does the position of Moon to all the births on that day denote the same result to all. Or whom does Moon indicate? For Aries lagna born, Moon is lord of 4 in 4; So it denotes mother; to Taurus - born it indicates younger brother and sister, to Cancer lagna born, the person himself; to Virgo born, elder brothers, sisters, friends, younger paternal uncle, son-in-law, daughter-in-law etc., to Scorpio born, Moon denotes father; to Capricorn borns Moon indicates wife; to Pisces borns, children, etc., When such is the case, if the position of transiting planets is favourable will the person enjoy or such a relative as is indicated by Moon according to one's lagna enjoy? which appeals to us? Which comes correct. Therefore how to judge, is to be solved.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=207|p. 207]]

Sun is taken by Westerners. Similarly judge whom Sun indicates. Suppose Sun is in Leo. to Aries borns Sun indicates children; to Taurus - borns mother; To Gemini - borns younger brother, etc., Therefore good aspects to Sun will bestow desirable results to such a relative as is shown by Sun by occupation and ownership and predictions offered taking the position of sun, excluding lagna is also not correct.

But when one has no horoscope, then to offer some mental solace, there should be some base, some method. So Hindus take Moon; Westerners take Sun, That is why the general predictions which are published taking either of the luminaries have to fail and they fail in majority cases. Not knowing the truth one may attribute the difference to one's will. As lagna indicates one's health, longevity success or failure etc., one must take only the lagna, then the position of all planets and the l2 cusps.

Requisites: Place of birth, actual time of birth, date etc., Krishman's Ephemeris for the year of birth and Table of Houses. Erect the chart using these. Deduct Krishnamurti Ayanamsa for that year. Mark all the twelve cusps; also all the planets and fortuna.

Next write down First house; Second House; Third house and so on till Twelfth house, one below the other.

Always take that a house extends from that cusp to the next cusp (exact degree and minute). Do not mix up - Note down in which house each planet is.
