# Life with Wife - Root of Fruit

Though it may appear to be funny, nowadays, I am doing experiments upon such people who are not having children for the past 4 years or 5 years or 6 years after the marriage by giving them the most auspicious time to be friendly with their wives so that they may have at least one child. The time selected was when Moon was transiting in such a star, the Lord of which is the significators of 2, 5 and 11 and not at all connected with 1,4 and 10 and the sub selected also should be governed by the significators of 2, 5 and 11 and sub transited by the Ascendant is ruled by the significators of 2,5 and 11. This is a case wherein I want to prove that a lady who cannot conceive for years, becomes the mother, in due course. One also should note that the Dasa and Bhukthi should be governed by the significators of the houses 2,5 and 11 and not connected with 1,4 and 10. After I come out successful in at least a dozen cases, I shall write articles in my Magazine, how the auspicious moment was suggested only in such cases where they were destined to become the father. But if a person has no chances to have any child according to the Karma done by him, then I will also find out from them whether they adhered to my principle or that particular time could not be made the best use of. All the facts will be published in 1972, after noting down whether there was abortion or miscarriage or normal child birth or failure in the attempt. Such a research done is really useful than to select auspicious time using the Traditional method.
