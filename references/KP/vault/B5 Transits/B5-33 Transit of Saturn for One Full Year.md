# Transit of Saturn for One Full Year

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=312|p. 312]]

Let us take the year '1964'

On 26th January, Saturn passed on to Nirayana Aquarius and up to 23rd March, it moves in direct motion in the Nakshathra Dhanishta; then it transits in the same direction in Sathabhisha star ruled by Rahu the Ascending node till 15th June 1964. On 15-6-1964 it commences to retrograde, and till 17th September 1964, it is in Sathabhisha, from 18th September Saturn by retrograde motion, transits in Dhanishta, governed by Mars in the same sign Aquarius - Kumbha. On 2nd November it takes a direct course, and throughout the year, it is in Dhanishta.

One should note that Saturn is not the only Planet which indicates what a person can expect in 1964. Every planet contributes to certain events in one's life according to (Hindu) System (1) its lordship, (2) the position which it occupied at the time of one's birth, (3) the houses owned by the planet (4) the house transited by the planet, (5) (Western System) the various aspects it forms with the other planets with its radical position, with the Ascendant, with the meridian and also its conjunction with the planets in the sign in which it transits and (6) (K.P) the nakshathra or star in which the planet transits, and the house signified by the lord of the nakshathra and Sub. All the points are very important, according to the system followed. But one should follow only K.P.System. Both the nature of event and the time of event will be correct only when properly judged, using K.P System.

**Western:** - It may be said that good aspect from planet to any other planet may get cancelled by the adverse aspect to another planet. It is not correct. Both the results will be enjoyed and experienced. If Saturn as lord of 11 forms good aspect with Sun, lord of 5, and bad aspect with Venus, lord of 2 and 7, latter adverse aspect will not rob away the former beneficial aspect. Actually, due to the favourable aspect to Sun, lord of 5, he may gain in races, speculation, or betting, or be benefitted by children, and because of the adverse aspect with Venus, Saturn may indicate ailment to wife some worries at home and also uncommon expenses. The day will not pass, without all these events; one will not cancel the other. It may be that, whatever he gained he had to spend away. But he ought to have felt happy for the success in speculation of gain through children and miserable, due to wife's ill-health, and in his ledger he should make both the entries, the first is the income through speculation or children and also the expenses of the medical bill, etc., due to the adverse aspect.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=313|p. 313]]

So, also if a planet forms good aspect with any other planet which also receives evil aspect from a third planet, then also both the results will be enjoyed by the person.

Thus when an individual's chart is taken, and the transit results are offered; by considering all these vital points, the prediction will come nearly satisfactory and be to some extent helpful : but for a general prediction all these points cannot be included, as either lagna or Moon's or Sun's position alone is taken, that is the main reason why the transit results, given in general do not come to pass.
