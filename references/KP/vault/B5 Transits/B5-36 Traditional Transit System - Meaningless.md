# Traditional Transit System - Meaningless

Most of the traditional astrologers are not aware that the Gochara Transit System advocated by various sages, saints and scholars in different text books are useless, meaningless and not universally applicable.

Present authors reproducing those dicta in their own words and publishing in different languages, actually do harm to those who keep their horoscopes side by side with such books without mentioning that these rules fail miserably in majority of the individuals.

According to Prof. Krishnamurti, only those who suffer due to Grahachara (bad time) will follow this Gochara.

Of all the lagnas according to Tradition, it is only the Moon lagna that is most important for ascertaining the Gocharapala. One ought to calculate and from the Moon's place predict the effects due to transits of planets through the signs counted from that of the Moon, sign by sign.

During transit, the Sun gives good results when he is in 3 or 6 or 10 or 11 counted from Moon; the Moon in 1, 3, 6, 7, 10, 11 Jupiter in 2, 5, 7, 9 and 11; Mars and Saturn 3, 6, 11 and so on.

Then they mention about Vedha. (Refer to previous chapter).

Let me give the Gochara position of planets at the time of one's marriage. So also study the horoscope of people at the time of their appointment, at the time when one receives President award, at the time when one wins in races, one gains in lottery etc. as per traditional system and KP you yourself will find the truth. Let us ignore those who continue to give as per tradition even though they know that it is useless and meaningless or they continue to give lame excuses for every failure. They have to live, they know only this system. Anyhow, they are committing sin.
