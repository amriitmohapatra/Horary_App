# Extract of Lecture on the Constellation and Sub Theory

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=222|p. 222]]

Friends,

All of you who have gathered here are, no doubt, my students since long number of years and also those who are very highly qualified and supremely intelligent, so as to quickly grasp, apply and with open mind appreciate the role of the Sub. When you all ask me to speak on this subject especially to know how it struck me, I would like to tell you my early part of life when this method came to my mind.

In 1939, I found that the Constellation Theory is correct where as the predictions offered taking the sign and the house alone failed miserably; brought ridicule to Traditional Astrology.

One of the horoscope which clearly indicated the above is as follows ([[#^b5-f006|Fig. B5.6]]):

His father died in 1917 January, leaving behind good business and decent property acquired during Kethu and Venus dasa.

But the native lost everything during his Sun, Moon and Mars Dasa. During Rahu Dasa, just before leaving India he consulted me whether he could prosper at least in Rahu Dasa. I encouraged him. He went at the end of June 1939. Thereafter I don't know what happened to him and to his family.

My finding was that Kethu and Venus were in the Constellation of planet in 11. So they gave gains, even though they were in 12 threatening loss as per the misleading traditional method.

Sun, Moon were in the Constellation of planets in 12. So, they gave loss. Constellation or Stellar System is correct; so useful.

I am sure he would have prospered during Rahu dasa till the end of his life in Rahu dasa as Rahu was in the Constellation of Mercury in 11. (Though Bhadhakasthana to Libra Lagna born.)

Similarly, I verified series of charts, noted down their results, did postmortem and discovered Krishnamurti Padhdhati by sub-dividing the Constellation into Subs, as I could fix an event within a range of 13 days, instead of exact date. There was also another handicap.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=224|p. 224]]

If one runs Mars dasa Kethu Bhukthi, I used to predict that a matter will materialise between 13th April and 26th April as in that fortnight Sun will pass in Nirayana Mesha owned by Mars and Kethu Star Aswini.

If it were to be Sun dasa Kethu Bhukthi, I predicted in the second fortnight of August as Sun would be in Sun sign, Kethu star Makam. If it were to be Jupiter dasa Kethu Bhukthi; then the matter will materialise in the second fortnight of December as Sun would be in Jupiter Sign Kethu star.

But for Kethu Bhukties in other dasas, I could not give, even this range.

If one had been running Mars dasa Venus Bhukthi, I pitched up the days when Sun was transiting in Mars Sign, Venus Star; (Aries) or Venus sign (Libra) Mars star. Thus when I proceeded, I found that in Mars sign, there is no Moon, Rahu, Mars Stars; in Venus Sign, no Saturn, Mercury Kethu Venus Stars. Hence to dasas, many Bhukties had no constellation.

Then it struck me that as long as Stellar theory is correct, instead of taking Sign, take the Star to represent dasa, and the sub to indicate Bhukthi. Now every period and subperiod has 3 positions in the Zodiac at a distance of 120° from one another.

When I had been doing research on the Constellation Theory, first I thought that if a person were to gain during the period of a particular planet then the day ruled by it, three Constellations governed by it, the signs owned by it must prove to be favourable, and also advantageous. So I started to note whether the above will come true. Prior to 1932, I had gains during the conjoined periods of Venus, and Rahu, even though the Dasa lord was favourable or not. Even when A Dasa B Bhukthi was operating the combination of Venus and Rahu Anthras and Sookshmas prove to be good. So I thought I could note down in my diary for one full year all the Fridays and also the days when Moon was transiting either in Venus star or in Rahu star. On those days only I played cards and before 1939, I have found that during some such days I gained and during some such days I lost. No doubt those days were either Friday or Wednesday indicated by Rahu in my horoscope (Rahu was in Gemini) or I played on Bharani, Poorvaphalguni or Poorvashada star days or during Arudhra, Swathi and Satabhisha star days. Swathi proved to be much better than any. I thought it would be due to Rahu Star in Venus sign.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=225|p. 225]]

In 1939 I came to the conclusion that whenever I played the cards on the star days governed by Venus when Moon was in the 2nd pada, I was coming out successful. In Rahu's stars days when Moon was in the 3rd pada I had won. Then it came to my mind why I should not divide a Constellation into 9 Subs. I thought a child born on a day when Arudhra is transited by Moon then if the time of the birth were to be within four hours from the commencement of Moon's entry into Arudhra the child will be having Rahu Dasa, Rahu Bhukthi at the time of the birth. Then for a few hours when Moon is further Progressing in that star the sub is ruled by Guru. Next few hours Moon will be in Saturn sub i.e., a child born at this position in that Constellation will have Rahu Dasa Saturn Bhukthi at the time of the birth; A few hours later if the child is born it will have Rahu Dasa, Mercury Bhukthi at the moment of the birth. Thus on an average, the 24 hours duration of Arudhra star sub divided among the 9 planets commencing from Rahu in the cyclic order of Vimshottari Dasa. There after from 1939. I thought in Rahu star days the sub of Venus will be auspicious and similarly in Venus stars days sub of Rahu will be for victory. This I wanted to test and within three months I found that my finding was very correct. Every day I had been winning. So I had instructed one of my children as follows:-

If supposing I expect that I would win between 6 and 8-30 P.M., I used to ask one to come to the club where I used to play and simply call me saying that somebody has come for consultation. I go to the Club some 10 minutes earlier so that to start with I go on losing. Then when exactly Moon enters into Rahu Sub I start winning. When I know that I would lose, as per instruction any of my children will come and call me in the last 2, 3 minutes. As soon as I was called I purposely began to lose so that in the beginning and in the end I lose for a few minutes whereas for hours I win. Later I went to races simply to note how far it works and there also I found that I lost during the time when other Subs operated whereas invariably, I had been gaining whenever I bet when the time was auspicious as Moon would be transiting in either Rahu Sub or Venus Sub. In playing cards since it is very frequent I could not further develop my knowledge but in the races I further found that it is not enough if one bets and gains during the beneficial star day and in the favourable Sub. Even though the ultimate result is gain, yet we lost in a few, we gained in a few. Why? Then I found that even though the Sub period runs for 4 hours unless the Ascendant rising in the East also transits in such a favourable Constellation or sub at the time when the horse passes the winning post; even in such a period there was failure. Therefore, one must note down which group of planets will offer success and victory over enemies or gaining in races or winning in competition. Then one should also select the position in the Zodiac governed by such group of planets. Whichever planet transits those point, then success is assured. Prediction comes out correct. Thus I did research till 1951 when I started a Astrological Researach Institute. In a few months, during my lecture. I narrated the above and coached them up in this method. (Thereafter they never came to me to play cards). This is the way how I found out the use of the Sub and thereafter developed it.
