# Moon in the Eighth House

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=269|p. 269]]

Moon is generally mentioned as a planet in Astrology. But; according to the definition given in the dictionary it is not; it is one of the luminaries. It is the nearest to the earth and it alone revolves round and round the earth. Daily it moves around 13 degrees in the Zodiac.

It is a feminine and a changeable planet. Its effect is modified by the lord of the star and sub occupied by it, by the planets conjoined with it and also aspecting it.

Moon is termed as "Mathi", meaning "Mind". There are in all, 12 planets. But astrologers have found it possible to predict the results of Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Kethu, Uranus and Neptune only, the results of Pluto are still being studied and it is in an experimental stage. Hence, leaving Moon and Pluto the other ten planets may occupy any position in the horoscope. In few cases Moon may be in evil constellation and sub. Those who afflict Moon, will cause trouble through the source indicated by them: i.e, as Moon governs the mind, a person will be worried with such matters, as are indicated by the lord of the star or sub or those with whom Moon is conjoined or by whom Moon is aspected by. No man in the world has peace of mind, as Moon becomes evil by any of the above connections.

Eighth house, according to Ramadayal, author of Sanketa-Nidhi, is the house of mystery and according to us, of misery also. It shows the fear and danger from enemies, danger caused by diseases, death, imprisonment and the like, crossing a river, theft, robbery, siege of a fort, fighting, wrong action etc.

Kalidasa in Uthra Kalamritha says that the following matters are to be investigated from the 8th house:

Longevity, happiness or sorrow, defeat, bonus, money of the deceased, affliction of the face (as this house opposes the second house indicating face), urinary disease (8th house shows bladder and genital organs), calamity, brother's enemy (as it is the 6th house to the third), teasing a wife's tuft of hair, enemy's fort, laziness, punishment from Government. (10th house shows Government 8th house is the 11th to the 10th; Hence, the fine paid by the native is the gain to the Government), fear, loss of money (only when evil planets occupy), giving a debt (when a benefic occupies the 8th house), other's money received without knowledge, permanent possession, wickedness, sin, murder of a living person, amputation, hearing bad news, chain of unlucky events, war, agony, etc.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=270|p. 270]]

The kind of death, wills, legacies, gratuity, provident fund, insurance business, money through partner and through wife are also judged from the 8th house.

It also indicates disease of the younger brother, speculative gains to mother, landed property and conveyance of children, enemy's younger brother, wife's movable property, her vision, danger to her life, father's life in a foreign place, or separation, honour to elder brother, or friends.

To a nation, it shows the conclusion of a peace treaty, public morality, deficit budget, as regards exports and imports, estate duty, encroachment by others, loss of territory, loss of the leader, national grief, calamities, earthquake, volcano, floods, fire, famine, infection, disease, causing much mortality, serious accident, etc.

Moon as lord of lagna in the 8th house, Aquarius, Kumba in Dhanishta star actually causes Raja yoga, and offers most brilliant results, when one in an infant stage, running Mars dasa and the sub of Moon. Later in every Moon sub-period he will have a rise in his career. Moon in Sathabisha causes worry and anxiety, unless Rahu, at the time of birth, is the significator of favourable houses. Moon in Poorvabhadrapada star gives success after much struggle; it causes anxious ill health; it increases enemies; but one can win in election or competition. Partner may lose money. Otherwise, wife may cause worry due to her bad luck, or hospitalisation.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=271|p. 271]]

Moon, as significators of 2 in 8, in Uthrashada star, gives loss through brothers or while making short journey, or by editing books, or by Government officials. One may be picked especially in a public, Government office, bus stand, railway station, etc. Moon in Sravanam star gives wealth and not health. Moon in Dhanishta, governed by Mars, shows that one will have litigation and will be lucky to have grand success. One may suffer from any chronic disease during other sub-periods. Whereas Moon gives cure in its period, as it is in the constellation of lord of 11, Mars.

Moon, as lord of 3 in 8, causes anxiety and worry, if it would have been in Moolam star. But, one can make money. If it was in Poorvashada. He may become a partner and give finance to run a show with satisfactory bank position. Moon in Uthrashada star indicates sale of landed property and reduction in the permanent holdings. Moon causes troubles through vehicles, mother, etc. A few will change their residence to avoid the persistent trouble given by the house owner if Moon was in Uthrashada star.

Moon, as lord of 4 in 8 in Visakam star, gives success in higher education, long journey and life in a far-off place, after experiencing lot of troubles. Separation from family, hospitalisation of father and sale of property are also indicated. Moon in Anuradha star indicates that one can have through legacy or inheritance, landed properties and one may serve in insurance or chemical engineering industry or in mines. It indicates enjoyment of life in decent quarters, erected by the Government, and also purchase of property at competitive prices through the influence of friends. Moon, in the constellation of Jyesta ruled by Mercury, threatens, enmity, disease, frequent change of residence, debts, purchase of property through any society or by raising loan. So also, one may buy a car under hire-purchase system.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=272|p. 272]]

Moon, as lord of 5 in 8 in Chithrai star ruled by Mars, indicates that the native will lose money in speculation or by producing pictures, in cinema drama or by betting. It also shows ailment to brother or misunderstanding with him. Moon as lord of 5 in 8 in Swathi, threatens that one may divorce the partner who will never cooperate; or the partner may be ill. At least it gives separation for sometime due to lack of understanding and adjustment. Moon in Vishakam star in 8 gives success in speculation, unexpected gains by betting, lottery, etc. One may serve in a maternity hospital or stock exchange or race club. One will become very popular and also rich, if the malefic planets do not afflict Jupiter, by forming adverse aspect.

Moon, as lord of 6 in 8 is most advantageous, only when it is in Hastham star. But, if it were to be in Uthraphalguni, the native will incur debts, suffer from abdominal trouble, indigestion, loose bowels, etc. Health will fail; bank position will deteriorate, enmity will increase. Moon in Hastham causes loss to the opponent and gain to the native. Health may fail, but the native will not lose money in his transactions. There will be law suits, but he will invariably win; may cause gastric ulcer. Health of brother also will fail and both cannot have good understanding. Separation and partition are threatened.

Moon, lord of 7 in 8 in Makam star, denies happy domestic life, especially in adult age, when one is to really enjoy peace of mind and be happy. It is dangerous to his partner's longevity. She may have fear complex or hysteria. Partner in business will be very wise and he may silently make up larger profits without the knowledge of the native. Moon, lord of 7 in Pooram star in 8, makes one religious and forces him to speculate; invariably, he will lose. Venus rules Poorvaphalguni. It is lord of 5 and 10; its star is occupied by the lord of the 7th house opponent - competitor - and Venus is lord of 11 to the 7th house. Moon, lord of 7 is in 2 to the 7th house. Hence, the competitor wins. The native will speculate as Venus is lord of 5. Moon in Poorvaphalguni gives opportunity for one to enter into cinema line or share broker's office or to take up music or deal in scents, petrol or milk. Moon in Uthraphalguni in eight causes ailment to partner, but much gain to him with minimum effort. Rather he will be a partner assisting with finance alone, whereas the native is to work hard, earn; give away major part to the financier.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=273|p. 273]]

Moon, in 8 in Cancer in Punarvasu star, shows disposal of landed property, hatred towards close relatives and loss of comfort etc. One may sell away the conveyance. To his mother, Moon offers most desirable results. She may have popularity. Moon in Pushyam is very bad, as the person will lose money, and have many enemies; he will be a pessimist. There may be fear complex, inferiority complex and failure due to his own laziness, reluctance, silence and negligence. Annoying letters and depressing messages will be received. He may incur debts due to business in petrol or mine products. Moon in his own sign, the 8th house in Aslesha star promises fortune, money through partner, house, property, etc. through wife or husband, as also vehicle through them and promotion in one's profession. It offers advancement in one's career, inclusion of a Mercurian to expand his business, name, fame and reputation.

Moon in 8 owning the 9th house indicates that father will be fortunate in a place other than his native place; father will have mixed results by speculation, etc., if Moon was in Mrigasirisha star owned by Mars. He may purchase a property at a price higher that the market rate. In the bargain he will lose. Moon in Arudhra indicates danger to father, failure in higher studies, unnecessary attempts to go overseas, etc. Moon, as lord of 9 in 12 to the 9th house in Rahu's star Arudhra, is definitely evil if Rahu would have been in the 3rd or the 10th house. Moon in Punarvasu star gives satisfactory bank position, gain of money, success in pleasurable pursuits, chances of marrying one, born in a stranger's family or "Love marriage", intelligent children and making money in cinema, music or speculation.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=274|p. 274]]

Moon as a lord of 10 in 8 in Karthigai star, will prove to be a Maraka. It may end his life even in youth, when other planets are weak and also afflicted, because Karthikai is ruled by Sun, who is a bhandhaka Sthana-adhipathi. Planets in the constellation of bhadhaka Sthanathipathi will give death in their period or sub period. As regards his profession, he will have satisfaction in his remuneration. He will be much respected. He can save money. He will have peace, pleasure, prosperity and permanent tie of friendship. Moon in the constellation Rohini indicates early demise of father, dissatisfaction in service, though well-paid always thinking of a change, desire to go overseas, etc. Moon in 8 in Mrigasirisha in Taurus indicates success in enterprises, a very liberal partner, a spend-thrift wife or husband, delay in marriage, difficulty in the state of married life, profit by insurance or gaining money by legacy.

Moon, as a lord 11 in 8 in Aswini star, is definitely evil. The native can neither have pleasant life, nor friends to oblige him, or at least console him. He cannot think straight. He will be confused. He will be rash and will land himself in trouble. He may have friends in the Police or Military or in such departments where the officials have extraordinary powers. In his weak and unguarded moments, he may take them into confidence and may talk about his wrong action. When Moon Bhukti operates, these friends will betray him and will give valuable assistance to the colleagues to spoil the person. Moon in Bharani star gives wealth, music, large circle of friends, much respect in society, separation from father due to the transfer in the father's profession, loss through paternal uncle, difficulty through son-in-law or daughter-in-law (5th house shows one's son or daughter and 11th house is the 7th therefrom. So their partner in life is indicated by the 11th house). Partner will speculate and lose; Whereas the native will gain. Moon in Karthigai star shows loss through friends, or by paying penalty to the tax department or fine in the court of law or even imprisonment. Father may dispose of the ancestral property

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=275|p. 275]]

Moon, as lord of 12 in 8 in Poorattathi star, shows that there will always be difference of opinion between the person and the partner. The partner will gain in all disputes and it is advisable to effect a compromise, rather than fight out a case. One can return the loan or give away the share of profit to the partner. Wife's health will not be good, though she may make larger profits and purchase movable properties and costly jewels etc. Moon in 8 in Uthrapathrapada star is dangerous for one's longevity. One may suffer from any chronic disease. Hospitalisation during the period of Moon may be necessary. Partners in business and wife at home will bring about loss and also unnecessary expenses, outweighing one's income. Enemies will cause worry, though success to the native is assured after a long delay. It is the delay and the period of suspense which will irritate the native. Moon in Revathi star will prove to be lucky. Good investment, sudden gains, satisfactory bank position, substantial profit, large number of benefactors and fortunate partner to carry on business are all indicated by Moon in 8 in Revathi.

Moon in 8, in the constellation of Sun, Significator of Bhadhaka or Maraka Sthanas cause death from typhoid, heart disease, cerebral meningitis, sharp fever or malaria etc., or give death by paralysis, dropsy, beri beri, intestinal diseases, catemental disorders, convulsions, excess of drinks, uremia, tumors and diabetes etc.

Moon in Mars star similarly deposited indicates death due to inflammation, plague, haemorrhage, boils, stones in bladder, abortions, profuse bleeding, fistula and dysentery.

Moon so posited in Mercury constellation causes death by mental derangement, nephritis. Vitamin 'B' deficiency, hysteria, nervous breakdown, paralysis and digestive disturbances.

Moon in Jupiter star as significators of 8, bhadhakasthana or marakasthana cause cerebral congestion, liver complaints, jaundice, dropsy, flatulence, pleurisy, carbuncle, blood poisoning, etc.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=276|p. 276]]

Moon afflicted by Venus, gives death by diabetes, tonsils, amoebic dysentery, cysts, diseases in the generative system, goitre, etc.

Moon, afflicted by Saturn, gives death due to cold wave, gall stones, chyluria, TB. of bones, T.B. of lungs, cancer, paralysis, Bright's disease and pus formation.

Western:- Moon, afflicted by Uranus, threatens danger by paralysis, hiccough, hysteria, gas trouble, breathing troubles and spasm.

Neptune, afflicting Moon, makes one mad after something or other and finally lose control. So, brain troubles, overdose of medicine, especially sleeping dose, use of narcotics, coma, etc., are indicated.

Moon, conjoined with many planets in 8 will cause sudden tragic and unnatural death. Moon in the constellation of lord of 6 or 8 and also afflicted by them will give a watery grave. 8th house shows boat. So it may be that the boat capsizes and the native loses his wife while crossing a river.

The above is applicable in natal horoscope to ascertain the results of Moon when Moon Dasa or Bhukti or Anthra operates.
