# Transit Saturn - Rahu Conjunction

Saturn a natural malefic, conjoins with the ascending node, Rahu, another malefic at 11-30 A.M. on 25-4-1968 at 24° 35' in Pisces in Jupiter's sign, Mercury star and Rahu sub.

According to Krishnamurti Padhdhati, whomsoever have Mercury and Rahu favourably deposited in their horoscopes will be benefitted then, whereas those who have Mercury and Rahu ill - posited must suffer at that time (suffering will be ill - health, financial loss, enmity, failure in attempts, etc., depending on the matters signified by Mercury and Rahu in their horoscopes). Mercury indicates the nature of the result whereas Rahu shows whether in that matter the native will gain or loose, proceed or meet with obstacles, maintain health or suffer etc.,

Mercury governs the signs Gemini and Virgo. Mercury rules the star Ashlesha, Jyesta and Revathi. Planets including nodes posited in any of the three above stars will offer the results indicated by Mercury. Mercury can occupy any Bhava. Then, the planets and the nodes in those 3 stars will offer the results of the matter signified by that Bhava.

Therefore, people born in Aries, could have had Mercury in any of the twelve Bhavas, Though the lagna remains the same, Mercury by occupation indicates any of the 12 houses and so, the planets or the nodes in any of the three constellations of Mercury, will offer absolutely different results. If there were to be no planet, in any of the 3 constellations of Mercury, Mercury will offer the results of the house occupied by it. As it can be in any of the 12 houses, and each house has its own results which vary widely from each other and in some cases they are diametrically opposite. Therefore, to all Aries borns, planets in Mercury star will not yield the same results. They depend on the Bhava occupied by Mercury.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=308|p. 308]]

The source of the results given by the planets either deposited or transiting the constellation otherwise called nakshathra, asterism or star will be through the relations or matters indicated by the house occupied by Mercury and also owned by Mercury.

Again, people are born in different signs as lagna and different cusps of the houses therefore, will be in Gemini and Virgo owned by Mercury, so to different lagna borns, the source of the results will vary as Mercury will be the lord of different houses.

Anyhow, the results we have, can be broadly classified into two : i.e. good or bad, desirable or undesirable; successful or failure ; pleasant or painful etc. Hence Mercury, ruling the star Revathi, where the conjunction of Saturn and Rahu is to happen, indicates different nature of results to the people and Rahu sub shows whether it is advantageous or not, depending on the lord of the constellation in which Rahu was posited. Rahu might be in the 11th house but it could be in the constellation of the planet occupying 12th house ; thereby Rahu is incapable of giving a profit, whereas loss will be the experience. Also Rahu may be in 8th Bhava ; traditional astrologers may call Rahu as a malefic, but Rahu may be in the constellation of the planet in 11, when Rahu brings fortune. Hence, one should not pass a judgement whether a planet is a benefic or a malefic, only from the house occupied by it, but one is to note where the lord of the constellation is deposited. No doubt, Rahu in an evil sign can do such a harm as is threatened by that house, only when there is no planet in its constellation, Arudhra, Swathi or Sathabisha. Therefore, if at all, any astrologer is to predict the results to be enjoyed by Aries-borns or Taurus-borns etc., it is only general, it is not applicable to all, percentage of failure will be greater than the percentage of success. As you want the general prediction, I give below.

Saturn's transits. It is lord of 10 and 11 to Aries-borns, 9 and 10 to Taurus-borns : 8 and 9 to Gemini-borns; 7 and 8 to Cancer-borns and so on.

As the owner of these houses, it can offer the nature of results indicated by Mercury as lord of 3 and 6 to Aries-borns; 2 and 5 to Taurus-borns; 1 and 4 to Gemini-borns : 3 and 12 to Cancer-borns and so on.

1. Aries-borns : Short travel : new friends helpful ; proceeding on camp : receipt of traveling allowance, good relationship with brothers and uncle, release of books and journals, new agency, commission taken ; enters as a liaison officer, gets telephone, if it was long pending, victory, success in litigation, etc., provided Rahu is a benefic. If Rahu is evil, the adverse results in the above nature of results is to be judged. This rule applies to all lagna borns. The results are given to the lagna-Ascendant; not to Moon sign (Nakshathra or Sun sign) date of birth.

2. Taurus-borns : Gains money through music, opera, cinema, goes overseas, strangers and foreigners lend their assistance, gets into service in a far off place : Tonsil operation successful; So also, cataract and one can regain the vision. Success in higher studies, departmental examinations and thereby improvement in status is shown. Loss to younger brother, prosperity to children are marked.

3. Gemini-borns : Good health, recovery from disease, Construction of house : erection of machinery, purchase of vehicle, disease to mother, younger brother or sister either getting married or gaining through partner, success in education, litigation, higher status, promotion, admission for higher studies, gain through strangers are to be expected.

4. Cancer-borns: Loss by having brokerage or commission business - Loss of Liaison office. Loss during short trips, ill- repute and receiving irritating and annoying letters, Difference of opinion with partner, dispute, Publication in this period will not be advantageous.

5. Leo-borns : Even though the conjunction is in the 8th house, yet Mercury governing Revathi, owns the houses 2 and 11. Therefore grand success, social and financial success, victory over enemies, win in election, defeat to opponent in litigation to have a new financing partner in business. A successful time.

6. Virgo-borns Promotion, prosperity, name, fame, reputation, award, gains through business, new contracts, agreements, good health; grand success, purchase of property by partner. Equally father also makes money, whereas elder brother will lose heavily; children will fall ill and treatment will be given in headquarters hospital.

7. Libra-borns : Long journey, overseas higher education; speculation or gain through music or cinema in far off places, sale of property, retirement from service, termination of service, are to be expected, Younger brother is fortunate, gets a wealthy partner, gains in profession.

8. Scorpio-borns : Gains through commission, legacy, brokerage, success in mediation. Purchases property, vehicle etc, success in examination. Receipt of bonus, gratuity, Insurance amount, wife will be more lucky, ailment to youngsters, marriage to children though this is Maha Maka-year or Kumba Mela; loss to father.

9. Sagittarius-borns : Good luck, Prosperity, is assured. Marriage takes place. One can have child born. New partner joins for business. Publicity will be made. Propaganda will be done. Salesmen will be changed. Speedy turnover, larger profits, good luck.

10. Capricorn-borns : Victory over enemies, easy overdraft facilities, scholarship for higher studies - foreign assignment, reputation, honour unexpected gains by correspondence, competition and speculation, good luck to children.

11. Aquarius-borns : Worries, anxieties, child may be missing, or may cause anxiety, Purchase of property on child's or partner's name, ill-repute, Loss in speculation, litigation etc.,

12. Pisces-borns : Marriage, purchase of land, house, vehicle, assistance through friends, success in examination, may leave the native place. Change of residence. Entry into new house. Loss to children. Gains to younger brother.

Thus, a general picture can be given by following Krishnamurti Padhdhati.

As regards mundane astrology, I have very clearly stated how it is in an infant stage and one cannot using traditional astrology, pinpoint any event. For example, if one predicts an event due to the conjunction of evil planets, why it should happen is to be scientifically explained. Otherwise the readers may take that the prediction is his wish. For example, if one has said that between March and June of any year, policies may be modified, we expect such whenever the conjoin.

On 17-10-1956 in Scorpio; 15-5-1945 in Gemini; 11-2-1934 in Capricorn; 24-7-1922 in Virgo; 2-5-1911 in Aries; 10-11-1899 in Scorpio, Saturn and Rahu conjoined.

Saturn and Kethu were together in conjunction, similarly once in 11 years. On an average, once in 5 1/2 years Saturn conjoins with either of the nodes.

Let us refer to Indian History and World History. Except that in 1939 when Saturn and Kethu conjoined (not Rahu), Germany began the Second World War, no incident had happened when Saturn came in conjunction with Rahu.
