# Dasa, Bhukti, Anthra and Transit

There are 27 stars. Planets taken by Hindus are 9. As mentioned already each governs 3 stars. Depending on the portion to be covered, in a star by Moon and the lord of the constellation, the balance of the dasa ruled by the lord of the constellation is calculated. Then, in cyclic order the other dasas follow. For detailed, clear and exhaustive explanation, kindly refer to the book 1st Reader-Casting the horoscope of "KRISHNAMURTI PADHDHATI".

As there are 9 planets, there are 9 dasas. Each dasa has nine Bhukties; Hence one may expect there would be 81 combinations. But actually the combination will be 45.

When we take anthras, there should be 9 X 9 X 9 = 729. But the same combination i.e. 'A' dasa 'B' Bhukthi 'C' Anthra; 'A' dasa 'C' Bhukthi 'B' Anthra; 'B' dasa 'A' Bhukthi 'C' Anthra; 'B' dasa 'C' Bhukthi 'A' Anthra; 'C' dasa 'B' Bhukthi 'A' Anthra; 'C' Dasa 'A' Bhukthi 'B' Anthra are the combinations of the three planets A, B, and C in 6 different conjoined periods of these three. Again Dasa lord itself may be bhukthi lord and anthra lord. They are 9 in number instead of 18. Then Dasa lord, another planets' Bhukthi and Dasa lord anthra or Bhukthi lord anthra. Thus you can have permutation and combination.

In the map of Heavens erected according to Krishnamurti padhdhati there are 249 subs and so 249 combinations. Of these certain positions in the Zodiac are ruled by the same group of [?planets]

For example we have,

Mars Sign; Venus Star, Venus Sub 13°-20' to 15°-33'-20"

Venus Sign, Mars Star, Venus Sub [?182°-40'] to 184°-53'-20"

These two points are ruled by Mars and Venus.

Mars Sign, Venus star, Sun Sub 15°-33'-20" to 16°-13'-20"

Sun sign, Venus Star, Mars Sub 137°-20' to [?138°-6'-40"].

Venus Sign, Mars Star, Sun Sub 184°-53'-20" to 185°-33'-20".

These three points are governed by the same group of planets.

Venus Sign, Jupiter Star, Moon Sub 209°-26'-40" to 210°, Jupiter Sign, Venus Star, Moon Sub [?256°-13'-20"] to 257°-20' only two.

Saturn Sign, Mars Star, Mercury Sub 300° to 301°-53'-20" in Kumba; Mars Sign, Saturn Star, Mercury Sub 215°-26'-40" to 217°-20' - Vrishika; Mars Sign, Mercury Star, Saturn Sub 237°-53'-20" to 240° Vrishika; Mercury Sign, Mars Star, Saturn Sub 177°-53'-20" to 180°. Thus there are four different points for the group of Mars, Saturn and Mercury.

Therefore when A dasa B Bhukthi C Anthra operates, that part of the Zodiac governed by these three will be transited by them, or luminaries and Lagna. For example if it is Venus dasa, Mars Bhukthi, Venus anthra, then the position to be transited will be Mars, Venus, Venus, or Venus, Mars, Venus. If Mars dasa, Kethu Bhukthi, Kethu anthra operates, then 0° to 0°46'40" may be transited; or Kethu Star, Mars Sub, Kethu sub-sub or Kethu Star, Kethu Sub, Mars Sub-sub will be transited by Mars, Kethu; or Sun, Moon and on that day, the moment when the ascendant which rises in the East for the locality in which the native, then, is so that the sign lord, star lord, and sub lord agree. One should not always calculate the ascendant for the place of birth unless the native is permanent in his birth place.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=197|p. 197]]

In the case of Rahu and Kethu, the planet with which they are conjoined, or the lord of the constellation in which Rahu and Kethu were at the time of birth or the lord of the sign can give the result in their Star or Sub: also the lord of the day will be ruled by any one of the above in the order given. Suppose Rahu is in Aquarius conjoined with Jupiter; and in Dhanishta Star. Then Thursday ruled by Jupiter; or if no conjunction Mars by Tuesday. If Saturn is stronger than Mars, then Saturn can give the result on Saturday. Whenever Rahu rules the dasa or Bhukthi; the matter will materialise during Rahu-Kalam followed in Tamil Nadu. This also will agree.

Table for 249 subs is furnished below, Lord of the sign, Star and Sub are also given. If one runs Venus dasa, Moon Bhukthi, Mercury anthra, refer Venus Sign, Moon star, Mercury Sub: Moon Sign, Mercury Star, Venus sub: Mercury Sign, Moon Star, Venus sub.

So also if one runs the conjoined period of Merc., Rahu, Jupiter, refer Mercury sign, Rahu star, Jupiter sub; note also Jupiter sign, Mercury star, Rahu Sub. If one runs conjoined period of Sun, Venus, Mercury, then Sun sign, Venus star, Mercury Sub, Venus sign, Sun star, Mercury sub, and Mercury sign, Sun Star, Venus sub are the three sensitive and fruitful positions which will be transited by Ascendant, Luminaries and the planets ruling the dasa, bhukthi and anthra.
