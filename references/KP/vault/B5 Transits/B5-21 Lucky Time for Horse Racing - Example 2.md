The method to find out the moment is as follows:-

Note down which dasa is on : which bhukthi operates.

Then select the 3 stars belonging to dasa lord and 3 stars owned by bhukthi lord.

In these 6 star days one will gain, if both the dasa lord and bhukthi lord are advantageous.

Note when - which moment - Moon enters into that star and which moment it leaves that star. The interim period or interval betwen its entry into a star and its exit is the duration of the star. It may be 22 hrs.or 23 hrs. or 24 or 26 hrs. and minutes.

Take the duration, convert into minutes. Divide by 120. Then if the period of a planet is only one year then the result obtained by dividing the duration of the star by 120 will be the time taken by Moon to move 1 /120 of the star. If one wants to know the duration needed for Moon to move the Sun's sub which is allotted 6 years in Vimshottari Dasa, multiply the result obtained for 1 year by 6.

If the duration needed for Moon to move the Moon's sub which is allotted 10years in Vimshottari Dasa, multiply the result obtained for 1 year by 10.

For Mars and Kethu multiply by 7.

For Jupiter by 16; for Mercury by 17; for Rahu by 18 : for Saturn by 19 and for Venus by 20.

When you add all these, then the total duration will be obtained.

Therefore one knows, which time to which time the sub-period operates.
