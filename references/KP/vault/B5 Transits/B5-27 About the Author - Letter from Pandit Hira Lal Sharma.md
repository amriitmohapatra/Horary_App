# About the Author - Letter from Pandit Hira Lal Sharma

The Honourable Guruji Jyotish Marthand K.S.Krishnamurti  
Brahmin Street, Saidapet,  
Madras -15.

Respected Sir,

Jai Samb Sadashivji !

The principles of Advanced Stellar System propounded by our Guruji Shri K.S.Krishnamurti under the title 'Krishnamurti Padhdhati' are strikingly unique and have revolutionised the Astrological World. The discovery of the system by the application of which to the practically handled horoscopes - clients. Astrologers may be a novice or scholar in the field - every one is convinced of the 100% accurate predictions. If just like the discovery of Electron - that tiny particle (within the Atom) discovered by English Physicist Joseph John Thomson (which won him Nobel Prize in Physics) which startled the then Scientific minds, the discovery of this Padhdhati has made Astrology not an 'art' but 'an' exact Science'. This credit goes to Shri K.S.Krishnamurti - our Guruji and consequently whole astrological world is indebted to him.

For last two decades, myself and other celebrities of our Institute had solely been predicting on the basis of age old principles which ones however correctly and strictly applied could not place us in a position to predict meticulously beyond 40 per cent margin of accuracy. Now all the members of our Astrological Institute have totally switched off all traditional principles and have been happy enough to switch on yours one.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=285|p. 285]]

May lord Ganesh bestow a long span of life so that the Science itself may be most benefitted by more and more discoveries propounded by our able Guruji Varahamihiracharya of our age.

How long will it take us to have your forthcoming books in Astrology ? Their outcoming is eagerly awaited.

Yours faithfully,  
Jyothir  
Pandit HIRA LAL SHARMA,  
16-09-1968

Hon.Director,  
Sri Markandaya Astro. Cultural Institute,  
Ballagarh, Mandir, PO.Banjar  
District Kulu (Himachal Pradesh)
