First of all, one should know which method, when followed, will give correct time, date, month and year of one's gains in races.

There are innumerable methods to find out the number of the horse which would win - when we read the books on races and consider the examples given by the author, the method is appealing. When we apply the same method on future races with limited knowledge we find that horse, so selected by us is either scratched or it chases the other horses. No other horse could be seen behind the one so selected: being last lost the race.

But to a good extent the previous history of the horse and its present condition are considered and experienced people guess and they come out correct only 50-50.

Certainty cannot be declared, as, such a sure horse tumble down, jockey may fall down or due to its peculiar moment in the course, it may be disqualified. Hence from experience one can't select anything as a certainty.

Mangoes are in the tree. A boy anxious to have a fruit throws stones; unless he is destined to have, he cannot. But if he is fortunate to have, then he will have that number of mangoes as he is lucky to have.

Hence, what are we to do?

(1) Can I have gains without pains.

(2) Is so, will it be through races.

(3) or lottery or what.

(4) When? Will it be only once in my lifetime or frequently. If frequently the exact timings.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=235|p. 235]]

Answer: (1) To gain without much effort one should have the period ruled conjointly by those planets deposited, in the constellation and in the sub of the significators of the houses

(Bhavas) 2, 6 and 11.

(2) If one is to gain through races then any of those planets mentioned above should be connected with 5th house.

(3) lf any of the planets deposited in the constellation and in the sub of the significators of houses 2, 6 and 11, is connected with 3rd house one gains by way of lottery.

(4) If the significators are not posited in 2 and 12 to each other, luck will repeat. But if the significators are in 2 and 12to each other, then during the period of the planet in 12 to the other significator and the sub-period of the planet in 2 to the major lord, one will win. If the major period is governed by one in 2 to the subperiod lord, i.e. if the sub-period lord is in 12 to the major period lord, one will lose.

But if the significators occupy any other position one will win often whenever their conjoined period operates.

Yes, a period runs for months. Does it mean that in every race and on all days, one will win.

No. On the whole, in this period he will have substantial profit. But to find out in which race and on which day. One will win, one has to apply only Krishnamurti Padhdhati.

One should select the star days governed by dasa lord and in that the duration of the time when Moon will pass in the sub of the Bhukthi lord. Further to note the time when Moon will pass in the sub sub of the significators of 2, 6, and 11. Lagna at that time also must be calculated for judgement.
