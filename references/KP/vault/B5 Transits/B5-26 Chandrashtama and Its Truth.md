# Chandrashtama and Its Truth

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=280|p. 280]]

Q. Why do the Astrologers advise the consultants to avoid Chandrashtama days ? Is there any truth ?

We have to take only the exact position of the lagna, which alone indicates the person. Also we should consider the exact position of all the planets (constellation and sub) and offer the prediction. Otherwise the prediction given by either of the western or Hindu system will be similar to blind persons, touching any part of an elephant and describing how its physical features appear to be or touching some animal and giving elephants description.

By Chandrashtama, Hindus mean those days whenever Moon transits for nearly 2 1/4 days in a sign, which is the 8th counted from the sign occupied by Moon in Natal Horoscope. They do not take into consideration whether at the moment of the birth time, Moon was in the very beginning or in the middle or in the end of the sign. Nor they take into consideration whether the transmitting planet is in the beginning or in the middle or in the end of the sign. They count sign by sign not from the exact position of Moon to the exact position of any planet. This is most unscientific.

Those who are born in Ashwini or Bharani or the first quarter of Karthik star, will have Moon in Aries. The other 3/4 of Karthik, Rohini and Mrigasirisha first half, will be in Taurus : thus all the 27 stars contained in the 12 signs so that each sign has 2 1/4 stars. So, for Aries-born, Scorpio is the 8th Chandrashtama. For Taurus-born, Moon transiting in Sagittarius, is said to be Chandrashtama. The Gemini-born, Chandrashtama means that Moon is transiting in Capricorn. So, for Cancer, which sign transited by Moon is said to be Chandrashtama. Aquarius, is it not ? Thus to each sign one is to note.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=281|p. 281]]

Astrologers consider that planets transiting in 8th sign must cause damage and do harm. But the truth is not so.

Now, coming to Chandrashtama, let me not give many examples to prove that no useful purpose is served by mentioning in the monthly predictions, the days or dates when Moon will be in Ashtama. Why do they give ? What do they mean by that ? Do they expect that Chandrashtama days must be evil ?

They have not noted that during Chandrashtama day, some win the case, some come out successful in Election, some pass the examination, some get married, some have easy delivery of children, some gain by lottery, some win in the races and so on.

Is it wise, simply going on threatening people or like an ill-omen to mention these dates ? No doubt, whether one threatens or not, according to destiny one will win or gain in the period when planet transits in the 8th house.

Similarly whenever Saturn transits in many signs, the traditional astrologers threaten people by giving undesirable and disagreeable results, especially ashtama Sani, etc., They do not take into consideration that during Sade-Sati, i.e., 7.5 years Saturn, most of the people have grand success. If this is brought to their notice they say look at dasa, bhukthi etc., But when the twins horoscopes are handed over and asked why one of the twins has passed the examination and the other failed, they have no explanation. First of all they follow a wrong method. Secondly there is either limitation or only confusion. If one were to follow Krishnamurti Padhdhati, he can very well differentiate and come out correct in all cases, by taking into account the "Sub".

Let me give you an example of one of the very well-known politicians. The horoscope, which was published in page 20 in April, 1986 "Astrology and Athrishta" issue, is as follows ([[#^b5-f011|Fig. B5.11]]).

If according to Hindu system we take the position of Moon, it is in Aquarius and he has won very meritoriously with overwhelming majority against the wish and obstacles thrown by his opponents. Is he not having Sade-Sati and do we not find a group of planets in Ashtama (in the 8th sign counted from Aquarius) ? Saturn and Rahu are in 2 and Saturn is said to be causing Sade-Sati. Jupiter, Kethu and Moon are in Ashtama. If a person takes this chart and shows to an Astrologer to give the results as per transit how can he give correct results using Hindu System ? Will not he dissuade the person ? Is it not doing harm to the person and also bringing ridicule to astrology.

But follow Krishnamurti Padhdhati. On the day of the announcement of Election results, i.e., 9th January, 1969 in the night, when Jupiter, Kethu and Moon were in 8 and Sani and Rahu were in 2, he came out successful. According to Krishnamurti, Saturn was transiting in Mercury star and sub of Rahu, who is to give the results of the Houses 2 and 11, in his horoscope. It is said by Krishnamurti that houses 1,2,3,6,10 and 11 are the houses, which will promise success in one's competition, election, litigation etc., Therefore, even though Saturn was in the [illegible]th house from the lagna, as it was transiting in the constellation and sub of the planets occupying and signifying the house 2 and 11, he came out victorious. One may ask how can Rahu offer beneficial results during the time a planet is transiting in its sub. Rahu is in the 2nd house. It is in the constellations of Moon and sub of Mercury. Moon is in the 7th. 7 shows opposition. Moon is lord of 12. It shows that the opposition will be in a foreign place and position of Rahu was 18°24' in Virgo. It shows that it was in Mercury's sub. Therefore, Rahu is occupying Mercury's sign and also Mercury sub. Hence good luck has repeated. Guru was in the [illegible]th counted from Moon. But it was actually transiting in Mercury sign, Moon star and Rahu sub. Moon was transiting in Sun star. Sun is lord of the lagna posited in 11 in his horoscope. Therefore Uttaraphalguni Nakshatra is a fortunate one when one has regained the lost position.

It has ever been mentioned that our Deputy Prime Minister regained the lost position and had a better one during Ashtama Sani and also when majority of the planets were in Ashtama, counted from moon position (because he is born in Poorvaphalguni Nakshatra in Simha Rasi) and at the time of announcement of the result and also at the time of taking charge of Deputy Prime Minister; there was no Guru Balam according to the Traditional Astrology because Guru was in 12 and there were Ashtama Sani, Ahstama Rahu, Ashtama Surya and so on.

After reading this, I trust, the readers will follow Krishnamurti Padhdhati. and forget the traditional system which is useless.

Now my desire and my request would be that the traditional Astrologers should switch on to Krishnamurti Padhdhati.
