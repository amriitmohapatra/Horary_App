# Western System - Transit for Individual Charts

No planet is stationary excepting those for a very short period during the time when they commence retrograde motion or take direct course after retrograde motion. All planets are ever moving. Therefore, if we note down the position of the planets (a) at the moment of the birth of a person or (b) in the progressed horoscope these planets will be (a) either transiting over the point occupied by the planets (b) while transiting they may form either favorable or unfavourable aspects. They also take into consideration, the Cusps of the 12 houses both in the birth chart and also in the progressed horoscope. During transit a person feels the results, if there is either conjunction or strong aspects for e.g., 60 degrees; 120 degrees; 90 degrees, 150 degrees and so on Whereas one may not even notice a result if the aspects were to be minor like 18 degrees; 54 degrees and so on. One may pose the question whether the effect of transit will be similar to that enjoyed for the same aspect in progression. The answer is that the nature of the result will be similar but the extent, strength and duration varies. In progressed chart the experience will be for a long period whereas in the transit the duration will be very little. Let me explain giving an example; suppose Mars forms evil aspect with 8th Cusp, no doubt it shows danger and difficulties. In a progressed horoscope these results will be felt for some months. But if Mars during transit forms evil aspect with 8th cusp then the threatening period lasts only for a week. We may compare that the effect of an aspect being formed by progression will be similar to the rain in the rainy season which lasts for months. But the same aspect being formed during transit will be similar to the rain in summer for a day or for a few hours. To thoroughly understand the results to be enjoyed according to transit, the student must study the results contributed to the various aspects among two planets. Because the results is for a short period, one has to modify the results and put it in a mild tone. Supposing a lady has been born with strong benefics occupying favourable houses and if it is promised that she would be a career woman then any evil planet forming adverse aspect during transit cannot cause strong adverse results because there can be mild humiliation, irritation or worry.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=23|p. 23]]

According to Westerners the correct method of judgment is as follows:-

1. Find which planet moves to form the aspects with either the cusp or the planet in the natal chart, and also in the progressed chart. The duration depends on their speed. Therefore if Moon forms an aspect, it lasts for a fortnight. Mercury, Venus, Sun and Mars, forming similar aspects cause the results for a period of a few months. Jupiter and Saturn while moving, forming an aspect will cause the effect for at least six months. Uranus, Neptune, etc., will give the effect for years.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=24|p. 24]]

2. If the transiting planet moves forward and retraces i.e., retrogrades then the duration will be much longer. If a planet while in direct motion, in retrograde motion and again in direct motion forms the same aspect or a malefic aspect, it causes repeatedly the same results. One should not ask whether it can give marriage on all the three dates when it forms such aspects. One has to take that on all these three occasions, negotiation, fixation, celebration and if in one of the previous aspects, marriage was celebrated, then during the next similar aspects, happy life with wife is to be interpreted. Normally, the effect is said to last from the time the planet is about 8 degree away from the position for exact aspect and 8 degrees away from the same aspect. Therefore, a planet offers the results when it transits for 16 degrees. The faster the movement of a planet the quicker will be the results and it will be for a short period, whereas slow moving planets have to move 16 degrees and the duration is long. Therefore, the effect will be for a long period.

3. When one planet 'A' forms good aspect and another planet 'B' forms adverse aspect, both will operate.

4. When the aspect is exact, the result will be the utmost.

5. The effect caused by the transiting planet is more important in the natal chart, than that in the birth chart.

6. One should not omit to note the transit of the Sun over the position of the planets in the natal chart. Sun indicates major events. Mercury, Moon and Venus show minor results.

7. If a planet forms an aspect during its transit similar to the aspect which it had at the time of the birth in the natal chart, the results indicated by the aspect among them will be predominant.

Let it be explained with an example. Supposing Saturn at the time of the birth was forming an adverse aspect with another planet, then during transit whenever Saturn forms evil aspect with the same planet will cause very strong evil effect. But if it forms harmonious aspect then the extent to which it can do harm is much less.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=25|p. 25]]

8. Every year, as soon as you get the ephemeris, note the movement of each planet and find out when they transit over the position of the planets (that is conjunction) and during those days, they will give the results to a good extent.

9. If a planet 'A' forms good aspects with planet 'B' at the moment of birth, then whenever during transit 'A' forms good aspect with 'B' most advantageous results will be enjoyed. In all these cases whenever planets form aspects, they will do experience on the day when Moon by transit forms favourable aspect, though not the same. For example if Jupiter forms good aspect with Venus, the results will be enjoyed. Jupiter may form a trine aspect, but Moon will give the results even though it may be sextile, semi-sextile, etc.

10. Even though all aspects cannot be found between Sun and Mercury, Sun and Venus, Mercury and Venus in the birth chart, yet, during transit all aspects will be found. Therefore, whatever aspect during transit may be found among these the results will be mild. But at the same time as the duration is short, one can realise it and experience it to a small extent, Duration is short; the extent to which one can enjoy is also small.

11. During transit one should include the aspects like parallel also.

12. If in the Progressed Horoscope, there is favourable aspect and in transit also a harmonious aspect is being formed, one can enjoy most advantageous results. But if in the Progressed Horoscope, the aspect is evil whereas in the transit the same planets form favourable aspects then the advantage will be very mild, the aspects formed in the Progressed Horoscope lasts for a long period and it offers results to a good extent: the transit is not so [illegible]

13. The above is followed by Westerners and not included in my system.
