# Nodes Transit

(It takes nearly 1.5 years for Rahu and Kethu to transit from one sign to the other. The nodes Rahu and Kethu always move in the opposite direction to that of Sun and Moon.)

Rahu offers the results similar to that of Mars in transit. Kethu gives such of those indicated by Saturn.

Hindus do not include Uranus or Neptune.

The transit of the planets are used by the Hindus to reduce the duration of a long period according to dasa, bhukthi and Anthra. These rules must be included while judging the transit results.

1. A particular bhava results will be enjoyed at the time the lord of the ascendant during its transit passes in a sign which is 5 or 9 counted from the sign occupied by the lord of the house which is judged. For e.g. if the 7th house matter of a person is judged he will be successful if the lord of the ascendant transits in a sign 5 or 9 counted from the sign occupied by lord of 7, both in rasi and Navamsa.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=34|p. 34]]

2. When the lord of the ascendant transits the house in question.

3. When the lord of the house transits the 5th or the 9th house counted from the sign in rasi and in Navamsa occupied by the lord of the ascendant.

4. Or when the lord of the Bhava transits in the ascendant itself.

5. When the lords of the houses and that of the ascendant come in conjunction or aspect each other.

6. For each house there is a governor - Karaka. So if the Karaka of the house in question transits and comes in conjunction with lord of the ascendant or the lord of the house where Moon was deposited i.e., the chief governor of that house should come in conjunction with the lord of the Lagna or the lord of the Rasi.

7. Jupiter during transit transiting in a sign which is 5 or 9 both in Rasi and in Navamsa to the sign occupied by the lord of the house under judgment. Then the advantageous results of that house may be looked for. The opponents will be subdued and they come under the control of the person whenever lords of the ascendant and that of the 6th house during transit conjoin together, and in that sign lord of the 6th house is stronger, then the result will be just opposite. Supposing one is born in Gemini ascendant and both Mercury and Mars conjoin in Capricorn where Mars is exalted then he will face adverse results and the opponent will win. Whenever the lord of a house and the lord of the ascendant happen to be either natural enemies or temporary enemies and if they transit in 6 and 8 to each other there will be enmity, jealousy, etc. But if they are friendly and if they transit in favourable houses one is to predict desirable and agreeable results. If the lord of the ascendant conjoins with the lord of a house and the latter is strong, improvement of the matters signified by the house is be expected. Otherwise, no. just like, the above rules are given taking lord of Lagna into consideration, so also one should take the lord of the sign where Moon was at the time of the birth and offer the prediction.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=35|p. 35]]

Whenever Saturn transits either in Rasi or in Navamsa the sign occupied by lord of 8 or 12 counted from that particular bhava, the matters signified by those, both get destroyed in full. So also one can expect such an adverse result whenever Saturn moves in the signs which are 5 and 9 to Rasi and Navamsa occupied by the lord of 8 or 12 counted from the same house.

One is to expect the death whenever Saturn transits in Rasi and Navamsa occupied by (A) the lord of the 8th house (B) Gulika (C) Saturn or (D) the lord of the 2nd decanate counted from the ascendant.

Note the lord of the decanate to the ascendant; also find out the lord of the 8th and that of the 22nd decanate. Death takes place when Jupiter transits the Rasi or the Navamsa occupied by any of the three planets or whenever Jupiter transits the Rasi or Amsa which are 5 and 9 counted from the position occupied by any of the three planets.

(The three planets are, the lord of the decanate of the ascendant the lord of the 8th house and also the lord of the 22nd decanate).

Again one is to note down the transit of Sun. The rule given here by the sage is very much complicated. And one gets such an answer that Sun transiting in any of the 12 Rasis can give death. In the previous condition it is said that Jupiter should transit either Rasi or Amsa that is two signs. Thrikona to them another 4 signs i.e. 6 signs. These 6 signs are for each planet that is lord of the 8th house for him 6 signs. Lord of 22nd decanate 6 signs. Therefore, all sings are in any manner connected with to cause evil. Therefore, if a person were to die in any year, in any month this rule will answer, whether Jupiter is in Aries or any one of the 12 signs. It is really most deplorable and also paining to follow such rules. Similarly also they have said for Sun. For Moon also they have pitched up similarly. I strongly declare that this method followed by Hindus is really useless. If a man were to die at the age of 20, there are rules; at the age of 30 there are rules; at the age of 40, there are other rules; at the age of 90, even then there are rules. Oh God How long a time are the traditional astrologers to make the best use of the various rules? Is there no end to it? Can't they improve? Can't they give up and study my method.
