# Sun's Transit

(Sun normally transits in each sign for a period of one month. The results are given for each sign. Therefore, one should not take that all through the month the result will operate. But the position of other planets also must be taken).

1. When Sun transits in the first sign, that is, in the sign where Moon was at the time of birth, one can expect indisposition, ill-health, obstacles for success, less of prosperity, change of place etc.
2. Income is reduced; Expenses increase; there can be deception or eye-sore or head-ache.
3. Good health; increase in income.
4. Obstacles and delay; health fails; no happiness in domestic life.
5. Enemies threaten; health fails; no happiness in domestic life.
6. Increase in income; good health; success in every attempt.
7. Falls ill; goes on journey; financial worry.
8. Mental agony; digestive disturbances; dispute; difficulty; accident; injury.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=29|p. 29]]

9. Gives up hopes; meets with danger and ill-health.
10. Grand success; interviews great people; their friendship.
11. Larger income; speedy turn over; business expands; makes good gain; receives honour; maintain good health.
12. Gives up hopes; depressed much; wandering here and there without aim.
