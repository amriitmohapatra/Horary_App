## How to Predict ?

What is your ascendant ?; "Libra".

What does each planet signify ?

Sun, lord of 11, deposited in Rahu star, Moon sub. In which house is Rahu ? It is in the 9th house. Hence Sun will offer the matters signified by the 9th house, i.e. father, wife's younger brother, younger brother's wife, grand child, next elder brother's elder brother, as regards relationship; other matters are higher education, long journey etc., Hence during Sun period and whenever a planet transits the position occupied by Sun (though it is in lagna) 9th house matters will be enjoyed. Is there any planet in Sun's star ? None - In that case Sun will also indicate lagna bhava results. (If there is any planet in Sun star, then that planet will give to a greater extent, lagna bhava results.)

Is there Rahu or Kethu in Sun's sign - No. Then its results will not be shared by or represented by any other planet.

If there is any, then what are we to judge ?

If there is any or there are many, those planets situated in Karthik or Uthraphalguni or Uthrashada will give the matters signified by lagna bhava.

No planet is in Sun's Star.

So Sun will give both lagna and 9th house results as it is in Rahu star.

"Yes".

How will it give?

The nature of the result is shown by the lord of the constellation according to its occupation. So 9th house results.

Also, only when there is no planet in its star, the matters signified by the Bhava occupied by it will be given.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=302|p. 302]]

Apply systematically. Arrive at each, methodically. Proceed patiently and properly; then predict precisely.

Sun rules Leo. 11th cusp is in Leo. It extends upto 8°32' in Virgo from 7°32' in Leo. In this are, there is no occupant. So you should take that Sun, owner of the sign Leo, where 11th cusp has fallen as the significator of 11 because there is no planet in Sun's star. Sun has to indicate the 11th house matters also".

"So Sun indicates the results of the houses 1, 9 and 11.

"How will the above results be accomplished ? i.e. the source for this nature of result ?

"Source is to be judged from the matters signified by the Bhava or Bhavas owned by the planet and also the Bhava occupied by it.

Saturn is in Lagna Bhava and hence slow in grasping. When once learnt, then never forget as one repeats whatever is learnt within himself.

Take Moon. It is in Libra at 12°-15', i.e. in Venus sign Rahu star Saturn sub. Moon rules Rohini, Hastham, Sravana Stars. It is in Rahu star who is situated in 9th house. Hence any planet in Rahu star will give 9th house results. So Moon also will give 9th house matters.

No planet is in Moon's star. So the Bhava occupied by it being lagna Moon will give lagna, effects also.

Further in Moon's sign, the meridian alone is. In 10th house there is no planet. So Moon is to offer 10th house results also as there is no planet in its star. Hence Moon is to bestow the results of the houses 1, 9 and 10 during its periods, sub periods, etc., and any planet transiting in the position occupied by Moon will indicate matters connected with the houses, 1, 9 and 10.

The source will be from first and tenth houses, as it owns the 10th and occupies the first Bhava."

Mars is in the 7th house. Its stars are Mrigasira, Chithra and Dhanishta. Hence Jupiter in Dhanishta will give the results of 7th house. Mars is in Venus star, Rahu sub. Lord of the constellation is in lagna. Hence Mars, though lord of the houses 2 and 7, and is in 7, yet will offer the results of the lagna. Second house is occupied by Mercury. So, the second house matters will be given by the planets in Mercury constellation and if there is no planet, in Mercury star Ashlesh, Jyesta and Revathi, Mercury will offer second house matters. Actually there is no planet in its star. Therefore Mercury indicates both the second and 12th house matters.

The source will be through 9th and 12th houses. As regards Mars the source will be 7th and 2nd houses and the result will relate to lagna.

Saturn is in Lagna Bhava, it is in its own star, and sub. Mercury is also in Saturn's star. Therefore, Saturn as it is in Lagna Bhava and Mercury, in Saturn's star, show lagna results. (Mercury has another portfolio, i.e., the 12th house.) The source or in other words how it will be accomplished is judged from the houses owned by it; they are 4 and 5. As there is no planet in the 5th, 5th house results also will be given by Saturn. Even though he owns the 4th house, as there is Jupiter in 4th Bhava; planets in Jupiter star or Jupiter himself will give 4th house matters through 6th house source. Saturn gives mostly through 5th house and partly through 4th house matters. Thus, one should analyse and keep ready, a ready chart wherein for each planet, which houses, matters they will offer and which is the source for the materialisation of the nature of the result.

Sun gives the results of the houses 1,9 and 11 as there is no planet in its star and it is in the constellation of one in 9 and it is the owner of the 11th. The sources are 1 and 11.

Moon gives the results of the houses 1,9 and 10 through the 1st and 10th houses.
