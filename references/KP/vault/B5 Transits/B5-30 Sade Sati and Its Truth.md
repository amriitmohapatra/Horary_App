# Sade Sati and Its Truth

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=296|p. 296]]

Sade-Sati means that Saturn will be transiting in the 12th sign to the sign occupied by Moon (whatever the position of Moon be in that sign either in the first degree or in the last degree of the sign), then Saturn transits in the sign where Moon was deposited and in the second sign counted from that tenanted by Moon. Therefore, the three signs, the 12th sign to the sign where Moon was, the sign occupied by Moon (called Janma) and the second to it are considered to be the three consecutive signs which when transited by Saturn, it is said to be Sade Sati or 7 1/2 years Saturn. People who go by mere traditional convention and the general principles will sing a song or reproduce a sloka and translate it that it is 7.5 years Saturn which is harmful; one will be dissatified, there can be denial, disappointment, depression, difference, dispute, disharmony and no desirable results. Thus they threaten. But, I am very confident and I can boldly say that they do not care to correct themselves after knowing the facts. Why ? It is not unlikely that they know well that people born in this star, get married during Sade Sati, have promotion, make purchases of land, construct houses, possess new car and enjoy all fruits of life. Those who had no business at all or no profit when Saturn was transiting in the 11th house, (considered to be lucky according to tradition) they actually pay a much larger amount of Income-tax. Why ? When they have Sade-Sati the profit is much greater than the previous years, even though the rate may be reduced.

But, when a person is to note down the stars in which certain people were born (the sign where Moon was) and also the 12th and the 2nd sign, they will find that the general Principle which had been expounded by our sages do not agree at all with the results which we enjoy. At the time when they predict adverse results, we enjoy pleasure. At the time when they predict and promise good future, there is actually much of pains. So, generally at the time when they say that there will be pains, we enjoy; and we really suffer at the time when they encourage offering auspicious results. It is really the misfortune of the consultants that they approach those Astrologers who do not care to find out how far it has acted and how they have to modify the rules.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=297|p. 297]]

Let me treat this in brief. Saturn will transit in Pisces. What are the three Nakshathras in that sign ? The first 3° 20' happen to be Poorvapadra ruled by Jupiter, who owns the houses 9 and 12 to Ariesborns. It is after all 3 degrees 20 minutes. Let us leave it. Then, there are 26 degrees 40 minutes in that sign which are covered by two Nakshathras (stars), Uthrapadra and Revathi. Uthrapadra is governed by Saturn. It owns the signs Capricorn and Aquarius which are the 10th and 11th to the Moon sign Aries. Consider in the following manner. When lord of 10 and 11 transit in the constellation of lord of 10 and 11, what will it offer ? Can anybody predict that there may be any evil even though it will transit in Meena-Pisces. By lordship, Saturn promises to do good. But if the sublord is adverse then during transit (Gochara) it may offer evil results. At that time, Saturn is incapable of doing any good; it is helpless ; its hands are tied. A father may have money. He would like to send Transit Money Order to his own son. But if there is the postal holiday, what can he do ? Similarly, Saturn as lord of 10 and 11 to Mesha rasi is a strong benefactor as regards friends, finance, fortune, pleasure, profit etc., even though it is Badhakasthana Athipathi which may affect only the health, if its dasa or bhukthi is operating or the dasa or bhukthi is ruled by the planet in the constellation of Saturn. Excepting that, Saturn has to necessarily do good to Mesha rasi-borns when it transits in Saturn's star, Uthrapadhrapada. So, if it transits in its own constellation it is auspicious and advantageous to the person if it is the significator of beneficial houses. Saturn gains strength. It will surely do good, irrespective of the fact that Pisces is the 12th sign to the sign Aries, where Moon was. At the time when Saturn passes on to Revathi, it offers the results signified by [?Mercury,] the lord of Revathi. Mercury owns Gemini and Virgo which are the 3rd and 6th houses. Third house shows short- journey; it indicates that one will be helped. Also, there will be prosperity through the neighbours, cousins, brothers and sisters; to make money by editing, publishing, etc., is also indicated. As lord of 10 and 11 transiting in the constellation of lord of 6, it shows that it is the time the native will take all preventive measures against infectious diseases and that he will gain as the opponent is destined to lose. The opponent loses, thereby the native gains.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=298|p. 298]]

During election those who are born in Mesha rasi, if their lagna also happens to be Mesha, they can take it for granted that they will have thumping majority, victory over enemies and they may be confident that the enemies will lose their deposit unless Saturn in one's horoscope is the significator of 8 and 12. But, Saturn is such a powerful planet and it is in a fruitful sign. Therefore, it can never disappoint when it is in its own constellation. It is really very beneficial to people born in Mesha Lagna. It is certain.

For general public, not taking individual charts, let us see if a person is born in Rishaba lagna and Mesha rasi, then you may be sure that he will win whether it is in Uthrapada or Revathi as both the stars are ruled by the benefics to Rishaba lagna. To Mithuna lagna, if Saturn were to transit in Uthrapadra then there will be mixed result, as lord of 8 is Saturn and lord of 12 is Mercury. To Simha (Leo-borns), it shows that the native will have victory over enemies most easily and opponent will lose against expectations. Leo-borns may have a strong opposition. Does not matter. Because those who promise that they will work for the opponents will finally let them down without their knowledge. To Kanya lagna, we have to take that Saturn will ultimately do good and compensate, as he may have to lose some money during the 2.5 years period. To Thulam (Libra - borns), it is really against their interests. So, people born in Libra may very well withdraw at least in the last moment when the deposit amount may be refunded to them. But suppose somebody wants to take a financial partner to do business, better he selects one born in Libra, as the money that is received by Libra will be mostly given away to the partner. If a girl were to marry a Libra-born, she can expect such advantage and gain through her husband. People who were married a few years ago, if they had no children so far, they can expect a child when Saturn transits in Uthrapada.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=299|p. 299]]

Now one may ask "Sir, I am born in Libra. Does it mean that all Libra-borns cannot come out successful in the election." No. It is not so. Suppose one is born in Libra and at the time of birth Saturn was not the significator of 8 or 12 then what will be the result ? Saturn cannot do that which it has to do. If Saturn has to do harm to Libraborns, it does offer, but it is insignificant as greater, good results are enjoyed when other planets contribute for success. It is similar to a person gaining in 5 races and losing in one race; ultimately there is profit.

Therefore students are to note that the lordship of the planet ruling the star decides the fate and the house signified by Saturn indicates the source. Coming to Scorpio, he is another loser. Coming to Sagittarius-Dhanus, he will come out victorious.

Capricorn people will come by most desirable results. He can make fortune. "Make Hay when the sun shines". So reiterates Saturn when it is transiting in Uthrapada Nakshathra. People born in Makara lagna, people born with Sun and Moon also in Makara they will note that when Saturn transits in Uthrapadra star, they will come by fortune. It is not unlikely they gain in lottery; they have some unexpected income and it will be sudden and substantial. To Kumbalagna, Saturn transiting in Uthrapadra, shows that there will be mixed results; so also to people born in Pisces Lagna.

Consider in which constellation a planet transits. Note down the matters of the houses signified by the lord of the constellation. That alone will be offered by the transiting planet through the source indicated by transiting planet and the sub shows the success or failure, pleasure or pain.
