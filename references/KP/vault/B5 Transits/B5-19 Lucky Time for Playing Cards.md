# Lucky Time for Playing Cards

A LUCKY TIME FOR PLAYING CARD

Practical Method:

Whether one has got a horoscope or not, there should be a method by which it should be possible to find out the auspicious period in a day when one can relax himself by going to a club to play cards or any other game. What he should do and furnish to the Astrologer is a diary wherein he should note down his gains and loss on various days, in the past mentioning which minute to which minute he won and which minute to which minute he lost. Also he should mention the ultimate gain or sucess on that day. Then the Astrologer is to adopt the following principles.

1. Take date by date. Then note down when Moon entered in to a constellation on that day and the duration of its stay,which is the interval between Moon's entry into a star and its passing on to the next star i.e.completing and just leaving this star.

2. Find out the number of hours and minutes which Moon takes to pass on in that constellation.Each constellation is of 13 degrees 20 minutes and to pass it, it may take 24 hours or 25 or even 26 hours or in 22 hours it may pass; most irregular: never uniform: ever unsteady.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=230|p. 230]]

3. Then divide the constellation in the proportion in which each planet is alloted the number of years in Vimshottari Dasa. Therfore, if the duration is for X hours , then Venus takes 20/120 into X hours.Sun takes 6/120 multiplied by X hours, Moon 10/ 120 of X hours, Mars 7/120 into the total of X hours, Jupiter 16/ 120 of the X hours and so on.

4. If Moon takes, say, 24 hours to pass through a particular constellation, then divide it by 120. The number of minutes obtained will be to a planet, if it is alloted one year. So multiply these minutes (total duration divided by 120) by the number indicating the number of years alloted to a planet. For example, if venus is alloted 20 years, then the duration is divided by 120 and multiplied by number of years alloted to venus.

Since every hour is of 60 minutes and we divide the duration of hours by 120, it is quick to work by dividing the duration in hours by 2. That is, if 24 hours is the duration,then 12 minutes will be the time, if a planet is alloted only one year, in the vimshottari dasa. When Sun has 6 years in Vimshottari Dasa then 6x12 minutes will be the duration for transiting the Sun Sub in that star by Moon. lf Moon transits in the Sub of Rahu (as for everyyear it takes 12minutes) for 18years, Moon will take 18x12 i.e. 216 minutes. lf Mars Sub is to be transited by Moon, it will take 7 multiplied by 12 minutes;if it is Mercury, 17x12 minutes and so on.

5. Note, to which planet the constellation belongs. Then the first Sub is ruled by the lord of the constellation there after in cyclic order, each Sub is being transited by Moon. So from the moment when the Moon enters into a Nakshatra, Be adding the number of minutes for each planet commencing from that of the constellation lord in cyclic order.

6. Note down, from which minute to which minute a particular Sub is running in the constellation.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=231|p. 231]]

7. Note down, in which Sub period ,the native had won and which sub he had lost. Then one will find the group of planets which offer advantageous results and the group which always work against his interest. For the future, in the diary, note down the hours and minutes in a day, when lucky group's sub will be transited by Moon. There are the hours and minutes when one can surely gain.

Horoscope:

If one has got a horoscope, then note down those planets which are the significators of 6 and 11. Only those planets not connected with 8 and 12 will give profit. If a planet is the significator of 8 and 12, reject it, as it will cause loss.Therefore, you have to erect the horoscope of the native. Note down the significators. Find out which are not connected with 8 and 12and select those which are the significators of 6 and 11 and then work out the days when this group will conjointly be operating, i.e. when Moon will be transiting in the constellation of one of the planets, being the significator of 6 and 11 and among the sub of planet which is also the significator of 6 and 11 Then only, one can be sure of gain, even when one runs bad time .

Accordinn to Dasa Bhukthi :

In my experience I found that if the Dasa and Bhukthi is evil, then there will be obstacle for him to sit and play cards in the club during such short lucky time . There will be some disturbance. He will not be in a position to play. But the planets will create, such opportunity as to visit during the bad hours he will find it to be unlucky . Supposing one runs, according to Dasa and Bhukthi, a very good time, then he will not be allowed to play during the hours which are considered to be dis-advantageous . But he will somehow sit during the hours and minutes which are ruled by the benefactors and he will surely win. This very method of working goes to prove that nobody can change fate and everything happens according to the Divine Plan and even in these silly matters, we have no hand in it. We are helpless and our fate alone, wins the race.

Take it as the starting point .That is the Ascendant. Work out the other cusps for that latitude of judement. Note down the position of the planet at the time of judement. Find out the sub lord of the 5th cusp.

If the sub lord of the 5th cusp is deposited in any sign in any constellation, but in the sub of the significator of the houses 6 and 11 one wins most satisfactorily . Highest reward.

If the sub lord of the 5th cusp is deposited in the sub of the significators 2 and 10, the gain is moderate.
