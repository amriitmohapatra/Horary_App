# Is Chandra-Ashtama Ever Evil

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=277|p. 277]]

Then how has my desire been fulfilled on chandra ashtama day?

The following is the letter and horoscope ([[#^b5-f010|Fig. B5.10]]) of Mr. H Viswanathan who wants the scientific explanation for his success on chandra ashtama day.

On Thursday 30-9-1965 at 3.30 PM. I have been relieved on leave by my present employer for undergoing training in a leading firm after passing a professional examination. There had been delay in my relief to this day. Can you explain why I had my desire fulfilled on a chandra ashtama day?

(Sd.) H Viswanathan

Khandari Colony

Bombay

At the time of birth, balance of Venus Dasa was 9 years 5 months and 23 days.

**Answer:**

On 30-9-1965, Jupiter rules the day, Mars governs the sign Scorpio-Vrichika where Moon was transiting and Jyeshta was the constellation in which Moon was passing at 3.30 PM. and the Ascendant was the 1st degree in Aquarius.

On the day you were relieved, you had been running Mars Dasa, Mercury Bhukti, Mars Anthra. It commenced on 19-3-1965.

1. Generally if the Dasa lord and Bhukthi lord are strong in the sign, then the matter signified by them can be had when Moon transits in the sign, star and sub ruled by them. Actually on that day the star was Jyeshta, governed by Mercury in Scorpio, owned by Mars. Whatever be the position of Moon in the horoscope, whether it is chandra ashtama or not, the matter will be fulfilled.

2. You are born in Capricorn ascendant Moon owns Cancer the 7th house. It indicates those with whom you transact. It shows one with whom you work, one who is to issue orders, etc.

Jyeshta is governed by Mercury which rules both the 6th and 9th house. 6th house signifies service; 9th house is negative (12) to the profession house 10. Hence, to get relief in service, the lords of 6 and 9 will prove to be helpful. Further, Mercury was in the constellation Poorvapadra, ruled by Jupiter owning the houses 3 and 12 indicating changes in the routine.

Mercury owns the houses Gemini-Mithuna and Virgo-Kanni. It is to be remembered that Rahu or Kethu in Gemini or Virgo will offer the results themselves, and during the period of the planet in its constellation. Kethu is in Gemini. It represents Mercury, the lord of the sign and Jupiter, the planet which aspects Kethu. Hence, Kethu is the agent of Mercury and Jupiter who indicates change in the routine, transfer, higher education training or probation.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=279|p. 279]]

Mars and Jupiter are in Kethu stars, Aswini and Moolam respectively.

Mars Dasa, Mercury Bhukti, Mars Anthra shows that you should have a transfer. Mars, the lord of 4 in the 3 house [sic] indicates change of place. Mercury, the lord of 9, promises further study.

Hence, on a Jyesta star day you are relieved.

To your ascendant, Moon, the lord of 7, with whom you have to deal, was transiting in the 11th house. So the officer was helpful on that day and relieved you.

Moon is the lord of 7. It was in the constellation of Venus who rules the 11th house counted from the 7th house. So when the lord of the 7th transits in a sign, eighth from the sign occupied by it in the horoscope, and if that lord is in the constellation of the lord of 11 counted from the house which it owns, it shows separation. The period was Mars dasa, Mercury Bhukthi, Mars Anthra-Mercury was in Jupiter's star. Hence, on a Jupiter day, Thursday, on Jyesta star day ruled by Mercury, when Moon was in Scorpio owned by Mars, the officer had to relieve you.

The moment when you were relieved also needs explanation. The lagna was Aquarius at 3-30 PM. on 30-6-65 [sic]. It was in the 1st degree. According to Krishnamurti System, 1st degree Aquarius is Dhanishta star ruled by Mars and the sub belongs to Mercury. (Suppose a child is born with Moon in the 1st degree of Aquarius. Then according to Vimshotari Dasa, what dasa and bhukti will the child have at the time of birth? Is it not Mars dasa, Mercury Bhukti? So that area is ruled by Mars and Mercury). The general rule is that Chandra ashtama day is evil and that, one cannot have his desire fulfilled.

The students can verify in all horoscopes and invariably they can find that results depend on the lord of the constellation in that sign and the general statement fails miserably.
