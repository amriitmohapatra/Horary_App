# Mercury's Transit

(Normally it takes one month for Mercury to transit from one sign to the other. But it is such an erratic planet that occasionally it completes within 20 days or it takes even more than a month. On an average it takes only one month).

1. Education gets spoiled; there may be disharmony with relatives and there should be a control over the tongue.
2. Receipt of money; relishable dish and gains by one's oration.
3. Disharmony and dispute with enemies, disagreement with higher officials and also humiliation from opponents.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=31|p. 31]]

4. One can make money and enjoy life.
5. No peace of mind; disharmony in domestic circle and also anxious.
6. Attempts come out successful. Gain of money and also due to speech makes money.
7. Gets exhausted; there may be separation from close relatives and also friends; disagreement.
8. Very happy, due to success.
9. Relatives disagree; health fails.
10. Maintains good health. Increase in income.
11. Happy at home and makes money outside.
12. Irritation; disagreement; dispute; disease; ill-repute
