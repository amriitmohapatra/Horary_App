# Transit of Ascendant and Ruling Planets

Today 20-9-67 is Wednesday governed by Mercury. Moon transits at 353°-34' in Mercury star Revathi in Meena rasi owned by Jupiter. Time is 6 p.m. Lagna is in Pisces, Lord of which is Jupiter. Therefore, Jupiter, Mercury and Rahu rule this moment.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=227|p. 227]]

Place: - Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, Curzon Road, New Delhi. Peon enters the room; he announces "Sir, from this afternoon, there is no fan, light; electricity failed".

One student gets up. Another writes in a slip. The third asks "Sir, now we have Sunlight before it gets dark, please predict, when the electric light will come? If not, shall we go home".

Another says, "Sir, if you ask us to wait, we all will wait. But, let us know the time when it will come".

The ruling planets are:

(a) Lord of the star now Mercury - Revathi

(b) Lord of the rasi - Jupiter - Pisces.

(c) Lord of the day - Mercury - Wednesday.

(d) Sub lord of Moon Mars; Rahu is in Mars sign, nowadays take Rahu as long as Rahu is in Aries.

Take the point ruled conjointly by the significators; Mars and Mercury offer results in the earliest opportunity).

Now, the point that rises in the East is in Pisces. As Mars and Rahu, do not have stars in Pisces, take Mercury star earliest sub belongs to Mercury. Sub - sub Rahu.

Pisces : - 330° to 360°

Revathi: - 346°40' to 360°

Mercury sub : - 346°40' to 348°-33' 20"

Rahu sub sub : 347°43' to 348°.

This Nirayana position is to rise in the East. Krishnamurti Ayanamsa is 23°-18'. So Sayana Lagna should be Aries 11°-1' 11°18' Aries [sic].

Refer to Krishman's [sic] table of Houses, Aries 11°1' will rise at 18hrs. 31 mts. 7 secs, L.M.T. at Delhi 28°-38' N and 77°12' E.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=228|p. 228]]

Therefore, the difference between L.M.T. and I.S.T. = 21 mts. 12 seconds.

If we add this to the figure we get 18 hrs 52mts. 19 seconds. 11°-18' is over by 11 hrs. 53 mts. 20 seconds [sic].

I predicted that it would come at 6-53 p.m. I.S.T. and it did come. Tube light will take a few seconds. We have to use common sense also.

This prediction given to the class regarding the supply of electricity at 6-53 p.m. by the Professor on Wednesday 20-9-67 at 6 p.m. came very correct to the minute. We endorse our appreciation.
