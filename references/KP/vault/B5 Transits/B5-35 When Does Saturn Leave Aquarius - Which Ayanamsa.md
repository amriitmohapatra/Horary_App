# When Does Saturn Leave Aquarius - Which Ayanamsa

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=359|p. 359]]

**Question:**

Sir,

Regarding the sex of the child to be born, one may fail when one predicts. We can excuse because the child is in the womb of the mother and one cannot see it. One can try to predict and may fail. But the movement of Saturn from one sign to the other is a matter which anybody can see, and can mention when it actually enters into a particular constellation or sign. There are many modern equipments and instruments to aid our vision, yet how is it that each person gives his own date for the transit of Saturn to Pisces. Prediction may vary. But how can the transit of a planet from one sign to the other differ so widely? When Raphael Ephemeris is a correct one, why should not we follow the correct Ayanamsa and use Raphael's Ephemeris?

**Answer:**

Dear Sir,

Generally the date on which Saturn enters into any sign is worked out by referring to the Raphael's Ephemeris and deducting Krishnamurti Ayanamsa therefrom. Each person follows his own Ayanamsa and claims that his alone is correct. Each wants to establish that it is correct by taking instances and then applying only the Western system of prediction. That is, they take the relative position between the cusp and the planet or between one planet and the other and offer the prediction. They should note that whatever be the Ayanamsa, the relative position will be the same. The readers are aware of this fact and such an argument is meaningless.

If the difference in age between husband and wife is 6 years, at all ages the difference will be six. Similarly the relative position between two planets will be the same whatever be the Ayanamsa.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=360|p. 360]]

Therefore, to establish that a particular Ayanamsa is correct by taking only the relative position is not the proper method. Therefore, one has to follow Stellar Astrology and especially the "sub" propounded by Krishnamurti. Then let one note down, when a person is running 'A' dasa, 'B' bhukthi and 'C' anthra; on which day in which star and in which rasi and lagna, he has got a child. The significators must agree. Again note the dasa and bukthi at the time of marriage and find out the ruling planets of the partner. They also must agree. If you find that there is some disagreement, immediately note down the lord of the constellation in which the planet was. If the lord of the constellation would be stronger than the planet itself, then the lord of the constellation, will rule the star or lagna in which the partner ought to have been born.

If one were to note down the results according to Stellar Astrology, using various Ayanamsa (which gives different periods of dasa at the time of birth), one will find that Newcomb is correct. I follow only Newcomb. The Ayanamsa which I follow and which is followed by Mr. Lahiri, or late C G Rajan and Madathu Panchangam are all more or less the same. The difference is negligible. There is no meaning in finding fault with others but we have to do good by following the correct one. We should not do any disservice by being adamant. Let me say that Saturn enters into Pisces Meena only on 7th April, 1966.

There is a Holly place called Thirunallar in South India in Tamilnadu. People visit the place, saying that the Emperor Nalan completed his 7.5 years Saturn period and his sins were washed off and he was freed from such evil results of Saturn by visiting Tirunallar. Most of the people do not want to find out on which date actually Saturn moves from one sign to the other but they simply follow a particular calculation which needs correction.

Whatever be the day when Saturn transits from one sign to the other, for one to pray to God, it does not matter whether he does it exactly on the same day or a few months earlier or later.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=361|p. 361]]

Anyhow the prayer will give mental solace and therefore, those who would like to visit Tirunallar can very well visit the place according to the timings given by Madathu Panchang or C G Rajan's one. Vakya Panchang and any other statement by the Astrologers using incorrect Ayanamsas may be ignored for our advantage.
