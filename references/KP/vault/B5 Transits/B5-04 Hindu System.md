# Hindu System

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=26|p. 26]]

What Westerners call as 'Transit' is known as 'Gocharapalan' by the Hindus. As already mentioned, Hindus take only the position of the Moon, i.e., the sign which it occupies, whether it is in the very beginning or in the middle or in the end of the sign. The movement of planets are also taken sign by sign, and never the relative position or the exact longitudinal distance between Moon and the planet is taken for consideration. The effects of the Transit of each planet is given. It is also mentioned that whenever Sun and Mars enter into the neighbouring house they offer the result immediately, whereas Jupiter and Venus while transiting in the middle of the sign will offer the result indicated. Saturn does not give as soon as it enters in a sign; but only at the time when it leaves the house. But Moon and Mercury, always give the results whether it is in the beginning or in the middle or in the end of the sign. The extent to which a planet can give its results would be according to the strength or weakness, followed by Hindus, whether it is exalted or Vargothamamsa or Swakshetra or in neutral sign or friend's house or enemy's camp or debilitated. The position of the sign denotes only the extent to which it can do good. One should not mistake that exalted planet will not offer evil results whatever be the position which it occupies. In that case whenever Jupiter is exalted, the whole world should thrive; none can die; none can starve; every one must be successful. How can it be possible? In the court of Law, one can win, the other is to lose, for both, Jupiter is exalted. Therefore, we should not interpret that exalted planets will invariably do good. Similarly also we can't say that the debilitated planets will do harm. It is absurd, if one were to interpret in that manner.

Again it is said that one should first consider the Dasa System which is superior, and then alone find out what one can enjoy by the transit. Our Sages are of opinion that unless the dasa agrees with that of the transit what ever is said for transit system will not operate. That is, unless the dasa is good, favourable transit cannot do good. Unless the dasa is bad, adverse transit cannot cause adverse results. Here I differ from them. Transit and dasa must agree. Their method of judgement is wrong. If one follows K.P, he can understand that both agree hundred per cent and the transit is useful to find out the exact moment of the result and it is just like a second-hand to note when exactly a matter will materialise. If dasa can be compared to a day, Bhukthi may be compared to hour, Anthra to minute and transit to second.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=27|p. 27]]

Again there is another dicta by which one is to modify by noting whether the results indicated by a planet transiting in a particular sign in relation to Moon, will operate or not. This is said as Vedha, Sun offers advantageous results while it transits in the signs 3, 6, 10 and 11 counted from Moon sign. But the same result will not operate if Sun is in 3 and some other planet is in 9. Sun is being aspected by any planet transiting in the 7th house, counted from the sign where Sun was. So if Sun is in 6, no planet should be in 12. When Sun is the 10th sign, 4th house should be vacant, If sun is in 11 counted from Moon, the 5th counted from Moon must be vacant. Similarly for Moon, it is said it can offer beneficial results when it transits in the signs 1, 3, 6, 7, 10 and 11 counted from that of Moon. The Vedha position for Moon is 5, 9, 12, 2, 4 and 8 respectively. That is, say, if Moon transits in the 7th sign counted from the position of Moon at the moment of the birth, then no planet should be in 2 counted from birth Moon.

For Mars, the houses 3, 6 and 11 are auspicious and the Vedha positions are 12, 9 and 5 respectively.

For Mercury the signs 2, 4, 6, 8 and 11 are favourable; Vedha positions are 5, 3, 9, 1 and 12 respectively. For Jupiter, the favourable signs are 2, 5, 7, 9 and 11; and the Vedha positions are 12, 4, 3, 10 and 8. For Venus, the signs 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 11, 12 are all favourable. The signs 6, 7, 10 are unfavourable; Vedha positions are 8, 7, 1, 10, 9, 5, 11, 6 and 3 respectively.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=28|p. 28]]

Saturn can do good when it transits in 3, 6, and 11: Vedha positions are 12, 9 and 5. There is an exception in this. No Vedha position need be considered if Vedha is between Sun and Saturn; and also between Moon and Mercury. That is, if Sun is in the third house and Saturn is in 9, then Sun will give the beneficial result and Saturn will not rob away. So also, if Moon transits in 10, it is advantageous and if Mercury were to be in 4, it will continue to do good and there is no Vedha.

Now, let us consider various results given for each planet when it transits in each sign, counted from the position of Moon.
