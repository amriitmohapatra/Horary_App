# Moon's Transit

(Generally Moon transits each sign in 2 [?¼] days. During this time one has to expect the following results).

1. Sound sleep; delicious food; gets clothing and gains jewelleries.
2. Humiliation; expenses outweigh; obstacles.
3. Sound health; makes profit, successful in attempts.
4. Indisposition; aimless touring; anxious; worried.
5. Health fails; attempts given up; mentally worried; depressed.
6. Pleasant; happy domestic life; gains money; health maintained.
7. Enjoys life; makes profits.
8. Health fails; disease; dispute; depression; loss of money sleeplessness.
9. Afraid of enemies.
10. Gains money; successful in attempts.
11. Friends, relatives and feast at home.
12. Expenses outweigh.
