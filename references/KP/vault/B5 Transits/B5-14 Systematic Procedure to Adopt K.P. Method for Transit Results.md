# Systematic Procedure to Adopt K.P. Method for Transit Results

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=187|p. 187]]

One must get the following data to have a correct chart. (1) Place of birth (2) Time of birth (3) Date, month and year.

If the birth is between midnight and the next sun rise, confirm, at 12 midnight which calendar date was over and which calendar date, sun is to rise.

Take Krishnamurti Ephemeris where sidereal time and position of planets are given for every day and 'Krishman KP Tables of houses'.

Use only Krishnamurti Ayanamsa. Work out. Erect the Nirayana chart and a ready reckoner for Dasa and Bhukthi, etc. Let us take one horoscope. The particulars needed are as follows:-

**Step 3:** Note the Planets tenanted In the constellation of occupants from first bhava (or house) to 12th in an order.

I. Saturn in Swathi, IV. Venus in Anuradha, Mars in U.P.Pada. Mercury in P.Shada. None in Moon Star, V. Moon in Visaka, None in Sun star, Rahu in Ashlesha, VII. Jupiter and Sun in moola, VIII. Kethu in Dhanishta.

So, Planets occupy only the houses 1, 4, 5, 7 and 8. Planets deposited in any of the constellation of the occupants are the strongest significators of the bhavas 1, 4, 5, 7, and 8. Then come the occupants.

**Step 5:** Note the planets in the constellations of the owners of the signs where the cusps fall

- I. None in Moon star
- II. None in Sun star
- III. Rahu in Mercury star
- IV. Mercury in Venus star
- V. Kethu in Mars star
- VI. Moon in Jupiter star
- VII. Venus and Mars in Saturn star
- VIII. Venus and Mars in Saturn star
- IX. Moon in Jupiter star
- X. Kethu in Mars star
- XI. Mercury in Venus star
- XII. Rahu in Mercury star

So, there are planets in the constellation of the owners of the houses 3 to 12. So these planets deposited in the stars of the owners are stronger than the owners to offer the results of the bhavas owned by them. But the Moon and Sun have none in their stars. So they themselves, have to give. Planet occupying a constellation is Deputy Secretary who is to execute what the lord of the constellation, (Secretary with a particular portfolio) indicates by occupation or ownership. If the Dy.Secretary is absent, Secretary does. So also if there is none in its stars, the owner gives.

**Step 6:-** Assort, arrange and prepare a ready reckoner for each planet to know which bhava results each planet will give when its dasa, bhukti, Anthra operates and which planets will offer the results of a particular bhava. To note the order of the significators, this is the rule. That is,

(1) Planets in the constellation of the occupants. (2) Occupants, (3) Planets in the constellation of owners. (4) Owners, (5) Very weak but yet those connected with a bhava are those planets conjoined with or aspected by the significators found out already.

For reading Transit results according to Krishnamurti Padhdhati, the following procedure is mentioned for students so that they do not miss any point but ever come out successful.

There are 27 constellations.

- 1, 10 and 19th stars are governed by Kethu.
- 2, 11 and 20th stars are governed by Venus.
- 4, 13 and 22th stars are governed by Moon.
- 5, 14 and 23th stars are governed by Mars.
- 6, 15 and 24th stars are governed by Rahu.
- 3, 12 and 21th Stars are governed by Sun.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=191|p. 191]]

- 7, 16 and 25th stars are governed by Jupiter.
- 8, 17 and 26th stars are governed by Saturn.
- 9, 18 and 27th stars are governed by Mercury.

Kethu is the significator of the houses 8, 10, 5, 7 and 4. Hence whichever be the planet that transits the Constellations governed by Kethu. i.e. whichever planet passes through the Stars 1, 10 and 19. i.e. 0° to 13°-20'; 120° to 133°-20'; 240° to 253°-20', these transiting planets offer the matters signified by the houses 8, 10, 5, 7 and 4. Kethu, the significator of 8, 10, 5, 7 and 4 offers these matters during the occasion whenever planets transit in any of its three stars; the Source is indicated by the planet transiting.

Suppose 8th house indicates money of the partner; 7th denotes partner and 4th house business of partner, then 8, 7 and 4 indicate the above matters. If significator of 5 transits, partner gains through music or speculation or through children. If the 12th house significator transits, partner makes money by service or by borrowing, making money by husband or wife or partner is shown by Kethu being the significator of the houses 8, 7 and 4. How the money is made, is indicated by the, houses signified by the transiting planet.

Next take 13°-20' to 26°-40'; 133° to 146°-40'; 253°-20' to 266°-40'.

They are called Bharani, Poorvapalguni and Poorvashada Stars. These are ruled by Venus. Venus is the significator of the houses 4, 11, 7 and 8. Hence any planet transiting in these constellations will bring out such matters of the houses 4, 11, 7 and 8. If in a housewife's chart, the above transit is found, it means, to her husband it shows 1, 2, 10 and 5 houses. So the husband may start Brokerage and jobbing business in Stock Exchange; or open a Book Shop in Race Club at the time when the 12th house significator of the housewife i.e. 6th house significator of the husband transits.

[[J_KP reader_5_transits.pdf#page=192|p. 192]]

Suppose significator of the 6th from 7th enter into the constellation belonging to Venus, do not expect the result on the day of entry. You have to wait till the significator of the 6th comes to the sub belonging to the significator of 11. If the significator of 6th house is Jupiter and it has entered in Poorvapalguni (133°-20' to 146°-40') constellation, till it touches Venus Sub and Mercury Sub-sub or Mercury Sub in Venus Star, one cannot be promoted, even though both Venus and Mercury are the significators of 11 and Jupiter, the transiting planet is the significator of 6. Mercury Sub in Venus Star commences at 144° and extends up to 145°-53'-20".

Star 3, 12 and 21 are ruled by Sun. They are called Karthik, Uthrapalguni and Uthrashada stars. They extend from 26°-40' to 40°; 146°-40' to 160°; 266°-40' to 280°. Sun is the significator of the houses [?2], 5 and 7. Whenever any planet transits, it shows love affairs, romance, payment of share or dividend to the partner. Love affairs will be pleasant when the significator of 7 transits the sub of 11th house significator. But when the same planet transit in the Sub of the significator of 6th house, the girl may go away and there will be separation till it comes to the sub governed by Significator of 11.

Saturn is the significator of 7. It moves slowly. So the duration will be long when Saturn transits. Sun is also the significator of 7. So when Sun transits in the same constellation and sub the duration of separation will be short

Kethu and Jupiter transiting cause separation for a longer period.

Stars 4, 13 and 22 are governed by Moon. They are called Rohini, Hastham and Sravanam. Moon is the significator of 4, 1, 5, 6 and 9. If the significator of 5th house transits either 40° to 53°-20' or 160° to 173°-20' or 280° to 293°-20' one will go on leave, make long journey, reaches his native place and remains there.

Whenever Kethu transits whichever matter is signified by the lord of the Star, Kethu offers through the matters signified by it. Kethu is the significator of 7th house. So, through partner or opponent (indicated by 7th house), the matter indicated by the lord of the constellation comes through. But it cannot come through 11th house matters whereas the same position transited by Venus or Mercury can give.

Whenever Sun transits, the matters signified by the lord of the constellation will come through, through the matters signified by Sun i.e. 2 or 5 or 7.

If Moon transits a particular constellation, whatever the lord of the constellation signifies will be given by Moon during its transit through the matters of the houses signified by Moon i.e. houses 4, 1, 5, 6 and 9.

If Mars transits a constellation, whatever the lord of the constellation, signifies will be offered by Mars through the matters signified by Mars i.e. 4, 8, 7, 5 and 10.

If the lord of the constellation indicates 'income' or receipt of money if the significator of 3rd houses transits, the income will be by M.O. or cheque; from brother or neighbour; from editing or publishing, by Brokerage and so on.

If the significator of 4th house transits, one gets as rent for the house let by him or one gets the yield or from mother.

If the significator of 5th house transits, one can have the income through music, opera, cinema or speculation or through children.

Thus the source is indicated by the transiting planet and what will happen is shown by the lord of the constellation, whether it will materialise or not, whether it is favourable or not, is indicated by the sublord according to the houses signified by the sublord.
