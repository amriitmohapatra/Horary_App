# Your Son Is Not Robert Clive

"Sir, I have a son. Very naughty fellow, very bad chap. I do not know what to do with him. He is a problem. Please advice me. Some say that Robert Clive in his youth was mischievous: but later, he had wonderful career."

"Have you got the chart of your son ?"

"Here it is, Sir."

"Dear friend, God sends children for many many purposes.

They are God's apostles sent forth daily to preach love, hope and peace. It is not only to keep up race they are born : it is also to enlarge our hearts and make us unselfish, as well as full of sympathies and affections. You should not allow them to have their own way but at the same time, you should not thwart them. If during childhood of your children, you feel that it is a headache to rebuke them and correct them, then when they grow up they will cause heartache to you. Children will, no doubt, commit mistakes; then and there they should be informed about the mistakes. They become wise, out of these mistakes and proper advice. We should think that boy of today will be the man of tomorrow and regular training is absolutely necessary. Therefore, every child must be made to repent for his mistakes. We should be careful. They should not be allowed to observe any of our defects as they are very shrewd observers and they will follow our footsteps. Hence with patience and great care, we should bring up children. The above advice is for all.

Characteristic and innate nature are found out from the sublord of the Ascendant. In a twin birth, one may be honest and the other may be opportunist and dishonest. As long as all the planets remain in the same position, in sign, star and sub and in the same houses, the only factor which moves fast within the few minutes interval of the children birth, is only the cusps of the houses. Hence we should develop the method by which the sub lord is taken and the reading is offered.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=252|p. 252]]

The houses 6, 8 and 12 are evil. They spoil the matters signified by the Ascendant.

If the lord of the sub of 1 is the significator of the first house, one will try to maintain dignity and will put down any enimical activity. The life will be long and also fortunate. Health will be good. Harmony and happiness are promised.

If the sub-lord of 1 is the significator of the second house, one will work hard for long number of hours and will be always after making money. One will be benefitted through industrious activities.

If the sub lord of 1 is the significator of the third house, there will be much mental development, One will gain by frequent-short journeys. Good relationship with kith, kin and neighbours. Though there maybe delay in coming out successful, one will be going on adding agencies.

If the sub lord of 1 is the significator of the 4th house, one will be interested in lands, buildings, estates, conveyances, mines, possession, furnitures, study, universities, etc. Good relationship with mother.

If the sub-lord of 1 is the significator of the 5th house, one will find delight in sports, music, opera, speculation etc. One will always be anxious to bring up one's children in the right path.

If the sub-lord of 1 is the significator of the sixth house, one will be having ill-health, one will be susceptible to diseases, one's action will cause loss to others, one can come out victorious in one's attempts and there will be disharmony.

If the sub-lord of 1 is the significator of the 7th house, one will have good partners in life. Good understanding with wife and partners. One will be fond of the other sex and try to be in their midst. One will have litigation in his lifetime. Marital happiness is promised.

If the sub-lord of 1 is the significator of the 8th house, one will have occult experiences and one's end will be unnatural. One meets with accidents. Survival or succumbing to accident depends on the period he runs. One gains through legacy but after great trouble. Nothing can be gained smoothly. There will be a lot of obstacles and hindrances.

If the sub-lord of the 1st is the significator of the 9th house, one is always desirous of proceeding on long journey. One will be religious. One will have higher studies. One can be successful in the research. One can have prophetic dreams. There will be gain through the relatives of one's wife, good relationship with them and the native's father,

If the sub-lord of 1 is the significator of the 10th house, one earns name and fame. Grand success in all undertakings. Pleasure of the superiors and those in power. One can rise to high social and professional position. Easy acquisition of inheritance.

If the sub-lord of 1 is significator of 11, all success. Many friends, smooth life, social and financial success.

If the sub-lord of 1 is the significator of 12th house, one has to suffer from secret unhappiness, secret enemies, and entertain the fear of imprisonment. One has to face misfortune. If the significator of 12 is also the significator of 2, 6 and 11 one will make fortune in a foreign place.

Thus it can be seen that the sub-lord of 1, if connected with 6th house, the person will behave in such a way that whatever he does it will be detrimental to all with whomsoever he moves, or he transacts. If the sub-lord is Mars, one will boldly and openly commit crimes. If it were to be Saturn, he will commit crimes without other's notice.

In your son's case, Saturn is in 6 and it is the sub-lord of the lagna. Further it is conjoined with Venus, lord of lagna. It is in a "Voice" sign. So he may at this age call girl students by name following behind them, and do such a mischief."

"Yes, Sir, this is one of the complaints I have [?received] from the parents of girls."

Saturn conjoined with Venus indicates that he can knock away other's footwear, chapals, etc.

"It is also true. He finds pleasure in going to music concerts with his one of the old chapals ; he attends the concert at my expenses and a few minutes before the concert is over he leaves the hall first; invariably he puts on a new one left there by somebody and he returns home. How did you predict chapals, Sir ?"

"Saturn and Venus are in 12 to 7. Seventh house shows others who participate in the music. 12th house is foot, Saturn and Venus indicate skin, hides and so leather chapals. Both of them are in the constellation of Jupiter in 3. Third house is for walking ; third house is assistance. I said further he will be capable of copying others or sign like others. He will not hesitate to sign your own signature in his progress report."

"Do you know my son? Have you heard of anything about my son, Sir"

"Why?"

"As though you are one of the members of our family, knowing his mischief, you are mentioning one after another and all are true. Now, Sir, I want to know whether he will improve or he will continue to be so ".

"For this one is to find out which dasa will run in the future. If the lords of the dasas are beneficial, even though he may have such innate nature, he will not have opportunity to commit crimes. If the dasas are evil, he can never improve. But he is to run Jupiter and Saturn dasas, from his age 25 to his age of 60. Both Jupiter and Saturn are to spoil him by committing the crimes".

"Then what shall I do with the fellow? How is he to live? What is the best job for him ?"

"Never put him in a bank, so that he has no chance to handle cash. Never allow him to be a lecturer or a clerk in any college. He will bring a bad name to himself and to another person of opposite sex. Never put him in any commission business as he will misappropriate the whole amount and will not remit to the head office. what is due to them deducting his commission Never put him in any business, as he will deceive partner and cheat the customers."

"Then what to do with this fellow ?"

"Let him work as the time-keeper in an industry and one to take attendance etc. as Meridian is in Saturn sign (Labour);

Mars star Dhanishta (Industry) Mercury to inspect or to keep accounts or to note down. The accounts unconnected with money is only the time-keeper's post".

"Oh! God. Some people told me that such mischievous people thrive well in their lifetime. But they did not so clearly mention the nature of the mischief which he does as you enumerated".

"Mr., your son is not Robert Clive. Simply follow what I told".
