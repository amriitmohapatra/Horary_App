# Will I Marry a Career Girl

One will marry a career girl if the sublord of the 4th Cusp is the Significator of 8 or 12 or 4.

In this case the Sublord is Mars, who is lord of 8 and it is in the Constellation of Mercury, lord of 4, sub of Saturn, lord of 12 in 12th Bhava. It means when you take the Sublord of 4th Cusp, and judge, it will be the Significator of 2, 6, or 10th house counted from the 7th house. Then the girl will serve or work or do business.

Husband and wife may both be teachers, doctors, lawyers and shareholders of business.
