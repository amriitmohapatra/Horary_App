The second issue was born on 16-11-1952 At that time Moon Dasa, Venus Bhukti, Jupiter Anthra was running,

All people having Saturn or Rahu or Ketu in 1 or 5 need not lose the first offspring. But, note the sublord of the nodes or Saturn in the Ascendant. If the sublord is the occupant or owner of 1, 4 or 10th house, then the first issue is short-lived or the first conception is abortive or short-lived. Prof. also explained how to find out whether it is abortive or still born or short lived. He has shown that if the conjoined period of the significators of the houses 6, 11 and Bhadakasthana operate during the intra-uterine period, then it will be abortive, if the conjoined period operates just before the delivery, it is still born. If it coincides immediately after delivery, infantile mortality: whenever they operate at that time the death of the first issue.

In this horoscope of the mother, Ascendant is at the 20°-46' in Sagittarius. Saturn is in the Ascendant at 27°-21' in Sun's star Moon sub. Moon is occupying 4th house which is twelve to 5. Therefore, the first conception was disappointing.

For second conception judge the houses 2, 5, 7 and 11. 7th is the 3rd from 5th : 3rd is the younger child : i.e. next conception by mother.

Second house is vacant. Saturn, lord of 2, has Mercury and Jupiter in its constellation.

5th house is unoccupied. Venus the lord of 5, has Moon and Mars in its star.

7th house is occupied ; Sun, Mercury and Jupiter are in 7th house : Saturn is in Sun's star. Rahu in Mercury Star; Sun in Jupiter star.

Mars is lord of 11 : None in its star.

Hence those planets connected with houses 2, 5, 7 and 11 are Mercury, Jupiter, Moon, Mars, Saturn and Sun.

Now, find out the sub occupied by the Significators, especially those connected with 7th.
