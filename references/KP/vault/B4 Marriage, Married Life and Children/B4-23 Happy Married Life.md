# Happy Married Life

## Happy Married Life

1. Jupiter or Venus being the lord of the sub of 7th cusp and being significator of 2 or 11.

2. Mercury, Moon, or Sun being the significator of 7 receiving beneficial aspect from 11th house significator.

3. Sun or Moon in 7 with mutual good aspect from the other being the significator of 2 or 11,

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=85|p. 85]]

4. Either of the luminaries in 7 aspected by Jupiter or Venus favourably being the significator of 2 or 11.

5. Venus, Mars and Uranus in good aspect is very advantageous provided they are not the significator of 6 or 10 or 12.

6. If Moon receiving good aspect from Saturn, Venus and Jupiter is auspicious. The couple will give expression to their pleasant feeling. Playful during union.

7. If the 7th house to Lagna or Moon sign is occupied by or aspected by benefics by lordship, one will have happy married life.

8. Venus in 1 or 2 or [?any] of the Upachaya sthanas, receiving good aspect shows peace, pleasure and prosperity after marriage provided it is not in the sub of the significator of 6 or 10 or 12. If it has both good and bad aspects, during the conjoined period of Venus and the planet forming good aspect one enjoys life. During the conjoined period of Venus and the planet forming evil aspect there will be disagreement and dissatisfaction.

## Married Life-Unhappy

1. If Sun forms evil aspect with moon and either is the significator of 2 or 7 or 11.

2. Malefics in 7 receiving bad aspect from the significators of 6 or 10 or 12.

3. Mars in 7 receiving bad aspect from Uranus, violence, separation-Saturn also forming evil aspect, divorce. So also, evil aspect to the 7th or 11th Cusp, brings misfortune.

4. Moon, Saturn, Uranus or Mars as significator of 7 having mutual adverse aspect or deposited in evil sub causes enmity during union.

5. Venus receiving bad aspect from the above planets does not allow pleasant union.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=86|p. 86]]

6. Moon forming disharmonious aspect with Jupiter, shows dissatisfaction.

7. Mars in 8th in the sub of Jupiter — Partner extravagant leads to financial trouble-followed by domestic trouble.

8. Mars in Moon sub threatens that the partner is short tempered, hasty and violent; cannot be controlled ; may be a drunkard especially when either occupies a watery sign.

9. Mercury in 7, afflicted by Moon, Mars, Neptune or Uranus, partner: nervous breakdown

10. 7th and 11th Cusp afflicted shows that the trouble will ever be there. Planets in 7 or 11 receiving good aspect show temporary harmony during the periods. If they also receive bad aspect, it is a hell.

11. 7th and 11th Cusp receiving good aspect shows mostly pleasure. Bad aspect to occupant will temporarily upset.
