# Sthree Dheerga Porutham

This agreement between the couple denotes satisfactory increase in their bank position after they get married.

It is declared that there is Sthree Dheerga Porutham if the boy's star is 13th and above, i.e., between 13 and 27, when counted from that of the bride. Some sages have said that there is Sthree Dheergam, if the male's star is between 8 and 27th, that is above the seventh star.
