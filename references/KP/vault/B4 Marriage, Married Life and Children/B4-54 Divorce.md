# Divorce

Divorce is a question which arises after one's marriage with the other legally. The cause may be any or many. Anyhow unless there is disagreement, disharmony and dissatisfaction among the couple, divorce will not come up. Just like opinions differ, so also husband and wife born in different families of different parents may not agree. The traditional method of finding out dasa-porutham will in no way, help the parents or the couple. Actually, if we note down the percentage of the people leading happy, pleasant, smooth life with wife with inseparable temperament, there are many who never consulted any astrologer. It may be an arranged marriage or it may be a love marriage. Consulting an astrologer, following traditional system, does not guarantee smooth, pleasant, united life with wife for a long number of years, bringing forth enough children to inherit the estates of the parents. To my knowledge, at least 60 to 70 percentage of the couple who had got married, after consulting the astrologers, who follow only traditional astrology, are fully dissatisfied and they do not find pleasure in the company of each other. In a few percentage, there is repulsion also, if the married life what we want is attraction and not repulsion. If an astrologer can change the fate of the couple, then Dasa-Porutham may be helpful. When the destiny is that they both must get married whether you consult the astrologer or not, where there is Dasa-Porutham or not, the marriage must take place and it does take place. When adverse time starts, both of them disagree. This disagreement may end in separation and the wife may be silent. This separation may end in violence : or this separation may be due to disease of either of the party. Therefore, as rewards [sic] divorce is concerned, one should know why they are destined to marry each other and then why they are destined to get a divorce. K.P. explains these facts crystal clear.

Houses 2, 7 and 11 are to be judged, for union (marriage), increase in the number of the members of the family by marriage and also to keep permanent tie of friendship with partner in life. These are judged from the houses 2, 7 and 11. But divorce is caused when the significators of 2, 7 and 11 are also the significators of the houses 1, 6 or 6 and 10 or 6 and 7 or 10 and 7 and so on. Therefore, the 12th house to the houses 2, 7, and 11 are all detrimental for smooth, pleasant family life with wife. Hence these houses are to be judged and then only one has to declare the result whether they will go for divorce. If the significators of 2, 7 or 11 happen to be the significators of 1, 6 and 12, then, separation will surely happen and then it may lead to divorce.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=196|p. 196]]

If separation is caused by the wife running away saying, that she has no mind to stay with the husband, then in one's horoscope, the significator of 7 will also be the significator of 6 and 9. If the person does not like wife, and he goes away, then, we have to judge the houses 3 and 12. 4th house is the place of residence. So 3rd is Vyaya to it. Therefore, one will go away from his residence proceed on a journey and thereby get separated.
