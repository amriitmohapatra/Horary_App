## Cant Change Fate-Marriages Two

Once a friend of mine consulted me to find out whether a boy's chart agreed with that of the girl. I cast the chart correctly and told him that Neptune was exactly in the 7th cusp of the boy and he has got the 7th cusp in Cancer on 16 degrees 56 minutes. I told him that this boy would be having two wives and the first wife should be a deceptive.

Since the girl was from a well known family, the boy's party did not give weight to my word and celebrated the marriage with that girl whom I said to be an unfortunate girl in having pleasure through husband ; but I emphatically predicted that there would be separation. The marriage went on in the morning hours. In Tamil Nadu, marriage will be celebrated during day time in the presence of relatives and friends. But actually they can contact themselves as husband and wife on a later date which may be a few days or even a few months later.

In this case marriage was celebrated in the morning and after that, the girl's party took the girl to their residence whereas the boy's party along with the boy reached their home, In the same week the happy celebration for them had been arranged in the night. When the boy was left alone with his wife in the closed bedroom, he found out that the girl was suffering from some threatening loathsome and contagious disease. Within a few minutes, he rushed out of the room and straight came to me. He told shedding tears "Sir, what you told will come true. My wife is suffering from such a disease, which is covered by face-powder. I found patches here and there in her body. I do not know what to do."

Later relevant certificates were obtained and he had the divorce, On a later date he married another girl. If the sub lord of the second cusp were to be in the constellation of a planet which is conjoined with Neptune such a disappointment and deception would have repeated. Luckily it is not shown. He lives happily with his second wife.
