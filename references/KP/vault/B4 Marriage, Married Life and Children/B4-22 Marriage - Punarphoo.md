# Marriage - Punarphoo

Whenever Saturn has got any connection whatsoever with Moon, there will be some obstacle or impediment, not only during negotiation but also at the time of fixation and even at the time of celebration of the marriage. This connection between Moon and Saturn causes delay, though ultimately both of them do much more good with a better partner.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=79|p. 79]]

## Example 1

On more an auspicious day [sic]. Let us see how Saturn acts. Instances are many wherein during negotiation, some 2 or 3 horoscopes are being considered to be most suitable ; we do entertain hopes one after another on all the three; all the three fail ; later on finally a fourth person is selected. It is one of the many results offered by Saturn.

## Example 2

Next, there are cases wherein the date of marriage is fixed and during the interval between the time of marriage and the celebration of the marriage, there will be some obstacle or the other. A few years ago, when I had been delivering lecture at Bharatiya Vidhya Bhavan, when there were many students attentively hearing me, two brothers entered, in a way, disturbed the class and presented a slip to me. In it, it was said that nearly two months ago, the marriage of the younger of the two had been fixed and it should be celebrated, nine days after the day when they met me. I do not remember the exact date, when it happened, though it is not difficult to find it out. Before this book is published, if I can find out, I will insert it in the last. The elder brother asked me to let him know, whether the marriage will take place on the date, already fixed or it should be postponed. I asked him "why do you doubt?" 'No, Sir, here is the horoscope and I also will give a number. That which worries us is that the girl, who is to be married ought to have had her monthly period even 8 days ago. Till now it had been dragging on and after all some 8 or 9 days more for the marriage. Therefore, unless she reports that she is in the monthly period within another 3 or 4 days, we will not be able to celebrate the marriage at the time already fixed. But we have to select some other day only after she takes her bath. We may have to reprint invitation. So whether we are to post all the invitation letters which are filled up duly, or are we to keep it, so that we can wait, see and if at all the girl's health will be alright we can post it ? So it is really a problem which had been put in the presence of the students and that too a problem, for which neither our Sages nor the Saints nor the savants of the Western countries have so far found out a method to announce the exact moment when the worry will end. But I immediately applied K. P. and told that the girl will have the monthly period commenced only on the same date when they put the query and further I added that they would get Trunk-call from Kumbakonam, at 10-35 p.m. saying that the bride had reported to them that she was in the monthly period. This headache, fear and anxiety were caused by the Punarpoo, that is the connection between Saturn and Moon, in the horoscope of both, the boy and the girl.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=80|p. 80]]

Now the readers would be eager to know what happened to the prediction.

It was really an astounding one. Correctly at 10-35, the bell was ringing, when a few of my students and both the brothers were just sitting outside, enjoying the cool breeze from the sea. When they heard the bell and the time was 10-15 p.m. [sic] not a minute earlier or later they rushed inside with full hope that what Prediction I gave, had come very true. They lifted the Phone, and the Telephone operator gave connection to Kumbakonam. They said "God is there. The bride has given green signal. So you can very well Post the invitation letters,". They added "Daughter had reported at 8 p.m. that she was in the monthly period. We tried to contact you from then. Only now it matured".

This is one of the many obstacles which is experienced by some percentage of the people, in spite of the fact that they take into consideration this affair while fixing up the date.

## Example 3

Once a father of a girl came to me and showed the horoscope of a boy living about 300 miles away from the bride's place. First, I told him that his daughter could not have her marriage celebrated in the year in which he consulted me and I added that the marriage would take place only in the next coming year. I continued and said that this boy who is working far away cannot marry this girl ; but her husband must live very close by, mostly in the same town. This year Saturn which connected with moon in the chart afflict the lord of the Dasa and Bhukti. So Saturn and Moon would cause Punarpoo.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=81|p. 81]]

Do you know what happened? It is very vary rare and such instances are very funny. In spite of my prediction, the father of the girl further proceeded and fixed up the marriage. The Bridegroom did not distribute invitation letters to one and all in his place. But he invited only a few personally. All arrived at the town, where the father of the girl was residing and in the evening according to the South Indian custom, the boy was to be taken to the temple, and then from the temple he should be taken by procession. (Probably, the idea behind our ancient's would be that this boy, who is to be married the next day, should be shown to many, so that they happen to be witnesses).

Around 6 p.m. the girl's father took the box in which the shoes to be presented to the bridegroom was. And then each person took something to be taken to the temple. All of them with music Nadhaswaram and Band went to the temporary accommodation wherein the party of the bridegroom were staying. The father of the girl invited everyone of them to come to the temple and the boy also came out with his friends.

In the bride's house, preparations were going on for a grand dinner in the night. And in the street a car had been adorned with Jasmine flowers and everything was beautifully arranged. The boy came out, stepped down to reach the street. One low-born ill-bred, but a clever fellow with his upper cloth on the hand, straight rushed to the boy, hurriedly put the upper cloth round his head and then he made a loop out of it and dragged the boy to the middle of the street. What a scene! what a confusion. Everybody was stunned. Nobody knew why such a thing had happened. But the father of the girl immediately recollected the prediction which I gave. (1) That this boy cannot marry his daughter. (2) In that year marriage will not take place. This was flashing in his mind.

Now, that person, who caught hold of the bridegroom gave blows after blows to the bridegroom and asked him "Do some thing to this girl. This girl was working in your quarters as a maid servant. You, a healthy and thirsty bachelor, when you were serving in the office, you lived in the office residential quarters. You tempted her; you spoiled her ; you gave her a child ; now what about this girl and what about the child. They are here, now. Unless you answer to this, I won't allow you to go and marry the other girl." So saying this person was going on dragging the bridegroom to a long distance away from the marriage-pandal though the bridegroom tried to resist. Guilty Conscience robbed away his strength. Nobody could venture to mediate. Even boy's parties stood aside being ashamed. They thought that any interference would be similar to throwing stone upon some filthy soil, which will emanate much of foul odor. So all were quiet, Somehow this bridegroom slipped away from all in that crowd. For hours he could not be seen. Later in the night, they came to know that he was in the waiting room in a railway station. While the bridegroom was dragged in the street, the bride's father got angry and he was so much annoyed that he threw the shoes on the head of the boy and immediately went to the kitchen and informed the cooks to stop cooking and went to the car which was intended for the procession, to remove all the flowers etc. and take back the car to the shed.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=82|p. 82]]

Saturn which caused Punarpoo had indicated that some such disturbance even to this extent, could be there, to upset the whole program and postpone the date of marriage or change the bridegroom. Therefore, whenever astrologers were to read the horoscopes of the bride and the bridegroom, you should see whether there is Punarpoo. If Saturn is deposited in the constellation of Moon and sub of Saturn or if Moon is deposited in Saturn's star and Moon sub, if Saturn is deposited in the houses 1, 3, 6, 7 and 10, then, such difficulties may arise. Normally we fix up the date for marriage. Due to some reason or the other we change the date, to suit the convenience of both the parties. So with pleasure, it is done. But there, where Saturn causes the delay, there will be much of expenses, mental worries etc. Therefore it is absolutely necessary, whether one can take a decision and accordingly act or one has to necessarily consider 2 or 3 dates, 2 or 3 horoscopes and at last when the time of marriage comes, then, to enter into a contract with the other party. One should proceed cautiously and slowly till the date of marriage nears.

## Example 4

While I was working in King Institute, Guindy, my colleague had a son aged about 24 to be married. He was working as the Inspector in the Transport Department.

No doubt, many parents of the girls of marriageable age approached him and one of them offered an attractive dowry. The father of the boy, my colleague wanted to see the girl. The girl's father showed the Photo of the girl. He was pleased and accepted the dowry. He gave me Rs. 400/- to purchase woollen suiting from Bangalore which place I had to visit on duty in a couple of days.

I bought the woolen things. Few days passed. In the meanwhile, the sister of the boy given in marriage was living in the next house to that of the girl with whom the marriage is arranged. That girl wrote a letter in such a way that the boy should reject the offer. She did not write to my colleague, her father, but she wrote to her brother, the bridegroom.

Since we were colleagues, his son knew me very well. So he narrated to me, what he heard from his sister and sought my advice. I told him "Go and see the girl. In your horoscope and the horoscope of the girl both Moon and Saturn are conjoined together. Her Star is the 5th star to that of yours. In that manner, it agrees. But there is the Punarpoo, Therefore, you go and see the girl. I am sure, you will reject her. After having received the money to reject an offer, there should be a sound and satisfactory reason. You can see the girl and reject. That is entirely left to you. Nobody can question it. So, you do like that," I replied.

He went to see the girl, taking the photo with him. According to photo she was thin and one could see the neck. But when he went to the girl's house and looked as the girl along with his sister, the bride was very fat. The photo was taken four years ago, and in those four years she had put on weight to the extent that the head and the body appeared together. The neck was short and stout. So he told the father of the girl, "Sir, even though this photo belongs to your daughter, she has put on so much weight that I don't like. Therefore, you write to my father and get back the dowry which you paid to him."

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=84|p. 84]]

Thus the marriage was stopped. She married another boy and he married another girl. This is the way how Punarpoo will act.

One may ask "Did you offer prediction for this match? " I need not say that they consulted me and I told even though there is the Poruttham, there is no Proptham.

I definitely told him that his son can never marry this girl. At that time, I had not been running a Magazine, so I could not publish then and there. Similar instances are many. According to the Traditional Astrology, 25 per cent of the population in the world will have the Dasa Poruttham to marry that girl because, say out of 100 people, 50 males 50 females. Out of 50 boys, in Dasa Poruttham, exactly 50 per cent of the horoscopes, must agree. therefore, when we see Poruttham, majority of the charts agree. But proptham, when you judge the ruling planets clearly indicate that one can marry only one of the other sex.

## Will the Married Life Be Happy ?

Excepting a few, most of the people, though they do not have good understanding with their partners, yet they pull on with dissatisfaction. A few lead an enviable life with inseparable temperament. A few misbehave and divorce.
