# Love and Life

## If the Sub Lord of the 5th Cusp Is in Aries

**Male:** You are extremely frank and enthusiastic in love affairs. Your good nature and charm will attract the opposite sex, You will take practical steps. You desire to have freedom and in love affairs and ever lasting affection. You can be happy, if your dominant nature is softened. Generally, you are impatient and emotional. Your love is very deep but if it is not reciprocated you are broke that is why many of you choose a wrong woman who will squander your money and also arouse your suspicion. Since you are also extremely bold and independent, you care for nothing, you do not hesitate to pick up a quarrel, you are not afraid of bloodshed. Your temper will be both short and hot. You can talk ; you can try to convince the other party. You will continue your efforts till you succeed or you fail miserably. You do not give up till you see the finish. Even during the time of misunderstanding you will start having an eye upon another girl. No doubt, if the wife proves to be faithful you will allow her to have her way in many matters. At the same time there will be limitations, so that break up of marriage will not come. Thus, you will prove to be a very strict as well as desirable partner. To please your wife you will make impulsive purchases, even on credit. Bad Arians will make mistake in love affairs though basically honest. If such an action indicates that you have broken your partner's heart, you will feel miserable and be unhappy. You will keep up the love affairs alive as long as your mate is willing to sustain interest in sex. If you find that she does not respond, you will satisfy your sexual pleasure elsewhere. You need flattery, anyhow you should cultivate tact ; you should understand the psychology of your mate. Generally you marry in haste and repent in the leisure.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=161|p. 161]]

**Female:** Like the male you also will have desire to dominate. You will be independent and act forcibly even directing the husband. You cannot relish subordination. You have your own principles and you act according to your conscience. You are thoroughly upset if you find that the partner is not honest but cunning and evasive. You will have the sane sexual inclination. You will be very passionate periodically. You expect mainly this from your husband so it is advisable to have control over your emotions. Normally it tempts you to have many affairs even before marriage. This you are aware even from your teen-age. It is advisable to have control over your emotion, anger and independence. Cultivate to give up pride and avoid speaking high of your own family. Do not expect so much that your husband should always attend on YOU, speak high of you alone and never speak well of any other lady, especially in your presence. If by chance, you are lucky and you marry a simple man, you will overpower and subdue him in all the activities. As the husband may be a spend-thrift, and he is the only earning member, you must save a portion of what he gives to you.

## If the Sub Lord of the 5th Cusp Is in Taurus

**Male:** You are not generally impulsive. You take long time to decide and select a partner in life. You will stick on to one after having made such a selection. And you will also leave no stone unturned till you come out successful. You try to lead a peaceful harmonious happy life. Yet there can be romance also in spite of yourself being faithful to your mate. Your emotions will be deep. You hate quarrel and confusion. You will be blessed with a loving wife much devoted to you. You will have affectionate and warm partner responding to your kind treatment. You enjoy company of only the pleasing member of the opposite sex. You are motivated by the thought of an honourable marriage, limited number of children gracious domestic environments and enough money. You are sensitive. You cherish your wife as the prized possession and in the privacy you are very amorous and extremely sensual. You will embrace her hungrily and satisfy her in full.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=162|p. 162]]

**Female:** Your sex life is closely indicated with your need for security. You will always be gracious in your approach to sex and love. You are sincere and emotional. Generally, early marriages meet with disappointment; divorce will be that last resort. You dislike to change anything. You try to have property and save. You cultivate that habit to be economical and increase the wealth. You do not mind about the black mailing of other people but you try to keep the home in apple-pie order (neat). Some of you will marry for material gain rather than romance because finance is of major interest to you. You are always mindful of making money and maintaining a pleasant home with necessary accoutrements/ equipments. Even if time passes you do not mind to wait till you find a man who will offer you enough financial security to ensure the fulfilment of your desires and dreams. You may also try to have business on your name or you may have partnership ; you will be very attractive, so you can make such a selection while finding out a husband for you.

## If the Sub Lord of the 5th Cusp Is in Gemini

**Male:** You are capable of making friends very quickly and also equally you will find fault with friends, that is why you take a long time to find out a suitable partner in your life. You will consider that marriage involves responsibility and you may not be able to undertake such an entanglement at an early age. Since you know that you are fickle minded, changeable and ambitious, you think that even though there is a proposal for marriage, you think why you should not wait for tomorrow, expecting that there may be something better. You consider sex as something to be enjoyed for the moment but throughout life it is a great burden. You also wish not to get yourself tied up to one; you prefer variety in life. The more the romances, the more happy you will be. So you will baffle your associates.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=163|p. 163]]

Naturally, the friendship gets broken, you care more to have an intellectual companion who will be steady and accommodative. You are fond of proceeding always with wife during short journeys. You expect your wife to agree with you whenever you want to change the surrounding and environment. You are also so clever that you will end any romance if you feel that that person is a source of only expenses, causing loss. You will desert over-bearing wife. You cannot tolerate constant pressure from her. As regards love affairs, you are like a child. You are at best when your wife understands you and behaves intelligently. You do not want to be a hen-pecked husband.

**Female:** You are generally intelligent. Your strongest appeal is mental companionship; you do not relish to be only a house keeper; even though you get married if you would have been already in service or well equipped in any service, you will not give up your outside activities. You feel miserable if your husband resents. You have to be more tactful if you want to lead a happy and harmonious life. As years pass on, occasions may arise when you have to choose either of the two, that is, to live along with the husband or to stick on with the job. If you give up the job then you take to domestic life with utmost sincerity and devotion : you will be highly refined.

## If the Sub Lord of the 5th Cusp Is in Cancer

**Male:** You will find that the life is not worth living without romance. You will sacrifice your comforts and remain loyal and affectionate. There is one defect in you. You cannot impress on the mind of the partner that you love her though you are sincere, honest and true. Your partner will consider you as feeble minded and changeable, even though you may be stubborn and determined. You enjoy your life in full. Marriage alone brings you a well settled life. You will be adjusting and accommodative, though occasionally exciting and fussy. Your wife will have the way for domestic comforts and prosperity. So you try to keep her cheerful and jubilant. You should try to please her in all ways. Though you are devoted to your wife, your interference in household routine will spoil your good intentions. The main defect in you is that you feel dissatisfied and so you indulge in criticism of some thing or the other. You are detached from reality. You prefer to remain at home and look after your aged parents with full devotion. To you, sex is incidental to your happiness and so you do not take up your sexual side even when there is the opportunity. You are less moody but a little shy. Your wife may leave the children under your care and go to any entertainment and you are a good husband to oblige her and a good father to attend upon them. You avoid divorce. Because you desire to maintain family life, putting up with all horrors of life, if you are unlucky.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=164|p. 164]]

**Female:** You are genuine in your life and sincere in your devotion to your husband. You can be a little moody ; yet you feel satisfied with anything offered by your husband. You prove to be a good mother but you share the responsibility of bringing up the children with your husband. You are adaptable, affectionate, beautiful, passionate, pleasing, sincere and sympathetic. You will never share any pains to attend to the necessity of your husband. In youth you will immerse yourself in romance, reading love stories which will stimulate and such poetry. You dress well, make up well, and love a person ; as you consider love is sex. You may criticise your husband for his financial instability if he does not provide properly; you accuse him. You can be easily pleased by your husband if he daily offers flowers and other small materials for your make up, etc. and also by celebrating your birthday etc, If he does not do, you consider that he no longer loves you. So, you retaliate and you do not allow your husband to satisfy himself sexually. If you think that he is using you for animal lusts you never yield but take bed in the next room. If you have to survive your husband, you do not mind taking a second person, as you want to lead a family life. You prefer to live with your children. You cannot be away from them. You are erratic in sex and thereby bewilder your husband. If you have lost two husbands you never hesitate to marry a third, not for sexual pleasure but for wealth. You make friends of your own sex. You find pleasure in keeping company with them just like one needs air, water and food. You need pleasure company. You are loyal and also emotional.

## If the Sub Lord of the 5th Cusp Is in Leo

**Male:** You are romantic. You love deeply and you will be carried away by love. You cheer up your wife most skill-fully like an actor in a comedy scene. You are an ideal lover as you will be fiery and passionate. You do not like any demonstration of love in public. You consider it below your dignity. You have abundant charm and you are attractive to the opposite sex. It is likely that your wife is jealous as you will be found in the midst of a crowd of ladies. Unless you make a proper choice of your wife, there cannot be proper understanding. She should be able to share your pleasure, when you give parties to others and you are excited in their midst. If your wife understands you and agrees with you, then you like her, love her and admire her. You will fit in domestic routine admirably. You will not allow anybody to disregard your wife. You are very proud of your family. You cannot tolerate any ill repute. You are always conscious of your social understanding and care for prestige. Understanding of each other is necessary to avoid any trouble in married life. Even though you may not like to be the master of your household yet you keep control of all bedrooms. Sexually you are demanding but always gracious. Your life will be sustained and marriage also will be happy if your wife returns your passion and show equal affection. You generally over-do. You should make attempts to right your wrongs. You will be an extremist, therefore you should cultivate such good habits to lead a harmonious life ; otherwise your disappointment will be great. And you may become embittered. Do not try to be too domineering.

**Female:** You are ambitious and ideal; you will manage your home admirably. You are a successful social worker. You can make favourable impression on all. Your devotion both to your house and also to the general public is supreme. You will be offered prominent position. You are a self-sacrificing lady with ever-lasting love. You cannot expect any good return from those to whom you lend your assistance at the risk of your own interest. You should not exceed your authority or spend lavishly. You must be kept under control. You will slowly appreciate your husband's good nature, affection, kindness and real love, to lead a pleasant progressive, prosperous and peaceful life. You may have pretty fixed ideas. And you may feel that your world is love and you will expect others to do only those which you desire and consider right. You should also know that your husband is your senior but not your servant. No doubt, you are loyal in marriage. Better you cultivate a good habit of proclaiming your husband's fine qualities to his and your friends, in order to make him happy you should also learn to forgive and forget as your husband cannot harbor any ill feeling or hatred. Your love for money is as large as the skill to spend it. You cannot save. You have a big heart, cheerful face and a spirit full of confidence. You will be upright, modest and chaste.

## If the Sub Lord of the 5th Cusp Is in Virgo

**Male:** You have your own ideals. It is always difficult to find one suiting to your taste. Therefore, there is long delay in selecting one to measure up to your ideals. You want everything to be perfect as for as you are concerned. Therefore, it is always possible that in the first few cases, you will be disappointed. Whenever you desire to make a selection you give more value to the intelligence of the person than to the sexual side and romance. You are a little shy and so you do not give expression to your affection. Do not be touchy ; cultivate to give expression to your love. You will not stand any criticism ; you demand good understanding but your temperament is to find fault with others. This must be given up. Just like you want others to appreciate your assistance and also intelligence so also whomsoever you love (whether intelligent or not), you have to admire her beauty (whether handsome or not), etc, and also give expression to your pleasure in her company. One step more you can go, you can make her feel that you are depressed and inactive in her absence you are rarely passionate. You should keep your partner cheerful, by your talk at the least and also fetch her such books to satisfy herself by reading. Your married life will be more on partnership basis. That is why many postpone their time of marriage and prefer to be a bachelor long period ; another reason is that you want to be very studious and do research work in a peaceful atmosphere ; but when once you get married, you provide everything for the wife and to other members of the family.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=167|p. 167]]

**Female:** You will be able to understand your husband properly and conduct yourself wisely, giving every encouragement to him, whenever he needs it. You are very adjusting. You will be a dutiful wife making the family life happy. You may be peevish and occasionally mean. You preach thrifty (sparing) to your husband and gradually he becomes economical within the few years after marriage. Your idea of marriage is legal partnership. That you should live in a business like way. You will run the home in a very regular routine manner without yourself wasting anything nor allowing others to waste, You do your duties quickly and most satisfactorily. You will not be able to please your husband in spiritual life, and also in keeping pleasant company. You consider that sex is mean, base and one day or the other troublesome. Therefore, this frame of mind is to be modified. You must fall in love with a person of your own choice ; then sex is one of the obligations. You do with no emotion there can be no exciting romance. You rarely relish him to touch you, even casually ; you are generally much regarded than liked.

## If the Sub Lord of the 5th Cusp Is in Libra

**Male:** You are the most lovable person in sex life. You are an expert in all love affairs. By your sincere love and beauty to capture, your partner beside your dress, passion, pleasant perfumery and sweet smile, it is your beauty which entices. Your passion grows strongly and also dies quickly. You enjoy immensely in the company of opposite sex. You find pleasure in good discussion. You can put forth your argument with clear vision and you are a pleasant conversationalist. You are cheerful, happy passionate and adjusting. You accommodate to a good extent, you provide the family with all necessities, comforts, luxuries and conveniences. You are very passionate and while expressing your love you reach the greatest height. You will feel very much disappointed if by chance response is not to that extent you expect. Whatever disagreement or dissatisfaction may be, you do not seek for divorce but somehow you try to adjust.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=168|p. 168]]

**Female:** You marry early in your life. You try to prefer the tradition. You are happy both at home and also in social life. Your standard of living will be high but you may not have a partner who can satisfy you. You are tactful and wise; you avoid hot discussion, you do not rub on the wrong side. You love and adore your partner and try to lead a luxurious life maintaining peace. You are ready to let the male do all the uncommon and disagreeable ones. You like to do such act and will never fail to thank him with a flush and smile. You are broadminded and cooperative. Your senses are always alert, energetic and understand your husband's needs. You can be pleased by flattery which is your weakness. You feel very happy when you are assured that you are the most beautiful. If your husband can understand you and keep you cheerful, you will richly reward him by intimacy.

## If the Sub Lord of the 5th Cusp Is in Scorpio

**Male:** You have complex moods; yet you are dynamic and energetic in romance and love affairs. You cannot have a pleasant and smooth life with the opposite sex, unless you are sympathetic and your affection is steady. If your partner appreciates you and speaks high of you, you consider that this world is really a Heaven to you ; but if she passes any criticism and disapproves your action you are depressed, disgusted, dejected, down-cast and broken down. Generally you give all comforts to your partner and try to keep her in good spirits. You expect demonstrations of affection from your wife. This is a bit too much as after all, she is a lady. Mostly you are highly sexed than people born in other sign. So you desire to have and satisfy yourself with the meanest type of all activities towards sexual side. There is one great good quality in you. You do not see any other girl except your wife. You are either a gentleman or a demon to your wife ; this depends upon her response and reciprocal action. You can indulge in prolonged periods of sexual excess, which would tell upon your health physically, mentally and emotionally. You should not be arrogant and also violent. You generally hit back hard and sting virulently, when provoked. You will prove to be an opportunist sexually, and romantically, so, before marriage you make some enemies.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=169|p. 169]]

**Female:** You enjoy your life if your partner show equally genuine affection and deep love. If you have a scorpio born as your husband, you will be fully satisfied; then both of you know how to dance and adjust; if there is any disharmony among you it will not be made known to outsiders. You can more for honour and family prestige. You, no doubt, work very hard. You are really true. But you suddenly lose your temper and loudly retort without any consideration for the position of the husband. Love for you is your life force. You think that you live only for that. You despise a man who is un-masculine. You fade away if your husband is not loyal and you feel that you are leading a life in the hell. You are rarely dominated by your husband. You make all efforts to further your personal ambition and try to improve yourself in every way. You take intertest in the success of your partner.

## If the Sub Lord of the 5th Cusp Is in Sagittarius

**Male:** Generally you lose your temper quickly and you repent for it immediately. There can be now and then some rupture between you and your wife ; if your wise is wife, she can understand you properly ; conduct herself tactfully so that both of you can lead a pleasant married life. As you are very much interested in games, sports, societies and clubs and you spend much time in such activities, it is likely that your wife mistakes you and concludes that the family life is not so important to you as the entertainments. Unless your wife is broadminded, adjusting and accommodative, there can be misunderstanding, though you are a person of integrity and morals.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=170|p. 170]]

Love is also a pleasant game to you. You consider the sex as a very small matter. You will never utter a lie in love affairs. If by chance you develop some fancy towards another girl you will straightaway inform.

Anyhow you will look after your wife with care. You do not prevent her from taking part in any social activity. You will treat your wife as a good sport and you expect her to be reasonable and understand you properly. Whenever you are seen with other girls she should take that it is nothing but friendship and that there is no physical involvement.

You are frank and honest. You are not capable of flattering your wife and thereby cheering her up. But you are dutiful and sex becomes a part of your routine as a husband.

**Female:** You are deep and passionate. You have very in common with the male counter part. You do not demand anything in love or sex. So also, you expect that the husband should not demand anything. You take part in women's club. You are devoted to your pets at home. You have the courage and ambition ; so you prefer to drive the car and to dominate. You can have early marriage. You are fond of real work and outdoor activities. You prefer to be carrier woman. You are humorous and versatile. Main defect in you is that you are always curious about others affairs. You never suggest anything to your husband unless you are asked for, but you try to help him in all matters. You will be calm, competent and considerate whenever your husband is wrong. You politely bring the mistake to his notice and do it with all humility. You offer him advice in the last. Your husband will consider that he is very lucky to have married you. So you are happy. You are warm and you give much freedom to your husband. You also expect the same from him. You cannot tolerate any one restricting your activity ; then you will rebel. If you feel that you are not getting that treatment which you deserve you lose your temper, even though you do not break with your husband, you just continue to live with him dissatisfied.

## If the Sub Lord of the 5th Cusp Is in Capricorn

**Male:** You may have affection but you do not give expression to it. You are not fit for romance, dance, etc. You are never emotional. You are very slow and cautious whenever you approach the other sex. You are never bold or rash. Further, in love affairs you never take the initiative. You always ask the other party tactfully attain her consent then only you will start proceeding further. You will also realise that the other sex gets worried because of your hesitancy, therefore you take a long time to choose a partner. Your sense of duty exceeds your ability to express real love. You cannot be regarded as a warm affectionate person. You will have opportunity to enjoy voluptuous females. You are fond of the most erotic forms of love making. Generally you become enchanted with much younger girls. You are delighted to have such a young wife ; you will promise anything to her. You may have occasionally love affairs. You expect affection and approbation from your wife. You remain loyal if you understand that she is very much devoted to you. If she fails to satisfy you and there is another girl tempting you, then you will err. You have strong appetite towards other sex. It is only she who can put you at ease by making you slowly understand that after all sex activities are natural ones. If you do not find satisfaction with anybody, you satisfy yourself.

**Female:** You are of totally different type. You remain to be devoted house keeper. You dedicate yourself to the comfort of your husband and children. You can be a demonstrator for dance, yoga, love etc. You are ambitious and clever, so you restrict the number of members of the family by birth of children. You adopt family planning. You know the difficulty of finance. You never gamble; nor you take any risk. You can cut down the expenses to the bear minimum. You enjoy harmonious and contented life, unless you marry a Scorpion born, you cannot fulfill your high sexual need. The next best would be Pisces born. You are deeply interested in all aspects of sex but you try to put on a show that you are indifferent. You do not give expression to it, nor make any one understand that you are interested in sexual pleasure. If one understands you and satisfies you in love affairs you will depend completely upon that person. He will win you outright. Never you are in haste but slowly and steadily you make the friendship. You will appear to be reserved, but it is only due to the fear of ridicule. You never entertain hopes as you are not optimistic. Unless things happen you never believe and have faith in anything. You have that patience and tolerance needed to come out successful as there may be delay and also obstacles.

## If the Sub Lord of the 5th Cusp Is in Aquarius

**Male:** You are intelligent and you prefer educated and equally intelligent partner. You are unassuming. You have strong attachment and a permanent one. You move with everybody very freely and you always prefer such a girl who is equally social, shrewd and studious. If you find that she does not come up to the standard you expect, then without even informing the part, you will try to make another choice. You never give expression to your love nor can you satisfy one whom you love, as the partner will be more passionate. You will be law abiding, true and honest. You are noble and magnanimous. If you realise that your partner expects something more from you, you simply sympathise with her. Nobody can fool you by flattery because you can find the motive of such people. You have your own individuality and mannerism. You follow your own principles. You will go ahead to do anything for your partner if it is within your bounds. You have to avoid solitude and never be worried or gloomy ; also do not be a pessimist. Enter into marriage with full understanding and commitment. As there are chances for being misunderstood, it is likely that your partner may let you down. Do not give room for such undesirable event. You need every encouragement. If she does encourage you, then you are the best husband. You will take up any responsibility boldly but yet you do not want to have a big family. You are more attached to your children than any other rasi (sign) born. You never hesitate but straightaway accept that girl the moment you find her suitable. You will straightaway tell her because you do not observe decency or sentiments.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=173|p. 173]]

**Female:** We find two extremes in the girls born in Aquarius; one will be very kind, modest and polite. The other is nothing but a devil in sexual experience. If you get a proper person you feel extremely happy. You need such. You are adaptable and affectionate. You love people. If by chance anybody tries to have you, you cannot exercise your will power, but due to your weakness you yield. If you find that any injustice is done to you, you rebel. You can have very happy married life, If your husband is equally learned and intelligent. If you by chance have a wrong mate, it will create a vital problem which you have to endure for your life time. You never rush to divorce or any other undesirable complications. You try to maintain the prestige of the family by your patience and make efforts to improve the existing condition.

## If the Sub Lord of the 5th Cusp Is in Pisces

**Male:** You are very emotional and also sentimental. You try to have a beautiful and intelligent wife who develops taste in fine arts. You want only broad-minded partners and you hate if your wife is niggardly (punny- mean) ; you always entertain some suspicion about your partner and occasionally you are jealous of her. It is the duty of your wife to understand your nature and try to give expression to her sincere love to you. She should flatter you to your satisfaction.

It is always advisable that you select your wife who is not free with everybody. Otherwise, you keep your love in suspense due to suspicion. Your temperament is also dull; during some moments you are warm ; in some moments you are reluctant. Some days, you admire and adore her. You are amorous and affectionate. Generally you are kind, sympathetic and loyal. You are normally good ; but occasionally moody. Throughout your life you will be attracted by the romantic side. If your wife is in any trouble, at a moment's call you lend your assistance. You do not think of doing harm, not that you are incapable but you consider it to be a sin. It is a problem to your wife to judge you as she will find contradictions in your nature. You have to avoid contemplating and dreaming always. You should avoid developing likes and dislikes. You will do all you can to maintain your family in a high standard to give absolute satisfaction to your mate and children.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=174|p. 174]]

**Female:** There are a few varieties of ladies born in this sign. You prefer to live in simplicity. You take no pleasure in any body's company. But you prefer to have a pet like a cat. You are philosophical. Somehow inferiority complex develops in you. Therefore, you select a husband who will be of great assistance to fight against your inferiority complex. He should bring out your best qualities and also express his love to you. Some of Pisces born will play dual part. If the sexual urge is not satisfied by your husband you will find some one who is compatible to the physical need. For some of you there will be lot of troubles. You will have trials and tribulations and you become depressed and pessimistic ; a few of you will try to have a new life with foreigners in another country. And they will be happy through some mysterious ways as you are very passionate. A few of you will conduct like a rat in the sinking ship. If you find that the partner is not up to the mark you will desert him especially when he is in difficulty. Some of you are prepared to sacrifice and forget your personal comforts if you find that the partner is devoted to you and completely depends upon you for peace and pleasure. You should cultivate the habit of keeping many matters within yourself. You should not confide anything with anybody. There is every likelihood of yourself being betrayed. Do not suspect your husband.
