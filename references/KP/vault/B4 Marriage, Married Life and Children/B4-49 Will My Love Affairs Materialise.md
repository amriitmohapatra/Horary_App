## Analysis

Moon in 3 in his own star and sub of Venus who is a significator for houses 2, 7 and 11 shows that the question relates to marriage. Venus sub shows it is a love affair.

Since the question relates to marriage, examine the sub lord of the 7th cusp. It is seen that Venus is the sublord of the 7th house.

Now, Venus is in 12 in the star of Jupiter in 2 and sub of Saturn in 9. Jupiter owns houses 6 and 9. Saturn rules houses 7 and 8. So, according to Krishnamurti Padhdhati, we should not hastily declare that Venus in 12 is bad. Actually, he signifies houses 2, 9, 7, 6, 8 in order of strength. Also, Venus owns house 11 and is posited in 12.

Hence even though matters appear unfavourable still Venus bring out success.

## Significators for Marriage

Houses 2, 7 and 11 stand for marriage.

Mercury, Kethu and Jupiter are in 2. Mars is in the star and sub of Mercury. Sun is in the star of Kethu. Venus and Rahu are in the star of Jupiter. Hence, Mars, Sun, Venus and Rahu signify house 2.

7th house is vacant. It is ruled by Saturn. Mercury and Venus are in Saturn sub. 11th house is vacant. Its lord is Venus. Mercury and Saturn are in Venus star. Sun and Moon are in Venus sub.

At the time of analysis, the ruling planets are:

- Lord of Day: Sunday ruled by Sun.
- Lord of Star: Moon
- Lord of Moon Sign: Virgo - Mercury.
- Lord of Ascendant: Aquarius - Saturn.

Therefore, the significators for marriage are Sun, Moon, Mercury and Saturn.

Rahu is in Saturn's sign and Kethu is conjoined with Mercury. We therefore find that Rahu, Kethu, Sun and Moon will bless the querist with marriage.
