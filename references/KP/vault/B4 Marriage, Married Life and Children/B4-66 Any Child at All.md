## Who Is Sterile?

The sub lord of the Asc. is Venus and the sub lord of the 7th cusp is Moon. Venus is in a mute sign and Moon is in a barren sign. So she is sterile. Can we confirm 7 [sic] Venus is in the star of Mercury and sub of Jupiter. But Moon is in the star of Mars and sub of Venus.

The ruling planets of the native is Jupiter, Mars, Mercury and Rahu. (i.e. Jupiter, Mars, Mercury and Rahu). The ruling planets of the female is taken as the significators of the 2, 7 and 11th house of the male, are Venus, Jupiter, Moon and Mars. The ruling planets at the time of judgment in Horary chart are Venus, Saturn, Moon, Saturn. As Venus and Moon are representing the female who were the ruling planets at the time of judgment confirms that she is sterile.
