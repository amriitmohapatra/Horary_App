# Profession of Partner

Consider the houses 4, 8 and 12.

- (a) Planets in the constellation of the occupants of the houses 4, 8 and 12;
- (b) occupants in 4, 8 and 12;
- (c) Planets in the constellation of the lords of 4, 8 and 12;
- (d) lords of 4, 8 and 12 show his or her profession.

From the significator's, decide the profession. In male's horoscope if Mars is a significator of the houses 4, 8 or 12, he will have a career woman as wife.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=90|p. 90]]

## Characteristics

Note the lords of the sign, star and sub where the 7th cusp has fallen. They indicate the characteristic of the first partner. The lords of the Sign, Star and Sub where the cusp of the second house falls indicate the characteristics of the second partner, if there are two marriages.

## Physical Features

Just like characteristic is judged, so also ascertain the partner's physical features from the lord of the sign, the lord of the constellation and the lord of the sub of the cusp of 7 for first marriage, cusp of 2 for second marriage and so on.
