# Two Wives

- (1) Lord of 1 or 7, weak—more than one wife.
- (2) Second house indicates second marriage. Lord of 2 in 9 and a malefic in 7.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=88|p. 88]]

- (3) Lord of 8 in 1 or 7.
- (4) 3 or 4 evil planets in 7.
- (5) Many planets in 7 unaspected by ford [sic] of 2.
- (6) Planets in exaltation in Lagna, Lord of Lagna in exaltation and malefic in 2.
- (7) Lord of 1, 2 and 6 in conjunction and malefic in 7.
- (8) Venus very strong in Lagna receiving aspect from Mars—boldly errs; Saturn aspecting Venus; shows that one is funky to go ahead.

As per K.P. if the sublord of 7th cusp is either Mercury or any planet occupying dual Sign, more than one wife.

## Age Difference between Partners

According to Krishnamurti System consider the lord of the constellation and the sub lord. They indicate whether the wife is aged or young: so also in the bride's chart, one is to judge the difference in age between husband and wife.

- (1) Saturn ruling the constellation or sub where 7th cusp falls, husband an aged person. So the difference is much.
- (2) Jupiter, Venus and Sun denote proper difference in age, if they rule the constellation and sub.
- (3) If Mars or Mercury or Moon rule the constellation or sub, the difference is very little. Husband will be young.
- (4) In each chart, find out whom Rahu or Kethu indicate. Accordingly read the result.

## Union

- (a) If the 7th Cusp falls in the constellation of Sun or sub of Sun, repulsion—no pleasure.
- (b) If the 7th Cusp falls in the constellation of Moon or sub of Moon, very pleasant, like Honey Moon.
- (c) If Mars rules, during union silly quarreling, male forces female to have intercourse—displeasure exceeds pleasure.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=89|p. 89]]

- (d) If Mercury governs, expresses one's desire, talks a lot of time and enjoys more than once to complete the course as there could be some disturbance while on.
- (e) If Jupiter is the lord, normal; pleasant; dutiful.
- (f) If Venus rules, 64 varieties of Leela. Both will have extreme pleasure: dance: many a kiss increases the Bliss.
- (g) If Saturn is the lord, after intercourse, dissatisfaction, displeasure, disharmony. Duration very short.
