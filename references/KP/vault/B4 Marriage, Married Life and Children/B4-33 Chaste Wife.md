# Chaste Wife

If the sub-lord of the 7th cusp is neither Mars nor Venus nor Saturn, and if the sub-lord of 7 is not deposited in constellation of Mars or Venus or Saturn, occupying one of the signs belonging to Mars or Venus or Saturn, then the girl will be chaste.

Once a friend of mine showed a chart and said that it belongs to his younger brother's wife. He asked me, "What do you think of this girl." I told him, neither the 7th cusp nor Mars, Venus and Saturn are connected with either her lagna or the 7th and also these three planets have no connection whatsoever with one another; She must be chaste.

He said, "Sir it is not for that I showed the chart. I want to know something else."

I asked him "What is that"?

He narrated that there is a defect in her generative system and asked me whether it could be set right. I told him that in her 12th cusp there is Neptune which was in Leo Nirayana 1 degree. I told him defect is indicated by the 12 house, disease by 6th and danger by the 8th. As the native has Kethu star Saturn Sub she lacks Conjugal Bliss. But as long as her Ascendant is in the constellation of lord of 11 aspected by Jupiter, she will be really chaste and the defect in her generative system would never be cured. A few years passed. The girl was taken to an expert in Women and Children Hospital. The Doctor reported after a thorough examination that Lord Brahma has forgotten to keep the generative system at all excepting that provision was made to pass only urine.
