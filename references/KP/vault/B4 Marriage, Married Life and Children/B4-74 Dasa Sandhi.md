# Dasa Sandhi

What is meant by "Dasa Sandhi" ? Is it harmful ?

**Answer:** By 'dasa', one means Udu-dasa or Vimshottari dasa. Udu Dasa Sandhi is the period when one major dasa ends and the other dasa starts. "Sandhi" means union. So dasa sandhi is the time when one dasa ends and the other follows.

If such a dasa sandhi of a person coincides with the dasa sandhi of the partner, husband or wife, a few astrologers threaten the querists by saying that the dasa sandhi portends evil, without considering any of the following points.

(1) If the dasa, which is to follow, ruled by a benefic or a malefic ? (2) Will one enjoy in the coming dasa or is he or she to face difficulties ? (3) If both the couple are to be fortunate after the evil dasa ends, and the favourable one commences, what is wrong in matching them for marriage ? (4) Can anybody marry a bride or a bridegroom other than what he or she is destined ? Is not the saying, 'Marriage is made in heaven' true ?

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=263|p. 263]]

(5) If a couple are to suffer, say, till December 1967, and they have to enjoy lucky time from the happy New Year 1968, will the chart of one show fortune from 1968, whereas the other denote continuation of misfortune ? Should not the bad dasa come to an end to both, and the good dasa start for them simultaneously. (6) A couple, after marriage, were not fortunate to have a child for some years. Later, just after dasa sandhi, they had a son born, as the new dasa which started, promised birth of children. If there is not such a change, in both the charts, how can they have the child ?

Some astrologers say, "If the horoscope of the boy does not promise progeny, match him with a bride who will have many children or vice versa." Do they think for a minute; if the boy is denied of a child, how can the bride have ? or if the bride should not have even one child, how can the male have ? Is it not meaningless or does it not mean something else ?

Such astrologers do not think deeply. They go only by general statements and proverbs. Can astrology avert any event ? Can one dodge fate by his will ? That which is to happen is sure to take place.

There are innumerable instances and we have the horoscopes of those people correctly cast.

1. If the lord of the dasa which is to follow happens to occupy the constellation of the lord of the previous dasa, that is running, there will not be any major changes in the events.

For example, a person serving in the military department from his Mars Dasa, will continue to hold service in the same department during Rahu Dasa, as Rahu was in Chitra star ruled by Mars. Neither would he have suffered during Moon sub-period in Mars Dasa nor in the sub-period or Rahu in Rahu Dasa.

One who entered in the Bank as a junior officer during Jupiter Dasa, had a pleasant time during Rahu Bhukti in Jupiter Dasa, and then had promotion and prosperity in Saturn Dasa, Saturn Bhukti. Throughout Saturn Dasa he had good support and improvement in status. He had children born in Jupiter Dasa. Saturn continued to give as Saturn was in Punarvasu star ruled by Jupiter.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=264|p. 264]]

Thus, it will be seen that the end of any Dasa need not be universally harmful, and the beginning of another Dasa also need not cause distress to all. Then astrology is made very simple to predict easily that the beginning and the end of a Dasa are bad and undesirable.

2. If the lord of the Dasa, that is to follow, owns a beneficial house, occupies a favourable house as well as the constellation of a benefic and is not afflicted, then the Dasa sandhi will be similar to day-down when darkness will disappear, and there will be bright light. The person will have a new cycle of prosperous life from the very beginning of the Dasa.

3. If in the horoscopes of a couple, both are running a brilliant dasa and an undesirable result is to follow, certainly both will suffer. Both must face difficulties. Can any one prevent them from getting married ? Not at all. Even if one says, that he had avoided such a combination, and made a selection in which there is no dasa sandhi in their life-time, it is only ignorance.

4. Majority of the people do not actually suffer in the end of a dasa or in the beginning of another. But the sufferings in the middle of the dasa is the worst and they have changes in career in any one of the Bhuktis in the middle of the dasa,

(a) Verify from the people who have retired from service and receive comparatively a small sum as pension. Have they retired during dasa sandhi ?

(b) Take the horoscopes of those who lost their wives or husbands. Did it happen during dasa sandhi ?

(c) Consider those who were unfortunate to lose their children. Had they dasa sandhi then ? At least was it at the dasa sandhi of either of the parents ?

(d) A man gets amputated leaves service and suffers a lot ; when we scrutinise their cases, neither he nor his wife had dasa sandhi.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=265|p. 265]]

Therefore, are we not convinced that good and bad happen when a dasa is running and the windfall or the pitfall to most of the persons was neither in the end of a dasa, nor in the beginning of the other ?

Therefore one need not worry about dasa sandhi at all.
