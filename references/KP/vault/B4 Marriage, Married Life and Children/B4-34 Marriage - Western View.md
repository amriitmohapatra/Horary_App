# Marriage - Western View

Marriage is the permanent tie of friendship. There can be no real friendship without confidence. One should make a correct choice though "Hanging and wiving go by destiny." God is helpful to one who won't marry till a perfect partner is found; if one finds a suitable partner then God helps still more.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=105|p. 105]]

One may marry late in one's life for economic reasons. One may feel that early marriages are permanent moralities and late marriage are temptations to wickedness. Always a happy marriage is a new beginning of life, a new starting point for happiness and steadiness. Romance will cease when the marriage is done and their history will start from then. Hence one should conveniently and leisurely observe and consider before getting married. But what is the use of repenting at leisure by marrying in haste. Johnson says "When we see the avaricious and crafty taking companions to their tables and their beds without any inquiry but after farms and money; or the giddy and thoughtless uniting themselves for life to those whom they have only seen by the light of tapers; when parents make articles for children without inquiring after their consent; when some marry for heirs to disappoint their brothers; and others throw themselves into the arms of those whom they do not love, because they have found themselves rejected where they were more solicitous to please; when some marry because their servants cheat them: some because they squander their own money; some because their houses are pestered with company; some because they will live like other people; some because they are sick of themselves, we are not so much inclined to wonder that marriage is sometimes unhappy, as that it appears so little loaded with calamity; and cannot but conclude that society has something in itself eminently agreeable to human nature, when we find its pleasures so great, that even the ill-choice of a companion can hardly over-balance them, those therefore, that rail against matrimony should be informed that they are neither to wonder or repent / languish that a contract begun on such principles has ended in disappointment."

But marriage, with such a partner after ascertaining whether they can have mutual love among themselves, is the crown of all our bliss. Hence one has to find out astrologically the suitable partner to lead a happy and harmonious wedded life.

If both the gent's and the bride's charts are positive then both will have many virtues in common: as also the vices, because of the latter, there must be clash, each prepared to fight to the finish. Both will be argumentative and adamant. It is not advisable to match such a couple.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=106|p. 106]]

If both are negative, both will suffer in silence which is very harmful both to their mental and physical health. It will lead to the development of very unfortunate circumstances.

If the male is positive and the female negative the result will be the best. The energy of the male and the gentleness of the female will be ideal to please the partner. Even if the financial position of the family may not be satisfactory, yet they will have a good sexual life without suffering from ill-health and emotional debilitations. Good.

Let us see how the positive female and negative male will be; the female will be dynamically strong. It is alright, if one has enough money, robust health and good position. Even then the weakness of the male will be accentuated to a good extent. Finally the female will appear to treat the husband with contempt or to ignore him by curling up her lips. She will find fault with the husband, scold persistently and annoy him. She will be a shrew, being shrewd

Therefore it is absolutely necessary to examine the horoscopes of both the male and female astrologically which reveals the personalities, health, character and temperament of both. After marriage will they complement each other understanding their true desire and nature so that the greatest possible harmony can be established to lead a good life, it it to be judged before marriage. Astrology assists in this.
