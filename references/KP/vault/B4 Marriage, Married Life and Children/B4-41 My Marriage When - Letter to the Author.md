# My Marriage When - Letter to the Author

Question: My marriage when? I was asked to give a number between 0 and 249.

At that moment the number which came to my mind instantaneously was 77. Within few seconds you gave the following predictions in writing. (I do not know how your goodself could calculate so quickly).

- (1) Negotiations after 2-12-69 alone will materialise.
- (2) Betrothal—Third week of February 1970.
- (3) Marriage—May 1970.

Now the actual dates.

- (1) Negotiations materialised on 5-12-69.
- (2) Betrothal date was finished on 17-2-70 i.e., exactly in the third week of February.
- (2) [sic] Marriage—Fixed on 7-5-1970.

There are people who laugh at those who consult astrologer. But I say that nobody can ever dare laugh at a person who consults our Guruji or the followers of His Padhdhati.

I may also state that another astrologer collected details of my past life, rectified my birth time according to his method and then predicted that my marriage could not take place before October 1970.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=146|p. 146]]

Long live our Guruji whose predictions on the basis of Horary are more true than another who rectified birth time collecting past data.

Thanking once again,

Yours faithfully,

S. P. G.
A 65/331, Moti Bagh-1,
New Delhi—23,
31st March, 1970.

No. 25, Road 11-12,
Petaling Jaya, MALAYSIA.

The Editor,
ASTROLOGY & ATHRISHTA,
12, Brahmin Street,
Saidapet, MADRAS 5.

Revered Sir,

This letter is to express my deep reverence and gratefulness to you in respect of your correct prediction and gracious blessings.

On 16th May 1970, my parents consulted you about the time of my marriage. You assured us that betrothal ceremony will take place on 31st May 1970 and marriage on 3rd September 1970. As the first part came very true, we invited you to do pooja and bless us.

We are very happy and grateful. You may publish my letter of gratefulness in your valuable book for the benefit of all the readers.

We request you to bless us on the day of may marriage if you visit Petaling Jaya.

With my respects and regards.

Yours sincerely,

S.V. S.
(Marriage took place on 3-9-70, as predicted.) — Author

Respected Sir,

I am glad to write this letter to you, for your disciple Mr. G. D. Kale's prediction about the date of my marriage has proved to be very true. I have been hearing much about you and your method "Krishnamurti Padhdhati" till now; but today I am myself a witness to the great accuracy of your method. Need I say that this incident has increased my measure of esteem towards you?

Yours truly,
(Sd.) A.P.D.

My dear Gurudev,

Namaskar.

I take this opportunity of sending you all our sincere good wishes for a happy New Year full of prosperity and good luck.

With your blessings the marriage of my daughter was celebrated on 24-11-69. She is now in U.K. and with God's grace happy. All these have happened as predicted, by you.

Surprisingly the predictions concerning my other daughter also have come very true. She got a flat in Bombay and her husband had promotion on the dates of prediction, and has been transferred to SURAT.

Yours Sincerely,

(Sd.) V.S. V.
