# Pregnancy

If it is between 240° and 252°, by worshiping Muruga, son of Siva, one can have a child. If it is between 240° and 300° he may marry another, pray, do charity and fast. He may have a child. Between 300° and 324°, the wife may have false pregnancy. If the result is between 324° and 348°, one will adopt a child. If it is between 348° and 363° one will not take adoption and one will not have a child. By doing Ashtaka or Parvana Sradha one can please the Manes and have an issue. Those who do not perform or think of their parents or do the rites, annual ceremony etc. will not have a child. There is danger to the children born alto. If it is Krishna Paksha Shasti, pray Muruga. If it is Krishna Paksha Chaturthi, propitiate Lord Ganesh : if it is Krishna Paksha Navami, pray Goddess Durga: Saptami and Chaturdasi, serpents ; Amavasya, Pithrus and Dwadasi, Sun.

## Pregnancy

- First month is ruled by Venus ; the semen continues to be in liquid State.
- Second month is governed by Mars: embryo solidifies.
- Third month-Jupiter-limbs are formed.
- Fourth month-Sun; bones gets formed.
- Fifth month-Moon-supplies the skin.
- Sixth month-Saturn-allows hair to grow.
- Seventh month-Mercury-develops nervous system.
- Eighth month-Lord of Adhana Lagna-umbilical cord takes food to the child.
- Ninth month-Sun-foetus feels thirst and hunger.
- Tenth month-ready for delivery-Moon rules.

Abortion takes place in that month if that planet ruling that month ill-posited and adversely aspected. Miscarriage will be in that month ruled by the planet in debilitation and afflicted.

## Short-lived Children

(1) If in the horoscope of the child that is born, malefics are in Lagna and seventh house conjoined with Moon and devoid of beneficial aspect, it will pass away immediately after delivery.

(2) Waning Moon in 12, malefics in 1 and 8, no benefic in a Kendra, short-lived.

(3) Moon conjoined with malefics in 7 or 3 or 12. short-lived.
