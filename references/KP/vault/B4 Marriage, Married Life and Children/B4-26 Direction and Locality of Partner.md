# Direction and Locality of Partner

If the lord of the sub or the sub occupied by the significator owns the house 4 or 10, the partner may be a co-tenant, or live in one's street, town, district etc: After marriage it does not involve a journey to reach husband's place.

If the lord owns 3rd house, the partner may be a cousin or a neighbour or one living at a short distance which can be reached by making land travel for a few hours or one fixed by correspondence, advertisement, etc;

If it owns the 11th house, he or she comes from friend's family.

If it owns 5 or 9; stranger, love marriage, foreigner, long distance. Thus one is to judge.
