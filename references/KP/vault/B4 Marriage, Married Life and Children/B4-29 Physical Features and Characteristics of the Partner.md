# Physical Features and Characteristics of the Partner

The best and the most correct method of finding out the physical feature of the would-be-partner: Note the sub-lord of the 7th cusp. Find out in which sign the sub-lord is posited. The result depends on the nature of the planet and also the sign occupied by it.

## If the Sub-Lord of the Seventh Cusp Is Sun

If the sub-lord of the seventh cusp is SUN and it is deposited in or the lord of the constellation is Sun in which sublord of 7 is in.

**Aries:** Middle statue, strong and well-made, complexion good, full eyes; bold, noble, will assert his right, comes out victorious, proud, social success.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=91|p. 91]]

**Taurus:** Short and well set. Not very fair, broad mouth, broad face, strong sportsman or athletic, over-confident, not proud, good debater, argues well and wins.

**Gemini:** Above average in height, well proportioned body, moderate complexion, long nose, piercing eye, most courteous, tactful, accommodative, can be influenced by others, pleasing manners.

**Cancer:** Short stature, repulsive face, not beautiful. Therefore do not approach any astrologer to know about the features of your would-be wife or husband when Sun is transitting in Cancer. If by chance it happens to be the sub-lord of the 7th cusp, he had to predict the distasteful ones. So avoid between 15th July and 17th August. Temperament good, harmless, cheerful, fond of other, sex (within himself), admires music, dance, sports, lazy, does not like to work.

**Leo:** Strong well-built, moderate complexion, big eyes, round and big face, most faithful and reliable, very punctual always, fair and just in his actions, ambitious and successful.

**Virgo:** Tall person with slender stature and good complexion very dark hair, fond of music and ever singing, always smiling and never provoking, cheerful, pleasant, spends much time on recreations.

**Libra:** Tall, straight upright body. Big eyes, thin hair, beautifull, never bold but ever talking about war, riot, etc. not much reliable, weak in his attachments and will find difficult to speak truth (For whom does the beauty appeal. Reject such a partner).

**Scorpio:** Square built, fleshy, full growth, broad face, moderate complexion, ingenious, very clever, discoverer, very brisk, ambitious, never accepts defeat or consider anybody as his or her equal. Fortunate.

**Sagittarius:** Tall, beautiful, body well proportioned, face oval, ever-estimate one's ability, austere, severe, plans for big thing

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=92|p. 92]]

**Capricorn:** Thin built, lean, wiry, tall long nose, soft hair sickly appearance, pleasing manners, nasty occasionally, makes many friends, commands others goodwill.

**Aquarius:** Tall, well made, round face, good complexion, tolerably good disposition, never deceitful, proud, ostentatious.

**Pisces:** Short, stout, protruded eyes, plumpy body, round face, unjust, partial, fond of pleasures, extravagant, hence spoils himself, unfit to be a referee in the sports and games.

**Gemini:** Tall, well built, upright, good complexion, Ingenius, unsteady, lacks decision, not fortunate in profession
