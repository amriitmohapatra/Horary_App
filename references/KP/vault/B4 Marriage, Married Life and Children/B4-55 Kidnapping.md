# Kidnapping

In Hindu Traditional Astrology, it is believed that exalted planets offer brilliant results and most desirable ones, causing the so-called Raja Yogas. When such is the case, I am unable to understand how majority of planets being exalted causing many Raja Yogas found in Rama's chart contributed for the kidnapping of Sita.

According to KRISHNAMURTI SYSTEM, the planets which are exalted need not at all give advantageous results. They can do harm. Everything depends upon the sub, occupied by a planet, whether exalted or debilitated. The significators of the houses 3, 6, 7 and 9 indicate that one's partner will be kidnapped. If the same person is to be kidnapped, in his or her horoscope the significators of houses 1, 3, 9 and 12 must operate. These significators should not be connected with 11th house. But if a particular planet is connected with both, the kidnapping houses 1, 3, 9, 12 and also reunion house the 11, then after being kidnapped, the person will be found and there will be reunion. This also applies to people, who run away due to fear during evacuation, like a refugee. In their cases also, one can predict, whether the partner is alive and when they will reunite.

In 1951, one Kashmiri Brahmin dealing in woolen goods, came to my house in the evening hours, and he narrated his plight.

He told that he and the members of his family were much afraid of the situation in his land, and he thought that there would be peace, law and order in India and he came to Amritsar; from there to Delhi; from there to Calcutta and then to Avadi He saw with his own eyes three bodies had been charred due to the fire in his house. He was living with his wife and three daughters and he did not know who was alive and which three of his kith and kin were burnt to death.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=203|p. 203]]

I immediately asked him to give a number and I took the position of the planets for that moment and give the following prediction : "Sir, the sub-lord of the 8th house clearly shows that your wife will outlive you ; hence as long as you are alive, she must be alive. Therefore, only your three daughters would have been lost in such a mishap."

Then he asked me, when he would meet her or when she would come to India, where he is, and so on. I replied that one will not be knowing the whereabouts of the other till a particular day. And on that day both of you will meet accidentally. And there will be reunion.

Readers will be very much thrilled to read the following : Mr. Punidhavel Mudaliar of Shanker Trading Company, I and about a dozen dealers in Stock exchange were discussing about the position of the market and traveling by the Electric train from Madras Beach Station to Saidapet.

On that day around 5-30 P.M. his wife who had been following the husband went after he had left each place to the various places where he was ; (Amritsar, Delhi, Calcutta etc.) and atlast she came to Avadi; she understood that he had shifted to Madras and when she came to the room where he was staying, she learnt that he had gone to his office and would be returning after 5-30 P.M. As she could not control herself, she walked the distance of three furlongs and in front of Flower Bazaar Police Station, Madras, she saw him coming with a tiffin-box in his hand. She cried and immediately ran to him and embraced him. She created a scene. It took sometime for them to be normal. They visited my residence at 8 P.M.

They waited till 8-30 P.M. They told my wife that they would come again the next day as it was getting late. They came to Saidapet railway station and were facing the other side to go to Madras Beach. When they were standing on the plat-form, the train by which I and Mr. Punidavel Mudaliar of Shanker Trading Company and others were traveling arrived and we got down. From the back-side, I could recognise that Kashmiri Brahmin was standing. So I loudly called him "Hello". That person turned, looked at me and cried so loudly that anybody, within a furlong distance could hear him.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=204|p. 204]]

Like a log of wood, he fell at my feet. He got up and drew his wife by her hand and introduced saying "This is my wife. What am I to say? Are you a devil? How could you predict so correctly ? It seems that my wife followed me all along and our three daughters were burnt, she alone is alive. Exactly on the day you had predicted, as you mentioned, we accidentally met on the road while walking in opposite direction. He lifted me and kept me on the staircase in the 4th or 5th step and narrated the whole story to the public. More than 100 people, who got down from the train, all gathered together, heard him and everybody admired.

This prediction was narrated by many for a long time and whenever I travelled by train, or by bus, in those days, people used to point me and talk about this matter. I followed only the rule, I have given in my book.
