Return from the foreign land means leaving his present place, making a long journey and reuniting with his kith and kin with whom he was originally living.

Leaving the place where one stays either temporarily or permanently is to be judged from the 3rd house making a long journey is to be judged from the 9th house.

Reunion with kith and kin is found from the 11th house. Therefore one has to judge the houses 3, 9 and 11. 3rd house extends from 27°11' Cancer to 27°1' Leo. Rahu is posited in that sign. Rahu's star are Arudhra, Swathi and Sathabisha. Therefore Kethu which is posited in Rahu star indicates that he leaves the place of residence, then 9th house extends from 27°1' Capricorn to 27°1' Aquarius. Kethu alone is posited in the 9th house. Therefore Kethu star Aswini, Magha and Moola are to be seen, to note whether any planet or node occupies any of these 3 stars. Rahu alone is in Magha Therefore, Rahu gives long journey. 11th house extends from 1°3' Aries to 5°3' Taurus. No planet is posited in this area. Therefore the lord of the sign in which the 11th cusp falls is to be taken. As the 11th cusp falls in Aries, the lord of the sign is Mars. Therefore Mars indicates the 11th house matter and also those planets which are posited in the constellation of Mars. The stars belonging to Mars are Mrigasira, Chithra and Danishta. Mars and Sun are deposited in the star of Mars Therefore Rahu, Kethu, Mars and Sun are the planets which are strong to indicate these results.

## Who Survives Whom

**1st Method:** You have to calculate the age of each individual separately. Add the age to the date of birth. Find out who passes away earlier.

**2nd Method:** If the sub-lord of the 8th cusp happens to be the significator of the houses 1, 6, and 8 and if the conjoined period runs in the life time of the native, the partner passes away. There is nothing as malefic or benefic occupying 2 or 8. This fails miserably. There are horoscopes, wherein the 8th house and the second house are occupied by only benefics and the lady has to become a widow. We, in our research, collected the horoscopes of widows and widowers and we have found that the system followed by Tradition does not satisfy. In many cases it fails. Whether you take sign by sign or Krishnamurti's method of house division, the best method is to ascertain the longevity of each separately as said in the first condition.
