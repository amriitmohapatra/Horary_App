# Will I Marry

I think that he who created me had totally forgotten me.

"Why do you say so?"

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=119|p. 119]]

"Look! I was born in 1931. Thirty seven long, long years have passed. Yet, I remain unmarried. Marriage is strange to me."

"If God is asleep, why can't you pray and wake him up."

"A few weeks ago, when I felt dejected, a young gentleman of my age came to me and during the conversation he suggested that unmarried people should pray to Goddess Bhavani as His Holiness Jagadguru Kanchi Kamakoti Peetadhipathi Sri Sankarachariar Swamigal recommended to all those aged persons, especially girls remaining unmarried. He added that within a year after he started to pray, he married a handsome girl and since a few months, she was leading a happy and harmonious wedded life."

"I said to him what!.. only last week, I got a picture of Goddess Bhavani. I have left it in my table drawer. Now that you advise me, I shall frame it and daily pray. I should do that which will give me relief."

"Now I would like to tell you, Sir, let prayer be on one side. You, please, judge my horoscope and predict correctly. Even if you say that I will remain unmarried, I do not mind. What else can I do? I shall console myself and never think about it. But if you give a date and it does not take place then, then the houses 2 and 7 indicating marriage will offer the other results of these houses. You know that these are maraka houses. So I will end myself when there will be no problem."

"True. Anyhow, let me scrutinise your chart."

He presents his chart saying that one well-known astrologer had erected the map.

"My dear boy, the horoscope when worked taking the lagna position, birth ought to have been at 8 P.M. If you take Moon position as is given in the chart, and calculate, the birth ought to have been at 10-00 P.M. If the position of Sun is taken and calculation it made, birth should be at 11-55 P.M. All these for the date of birth, i.e. 1-5-1931. But if you calculate taking Mars, and find out the birth time, it should be second May, the date itself should be changed wonderful."

"Sir, I told you that he is well-known to many. I did not say that a good astrologer did an honest work. To be known to many does not mean that he is good in his job. I myself know that his failures are many, miserable and innumerable. But he will tom tom (boost about it) if at all he has a lucky hit."

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=120|p. 120]]

"Alright let me work out your chart. Where were you born?"

"Karachi, please."

"So you are a refugee?"

"Yes."

"What is the actual time of birth?"

"10-02 P.M. I.S.T. on 1-5-1931."

"Then the following is your horoscope. It is calculated correctly referring to Raphael Ephemeris, Raphael Table of houses and using only Krishnamurti Ayanamsa, deducting from the figures (Sayana) obtained. Your Nirayana Chart is as given below ([[#^b4-f024|Fig. B4.24]]):"

Let us analyse. First let us see whether marriage is promised, consider the sub lord of the 7th cusp. It is Moon's constellation and Venus sub. Where is Venus and which house matters does he indicates? Venus is in Uthrabadra star ruled by Saturn, who is in the second house. Therefore, Venus (the only planet in Saturn's star) promises marriage. Not that he is the Chief Governor for marriage but because it is in the constellation of Saturn, occupant of second house.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=121|p. 121]]

"Thank God! He has not denied me. So far, I am happy. Please proceed."

"Next we have to find out which houses, are we to consider."

"Yes, they are 2, 7 and 11."

"Do you know why all these are to be judged?"

"I have memorized what all you had said. I agree with that American girl who wrote that she had memorized everything by reading over and over again without being bored even for a minute. 7th house denotes legal bondage, wife, she who legally shares the property, possessions, etc., though she may or may not share our sorrows. 2nd house indicates the family members. So, if marriage takes place, one more member will be added to the family from the moment of marriage; in a later date, birth of children to her will be added one by one, if there is no twin or triplet birth: 11th house shows long standing love, pleasure, permanent tie of friendship and partnership in life (numerologically 2 and 11 are the same. [?2] and 7 are the numbers of Moon)."

"How do you find out each house?"

"Second house means the arc commencing from second cusp and ending by the third cusp."

"Why do you not take half behind and half in advance and call the cusp as midpoint."

"I have totally forgotten the good old traditional system. I follow only Krishnamurti Padhdhati. You have asked us to follow the method followed by westerners and not that advocated by the Hindus."

"So, second house extends from 22° 21' Sagittarius to 26° 21' Capricorn. Seventh house means that area commencing from 21° 33' Taurus to 22° 21' Gemini. Similarly 11th house extends from 1° 21' Libra to 27° 21' Libra."

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=122|p. 122]]

"What are we to do next?"

"You have clearly said that occupants are stronger than owners. Constellations are stronger than signs. Hence only one single method is given by you for all queries. You do not expect us to go through tens of thousands of slokas, one contradicting the other, confusing the readers as well as the consultants. Come on, give out the order of judging the significators."

1. Planets tenanted in the constellation of the occupants of the houses 2 or 7 or 11.
2. The occupants of 2 or 7 or 11.
3. Planets situated in the constellation of the owners of the signs where these cusps fall.
4. Owners.
5. Planets conjoined with or aspected by any of those found out in the previous four conditions.

Second house: Occupant Saturn, owner Jupiter. Occupant's stars are Pushya, Anuradha, UP. Pada. Venus alone is in the constellation Saturn in 2 (Venus is also lord of 11 as 11th cusp is in 1° 2' in Libra).

Seventh house: Occupant Jupiter - Owner of Venus - Jupiter stars are Punarvasu, Visaka, Poorvabhadra - Jupiter alone is in Jupiter's star.

11th house is occupied by Moon - Owner Venus, Kethu alone is in Moon star. Bharani, P. Phalguni and Poorvashada are ruled by Venus. Sun and Mercury are in Venus' stars.

"When majority of the planets are connected with the houses 2, 7 and 11, how to eliminate the useless ones and select the useful ones?"

"Ruling planets at the moment of judgement must agree. Further the sub lord of each of the significators. If a planet is in such a position that its sub lord is connected with the houses 2, 7 and 11 then this planet is useful. But if the sub is lord of 1, 6, 10 and is the significator of 12 houses, marriage cannot take place.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=123|p. 123]]

Today Monday ruled by Moon. Star Ashlesha governed by Mercury, lagna is in Taurus owned by Venus, in Moon's star Jupiter sub.

Hence take Venus Jupiter and Moon.

Time of marriage will be Venus Bhukti Jupiter Anthra Moon Shookshma. That will be in the first week of August.

As the sub lord of the 7th cusp is Venus and it is in the 4th house, the bride will be in the town where you reside. Venus, the sub lord, denotes that she would be fair. Having waited all along, after all God favors you with a charming and handsome girl on or around on 1 August."

"Which year, Sir, you have not said. 69 or 70 or what?"

"Sir, every mantra is to be repeated at least for 6 months regularly, systematically and sincerely. Today is 1-2-69. "Let me make real attempt" for the 6 months. I shall send my invitation card this year itself in July."

"Do pray" is my advice: Whether you pray or not, planets promise partner for you in August 1969. I predict. But one thing, astrologer can only predict. But Sidhi-Purushas can bless."

"Then Sir, why can't you suggest one Siddhi-Purusha - Let him be anywhere. Now itself I will go and have such a blessing as to have the marriage in February itself. I can't remain single any more. Enough! I want marriage!"

"Why can't you bless me, Sir."

"Yes, I will bless in the 4th week of July 1969. You will get married in August 1969, you will attribute this to my blessings.

Fact is this. Whether one blesses or not, prays or not, puts on a gem or not, things will and must happen according to one's fate. We put on Gem, we pray God, we receive blessings, all these only when the time is ripe. So it is the destiny which is the master of everything. As I had already emphatically published in my book, now also I say "Karma comes first. God comes next." None can dodge fate! None can hoodwink destiny - None can bribe the God or Goddess or planets. The nature's divine plan will go on unchanged. These can be found out easily if you read Krishnamurti Padhdhati wherein advanced stellar astrology is dealt with after ruthlessly exposing the miserable failures, alternatives, contradictions, etc., in traditional Astrology.
