# Eunuchs

According to Hindus Sun in a masculine sign and Moon in a feminine sign, roughly 180 degrees away or one opposing the other, will cause the birth of Eunuch.

If Mercury and Saturn were to be roughly 180 degrees off or if the Lagna and the Moon occupy masculine signs receiving the Hindu aspect by Mars, or if Moon were to occupy a feminine sign and Mercury in a masculine sign receiving aspect from Mars or by both show that the person in his life time will prove to be useless for sexual life.

But according to K.P. the lord of the constellation and the lord of the sub should be governed by Saturn or Rahu or Kethu and all of them should be the significators of 4, 10 and 12.
