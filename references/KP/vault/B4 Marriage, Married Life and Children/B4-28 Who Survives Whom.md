# Who Survives Whom

According to Hindus, malefics in 8 are evil. Benefic in 2 wards off the evil. If there is no benefic aspect to the 8th house; but there is a malefic in 8, the person survives the partner. This rule fails.

But correct results can be had, if the Sublord of the 7th Cusp occupies a dual sign and it happens to be the significator of marakasthana and Bhadhakasthana, wife passes away earlier. The same rule applies to 2nd wife and you have to judge the 2nd cusp sublord.
