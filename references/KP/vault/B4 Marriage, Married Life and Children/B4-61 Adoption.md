# Adoption

(1) If Jupiter and lord of 5 are ill-placed and if the 5th house from the signs occupied by them is occupied by malefics one will adopt a child.

(2) If the 5th cusp falls in Saturn's sign in rasi or Navamsa and if Moon is in 5 or aspects the 5th house one adopts.

(3) If Saturn is conjoined with the lord of the sign occupied by Moon one adopts.

(4) Saturn, Moon and Mercury in 5- adoption.

(5) Saturn and Mars in 5th house-adopted.

(6) Saturn conjoined with Moon in 11-adoption,

(7) If the 5th house is occupied or aspected by Saturn or Mercury, and Gulika is in 5; adoption.

(8) Seventh house is also to be judged for adoption.
