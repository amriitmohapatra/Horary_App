# Marriage Denial - Grapes Are Sour

The following is the horoscope of a person who has now once for all decided not to marry anybody as all the proposals which he had from 1950 to 1970 failed after giving hopes after hopes ([[#^b4-f022|Fig. B4.22]]).

Every time entertaining hopes and every time getting disappointed: Even though the native is having robust health, good complexion, yet there is no companion for him to jointly have the pleasure, to share the income etc. According to Krishnamurti Padhdhati a person is denied of marriage when the 7th Cusp sub lord is deposited in the constellation of the significators of 4, 6 and 10 because the houses 2, 7 and 11 show the marriage, and hence the 12th house to these houses indicates denial of the marriage. Again 5th house shows either love affair or children. 4th house indicates failure in love affairs and also denial of children or separation from them. In this horoscope the 7th Cusp fall at 25 degrees, 56 minutes. Therefore, it is in Venus sign, Mars star and Rahu sub. Rahu is deposited in the 6th house. It is in the constellation of Moon in the 4th house. Rahu occupying Venus sign and conjoined with it shows that 6th house result is stronger than 7. Further Rahu is in the sub of Saturn in the 12th house.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=110|p. 110]]

Venus, lord of 7 is in the sub of Sun who is lord of 10 and who is deposited in the constellation of Mars and sub of Saturn; Mars is in 4, Saturn is in 12. All these rules show that he is not lucky to get married.

Actually chances will be nil for him; he will ever miss to kiss a miss. If Rahu would have been in Jupiter, sub, he would have been married and also loved a few more. Therefore, the students must note that it is the sub lord which is mainly responsible to give clear idea, and correct picture. One need not commit to memory many a sloka. The simple rule is, note the sublord of the 7th Cusp; if the sub lord is connected with 4, 6, 10th houses, one will be denied of marriage.
