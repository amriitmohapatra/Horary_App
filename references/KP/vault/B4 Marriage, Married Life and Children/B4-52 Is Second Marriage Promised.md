# Is Second Marriage Promised

*(Few Planets may be in the same Star : note how the sub indicates the truth)*

"Please come in," said I when a person just peeped into my chambers.

"Can I do anything for you, please?"

"Today exactly one year has passed, I lost my wife last year on the 29th September. She has left two children to my care and passed away. I want to know whether I will marry again and if so when."

"When were you first married ?"

"In 1962 on May 21st."

"Have you brought your horoscope?"

"Yes, I have erected a chart according to your instructions."

"Which Ayanamsa have you used ?"

"Only yours."

"Please let me verify, after noting the time now."

"Now it is 10-52 A.M., Sir."

"So the lagna for this moment is 12 degrees in Scorpio. It is Monday; star Bharani; Rasi Mesha. Hence Kuja (Mars), Sani (Saturn), Chandra (Mars) and Sukra (Venus) are the ruling planets. Let me see whether these agree with your chart."

He hands over the chart prepared by him.

"Look ! Actually your lagna is in the same position. You are born at 12-09 P.M. on 28-8-1935 at 20°28' North Latitude 85°54', East Longitude."

Kethu Dasa (according to Vimshottari Dasa) Balance - 5 years, 9 months, 1 day.

"Sun Dasa Rahu Bhukti operated between 25-4-1962 and 19-3-1963; Chandra Dasa Rahu Bhukti operated between 1-8-1968 and 1-2-1970. Note that Rahu gave you marriage in Surya Dasa and took away your wife during the Chandra Dasa.

"How is it ?"

"Rahu is situated in the second bhava (house). It is to represent Guru who is lord of 2 and 5. The 2nd house indicates about family members. The 11th house shows additions to the family ties. The 7th indicates marriage. Therefore the significators are Rahu, Sani, in Rahu constellation, Kethu in the constellation of Guru who is lord of 2, and similarly Kuja (Mars) and Guru in the constellation of lord of 2. No planet occupies the 7th house. Sukra is its lord and no planet is posited in any of the three stars ruled by Sukra. The 11th house is unoccupied, it is owned by Budha. Kethu, a node, is situated in Mithuna governed by Budha, lord of 11. Only Sun and the Moon are in Kethu's constellation."

"When Surya and Chandra are both in the same constellation, how is it that in Surya Dasa I got married and in Moon (Chandra) Dasa I lost my wife?"

"My friend, it is only Krishnamurti Padhdhati which can clearly explain such an event. The traditional methods of astrology have no answer. The constellation, Makha, is governed by Kethu which indicates, among other things, 11th house results though occupying the other sign of Budha which owns the 11th house also. So planets in Makha constellation have connection with the 11th house affairs. If the planet in Makha, lord of which is a significator of 11th house affairs, occupies the sub of a planet connected with 6 or 10 or 12, then separation or bereavement is to be predicted ; if it is in the sub of the significator of 2, 7 or 11, it indicates wedding. Now, the Sun in your horoscope was in the sub of Sani who is the significator of the second house, it occupies the constellation of Rahu in 2. The 2nd house, as already stated, is one of the houses to be judged for marriage, and Sun in the sub of Sani thus gave marriage. Moon, on the other hand, was in the sub of Sukra who is lord of 12. Sukra is in the constellation of a planet posited in 9 owning the 10th and sub of another planet in 9. Sukra, according to its lordship, can offer the results of both the 7th and 12th houses. Hence, Moon connected with 11, 8 (kethu) and 12 (Sukra) gave death to wife."

The rule is this. If a planet has to offer a desirable as well as an undesirable result, it will offer both. One result does not cancel the other. Both the results will operate in due time. Rahu as the occupant of the 2nd house has the power to offer marriage, but as it is in the constellation of a planet owning house 10, it is also capable of doing harm to the 11th house matters. Why ? Because the 10th house is the house of negotiation of the affairs influenced by the 11th house. The eleventh connotes permanent tie of friendship by marriage; the tenth shows breaking of this tie.

A bhukti lord which has to give both beneficial and harmful results will confer benefit in the dasa of a planet which is beneficially connected ; it will cause harm during the dasa of another planet which has to bring about evil. The bhukti lord is a turncoat changing according to its master, the dasa lord. Thus, Rahu cooperating with the dasa lord, Surya, gave marriage and in the dasa of Chandra it caused you bereavement in the shape of your wife's death.

"Sir, Chandra Dasa is now running. It will go on for some years now. Does it mean, then, that I would not get married during Chandra Dasa ?"

"No ; it does not mean that. Chandra is in the constellation of Kethu in a dual sign and can give both the results. Kethu, as I said already, is an agent of Budha, a dual planet, and so both the effects (8 and 11) will be there. Further, the sub-lord Sukra also a significator of the 7th house by owning that house. Hence in another favourable bhukti the second marriage can come.

"Why do you say 'can come'? Will it happen or not?"

"Note the sub-lord of the 7th cusp. If the lord of the sub or the lord of the constellation in which the sub-lord is situated is in a dual sign or happens to be a dual planet, then the second marriage is promised. Rahu is the lord of the sub of the 7th cusp; it is in Dhanus a dual sign. Hence two marriages are indicated. Further, Rahu is in the 2nd house which rules the second wife.

"All right, Sir, when will the second marriage come off?"

"This should come off in the dasa of Chandra which is in the constellation of Kethu acting as the agent of lord of 11 and sub of Sukra owning the 4th house; in the bhukti of Guru, lord of 2 occupying its own constellation and sub of Sani, a significator of 2nd house by occupying Rahu's constellation ; and in the anthra of Sani. It will be between 5-4-1970 and 21-6-1970. As at the moment of judgment, Chandra is in Bharani star, when Surya transits in Bharani and sub of Chandra, i.e. around 1-5-1970 the second marriage will take place. May you lead a happy and peaceful life and may He bless you all."
