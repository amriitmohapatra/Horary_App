# Will My Son-in-Law Contribute Peace

Son-in-law means, he is the bridegroom who has married the bride, daughter of the gentleman A, and A's wife B. Therefore, in both the horoscope of A and B which are correctly erected according to Krishnamurti's method we have to find out whether the sublord of the 11th Cusp in each horoscope of the parents of the girl happens to be the significator of 1 and 5. If so, he will contribute to peace of mind. But if the sublord of the 11th Cusp himself or the lord of the constellation in which the sublord was deposited happens to be the significator of 6 or 12 then there can be no peace of mind at all.

Again one should note that one may have 3, 4, 5, son-in-laws and a few will be very friendly and the other few inimical. So, in that case how can we generalise the rule ? So the correct method of finding out is as follows. Note down the sublord of the 11th and also the lord of the constellation in which the sublord is posited. If the significators of the 11th house and these planets happen to be the ruling planets at the moment of the birth of the son-in-laws, those son-in-laws will be friendly ; but if the sublord of the 6th Cusp himself or the lord of the constellation in which the sublord of the 6th happens to be the Significator of 6, 8, and 12 and not at all connected with houses 1, 5 and 11, they cannot be friendly. If these significators happen to be the ruling planets at the moment of the birth of the son-in-laws they will always cause worry and trouble. In some families you could have heard that for some years the son-in-laws happened to be bitter enemies and later became slaves. And in some families very friendly to start with and later became enemies.

Therefore, whenever the parents run good time ruled by the planets who are the significators of the houses 1, 5, 11, during those periods they are friendly and at the time when one runs the period of the significators of 6 and 1 [sic]; even that particular son-in-law whose ruling planet at the time of birth happen to be the lords of the period running, then there will be enmity.
