# When Will I Have a Child

Time of judgment is 5.37 A.M. C.S.T. on 11-12-1969.

Moon indicates the nature of the query. Moon in Kethu's constellation indicates the matters signified by Kethu who owns the 5th. The sub lord Jupiter is the chief governor for child. Jupiter is a strong significator of 11. Hence the query is about child birth.

Is it promised or not ? This question has to be judged from 5 and 11. The 5th cusp is in a barren sign and in the star of a planet (Venus) in 3rd from 5th. Further it is in the sub of a node in 11 from lagna.

The 11th cusp is in the star of Rahu, in 11th itself, and in the sub of Mars. The sub lord Mars is deciding factor and is in 12 to 11. Hence it does not promise.

From the above it is clear that even if she conceives, the result will not fructify or it may end up in abortions due to the aspects of malefics to 5 and 11, and specially due to the presence of abortive planet Kethu in 5.

For child birth, houses 2, 5 and 11 are judged.

2nd house is vacant. Its lord is Mars. It is in its own constellation and in the sub of a planet in 6 to 2.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=235|p. 235]]

5th house is occupied by abortive planet Kethu. It is in Venus, 3rd from 5, and in the sub of a retrograding planet (Saturn) in 8 to 5 denying any child,

11th house is occupied by Rahu. Rahu is in the star of Jupiter which is in 8 to 11, and in the sub of a retrograding planet in 12 to lagna.. Hence there is no doubt at all. She can never have a child.

Moon is in a mute sign and in its own constellation. It is in 7 and is a strong significator of 7. 7th house denotes partner in life. Moon is in the sub of Saturn which is in 2, denoting family and finance besides being the significator of 5. Hence this shows an addition of a child to the family through wife.

Since the querist is a male, consider the houses 11 and 5.

11th cusp is in Jupiter sign Venus star Rahu sub. The sub lord Rahu is in 3rd from 11th. Hence it denies.

5th cusp is in a mute sign and its lord Mercury is in 6th to 5th. This cusp is in Rahu star, Moon sub. The sub lord Moon is in 3rd from 5th besides being the strong significator of same. This also does not promise.

Generally 2, 5 and 11 are judged to note the time when one can have a child.

Here, the 2nd cusp is in Mercury star in sub of a planet in 6th or 2nd. 2nd house is occupied by retrograding Saturn which is on abortive note. 6th to 2nd and in its sub. Hence this too does not help.

5th house is vacant. Its lord in 6th from 5th, in its own star and in the sub of a retrograding planet.

11th house is vacant and its lord is Jupiter. It is in 8th from lagna, in the star of Mars which is 12th to lagna and in sub of a planet in 9.

All these factors do not promise and the native will never have a child.
