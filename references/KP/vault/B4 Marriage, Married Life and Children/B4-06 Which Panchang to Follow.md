# Which Panchang to Follow

Which Panchang to follow to erect the horoscopes. In India, innumerable almanacs are available. The authors follow any one of the 18 siddhantas. Therefore charts erected to the same birth using different almanacs differ much. The Nakshathras will be different. There may be change in the rasi. The dasa bhukti balance at the time of birth do not agree. Thus no two almanacs give the same position of planets.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=24|p. 24]]

As Drik System is astronomically correct, follow Drik system alone and erect the horoscopes, of those which are to be matched for matrimonial purposes. If you use Raphael Ephemeris deduct Krishnamurti ayanamsa; you will get Drik position. Any other ayanamsa, differing much, is useless and absurd.

## 1. Defect in Dasa Porutham

While judging the horoscopes one does not take into consideration, the Ascendant-Lagna and the position of other planets. Only the Moon's position (i.e.—the star and the rasi alone) is considered. It is incomplete and the prediction offered cannot come true in the state of married life. Dasa porutham itself is meaningless. Every rule has a contradiction or exception.

This method is useful only for those who do not know the correct time of birth but somehow remember his or her star.
