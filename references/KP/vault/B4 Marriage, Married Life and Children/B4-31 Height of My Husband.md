# Height of My Husband

The question, though happens to be relevant, especially when the girls are either short or very tall, yet actually there is no science to indicate the height of the partner.

In the year 1939, I was put a series of questions by the daughter of my boss. I had to describe the bridegroom, whom she would marry and also the time of marriage, which was five years after the date of consultation.

I told, that girl would marry on a particular day. I added that the person would be serving in Defense Department; his hip would be defective; he was not a business-man; he was not already closely related; he comes from a stranger's family, the marriage would be an arranged one and the height of the husband would be around 5 feet 8 inches.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=102|p. 102]]

Actually the marriage took place on the date of my prediction and it was celebrated at Bombay. A week later she and her husband came to the Tennis Court in King Institute, Guindy where I was working. I congratulated her. She told, "Sir, you were correct in all details. My husband is in Military. While he was in the foreign a bullet passed through the hip and there is the defect. He is not related to us before marriage time. Now he is my husband. Date of marriage came correct, But you failed in only one."

"What is it Madam"? I asked: "You said that he would be around 5 feet, 8 inches in height. Now, look at my husband. He is 6 feet."

Before she could finish her narration, I immediately convinced "Madam, on the day I gave the prediction he was 5 feet 8 inches. In these 5 years he has grown up to 6". Simply we laughed.

But yet, within me I thought, "Why did I answer this question at all? When we have no rule I would have plainly told madam, there is no rules; no science; no method to find out the height."

Therefore there is no method by which it is possible to give the height. Now a few of my students collect the horoscopes of both the husband and wife, note down their heights and also the other marks in the body, sores in the body etc. It will take another 6 months at least for us to come to a decision and give out the rule in my magazine, "ASTROLOGY & ATHRISHTA".
