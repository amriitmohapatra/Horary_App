# Can I Marry One Whom I Love

Love before marriage is likely Heavenly Bliss, why? The hopes, the dreams, the imagination, the pleasant feeling, the company before marriage, (within mind either to marry or to satisfy one self for a short period) may be classified as love. Always the expectation gives greater satisfaction than the actual attainment of one's aim. Love is to be judged from the sublord of the house and the significators of the 5th house. There may be, say, half a dozen girls whom a person may love. Yet that particular person who is born with such ruling planets which are the significators of the houses 2, 7 and 11 of the other will marry her or him. But if the ruling planets at the moment of the birth of the person whom one loves happen to be the significator of the houses 1, 6, 11, he or she will disappoint her or him. Therefore, you have to judge your horoscope and find out the other's birth time tactfully. One can get such information from the other only after having moved for some period. It does not matter. Before ones gets spoiled if one gets the horoscope and the Astrologer following K.P. judges and predicts that the person whom one loves will be true, one can continue go ahead till the date of marriage. But if the K.P. follower were to say that marriage will not take place but from a particular time the gap will widen day by day after a particular day, it will surely happen in the same manner and better to remain unspoiled after having understood the other. Nobody can change the fate. Even after being spoiled, one may consult, at that time the Astrologer's prediction must be given weight.
