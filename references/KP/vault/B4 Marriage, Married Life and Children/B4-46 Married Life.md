Jupiter Dasa 10 years 9 months 24 days.

Query is about your daughter. Number is given by you. So take the 5th house counted from the lagna shown by your number, as the lagna of daughter and then judge with the position of planets as is at the moment of judgment.

As 77 denotes Sagittarius Lagna. Position 13°20' to 16°40' So 5th cusp will fall in Bharani first Pada in Aries between 13°20' to 16°40'. For married life, planets connected with 6th house, i.e. significators of 6th house, bring about separation due to partner's walking out or desertion. 12th house significators denote that the native will seek separation, get out and live singly, 8th house significators denote disharmony and quarrel. 8th house does not threaten separation. The native will experience difficulties and continue to live together.

The seventh house counted from Aries is occupied by Mars. No planet is in Mars' star. Hence Mars denotes 7th house matters. 8th house is not occupied by any planet. It is owned by Mars. It promises lot of difficulties, difference of opinion and a patient victim to tortures. 12th house is occupied by Saturn. Rahu is in Saturn's star. Therefore whenever Rahu sub-sub period operates, the girl will visit her parent's place and remain alone. Jupiter and Kethu are in the 5th Bhava to the 5th. But Kethu represents Mercury lord of 6. Therefore during the sub periods of Saturn deposited in Mercury constellation and during Mercury and Kethu sub periods disharmony, disagreement, dissatisfaction and dispute are marked. Only when Venus sub period starts, i.e. after 2 years 8 months 6 days, the son-in-law will realise and he will be toned down. He will purchase peace and make all efforts to lead a happy harmonious life.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=159|p. 159]]

Divorce is not shown. Patience is to be cultivated by your daughter, as Aries happens to be the 5th counted from the Lagna that rises according to your number.

Let her pray Goddess Lakshmi whom Arjuna prayed according to his Guru's advice. (Lakshmi Hridayam.)

Since Jupiter and Venus favour happy life, the birth Nakshathra should be Visaka or Poorvashada. As these two stars are in either of their signs and stars.
