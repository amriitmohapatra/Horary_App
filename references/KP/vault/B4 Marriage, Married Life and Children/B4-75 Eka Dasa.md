# Eka Dasa

What is 'Eka-dasa' ? Why do a few astrologers consider it as harmful ?

**Answer:** By Eka-dasa, they mean that both the husband and the wife run a period ruled by the same planet. Here, the name of the planet alone is the same,

1. As the Lagna of both the husband and the wife will be different, the planet which governs the dasa will be the owner of a different house.

Therefore, they are the significators of different results and their effect will differ widely.

2. The lord of the dasa will be occupying different houses when counted from Lagna or Moon sign ; thereby the effect will be different, due to occupation.

3. The planets conjoined with the dasa lord or aspecting the dasa lord will be different.

4. In their horoscopes, the planets may occupy different constellations.

5. Thus, the results which are indicated by the dasa lord will differ, even though the dasa lord is called by the same name. It is not correct to say that the bride and the groom running the same dasa should not get married and if, by chance, they get married, this Eka-dasa will prove to be harmful.

The sages have declared that there will be satisfactory agreement between the bride and the bridegroom if the husband's star is the 9th or 18th or 27th counted from that of the bride (if 27th star in the same rasi). If they get married, they will enjoy good health for a long number of years.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=267|p. 267]]

Let us take a few examples :

A girl is born in Mrigasirisha star. The boy's star is Rohini. Both have Moon in Taurus, Rishaba. The agreement is said to be excellent for marriage, Let us work out the dasa. The girl has a balance of 6 years of Mars dasa at birth ; the boy has 5 years of Moon dasa. Therefore when the boy is 5 years old, Moon dasa ends; Mars dasa commences. If another year passes, he has enjoyed one year of Mars Dasa and there will be 6 more years of Mars dasa to run. Now his wife is born with Mars dasa balance of 6 years. Therefore they will be running Eka-dasa. How can it prove to be evil, when the 9th star is considered to be the best and the rasi is the same for both.

6 years after the birth of the boy, the girl is born. Though, for both Moon is in the same signs, all the other planets and the Lagna are in different sign. Therefore the results indicated by such planets must be different ; mostly opposite and partly agreeing.

e.g.

1. The planet may show acquisition of costly jewels to her. Should not the husband's chart show deterioration of his bank position to that extent?

2. The husband may need money. Wife's jewels may be pledged. Actually wife loses her possessions and the husband's bank position improves.

3. The husband gets scholarship and goes overseas for higher studies. The wife remains at home. Separation is common to both.

4. Wife is admitted in the maternity home. The husband visits the place, feels happy, distributes sweets and arranges a party on the day when his wife has given birth to a son. Pains to wife and gains to husband will be the result of the dasa though the name of the lord is the same.

A person may by nature be magnanimous. But by occupying a position, he cannot show his nature, it is to be modified. He has to give up his natural tendency and act according to the position. He may have to be miserly and, without sympathy, he may have to execute orders. Therefore occupation modifies and changes one's nature.

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=268|p. 268]]

A person, by company gets improved or spoiled. So also, the dasa lord by the conjunction of any other planet will give different results.

Twins are born in the same lagna, with the same position of the planets and dasa bhukti.

Do they not have the same dasa lord and the same Gochara-Transit results? They do. But yet, are not the results different? The name of the dasa lord is the same. Its lordship is the same. Its occupation is also the same. Then, why so much of difference in the results ?

Therefore this threat of Eka Dasa has no meaning. For most of the people nothing untoward had happened, nor can it happen. A few may suffer. It is not due to Eka Dasa, but because the lord of the dasa is not beneficial for them.

Readers need not take into consideration any of the threatenings of the astrologer, who rejects a horoscope by mentioning "Eka Dasa or Dasa Sandhi".

Each horoscope has to be judged independently. Whatever is found in the chart will alone come to pass. By marrying one having Eka-Dasa or Dasa Sandhi, one will never alter the results indicated by one's chart.
