The following questions are raised:—

- Q. 1 Is marriage promised?
- Q. 2 If so, when, where, and with whom?

## Question 1: Is marriage promised?

**Answer:** Yes. Venus in a fruitful sign, and in 1) [sic] from Moon, is an indication that marriage is promised. But Saturn in 1, forming an aspect with Venus shows delays, impediments, and disappointments, yet not a total denial since the Saturn, Venus quintile aspect is good, and Venus in Vargothamamsa is definitely stronger than Saturn in debility. Therefore a delayed marriage is promised.

## Question 2: If so, when, with whom, and where?

**Answer:** Marriage will take place during the conjoined periods of the significators for houses 2, 7 and 11, when transits are also in harmony.

## Planets and Bhavas

[[J_KP reader_4_Marriage-married-Life-Children.pdf#page=129|p. 129]]

The significators. Houses 2 and 7 are vacant. Both are owned by Venus. Jupiter and Saturn are in Venus star and Venus sub. So both are strong significators.

Moon and Mercury are in 11. Sun and Rahu are in Moon's star. Mars is in Mercury's star.

Rahu in Virgo represents Mercury and Kethu in Pisces represents Jupiter.

Of these, Jupiter, Saturn and Mars do not occupy the star or sub of planets owning houses 6, 8 or 12. We are presently not judging 6, 8 or 12, and so we shall take Jupiter, Saturn, and Mars as the strongest significators.

At the time of judgment, i.e., 3-12-1968, 10-45 A.M. I.S.T. at Hyderabad, the day is Tuesday, ruled by Mars. The star is Krithika ruled by Sun, Moon transiting Aries of Mars, while Saturn's Capricorn is ascending in the Eastern horizon. Therefore, Jupiter, Saturn, Mars and Sun are the planets who will bring about marriage.

Jupiter Dasa cannot bring about a result since other considerations indicate delay in marriage. Saturn Dasa, therefore, will bless the native with marriage.

At present, Saturn Dasa—Moon Bhukti is running, and lasts upto 12-3-1970. The next bhukti, that of Mars, between 13-3-1970 and 21-4-1971 is favourable, and requires closer scrutiny for further reduction in time range. The first opportunity, after providing for Saturn's delay, occurs in April 1970 when Saturn transits the star of Venus, while Jupiter transits Mars star in Venus sign, Libra. At that time, Saturn in debility in Aries in opposition to Jupiter in Libra will have the least delaying effect.

Hence, marriage will take place during April 1970. Jupiter, Saturn and Mars owning houses 1, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 show that the husband will be a distant friend, and the marriage will involve a long journey from the birth place. He will be well-versed in arts holding an independent position. Lord of 2 and 7, Venus, in the star of Sun, lord of 5, and occupant of 10, suggest that the native will be employed, or financially independent from her husband.
