# Marriage Is Made in Heavens

"Marriage is made in Heavens, God is great". This is proved in the following case.

A girl named Bhagyam was born in a family known to me. We were of equal age. We were class-mates. When she was 11 years old she had her marriage celebrated with one, named Madhana Gopala Krishnan. He was also around 16 years of age, then. One year, two years, and then years after years passed on The mother of the girl was very anxious as the girl did not report that she had attained puberty. Anyhow, since they were, even before marriage, related, the mother of the girl and the father of the boy agreed to set family for both Bhagyam and Madana Gopala Krishnan in the neighboring town. They lead their life for decades most happily. The nature denied them the pleasure through animal life. They did not have such a pleasure, because he cannot prove himself to be a man and his generative system was also equally defective and useless. This girl is also not fit for such a pleasure. But somehow, the parents have given the name Bhaygam, meaning fortune, luck, pleasure etc. So also Krishna means, one who will be in the midst of girls who are the devotees. Madhana Gopal Krishnan should mean, the utmost happiness and pleasure much more than Krishna. But see ! The unfit man bad married the unfit girl. This is the action of God according to their Karma, which proves to show that "God is great and Marriage is fixed in the Heavens."
