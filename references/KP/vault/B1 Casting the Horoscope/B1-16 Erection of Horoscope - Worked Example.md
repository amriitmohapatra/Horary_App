# Erection of Horoscope - Worked Example

## Particulars of Birth

- Date of Birth: 11-6-1930
- Time of Birth: 7-37 a.m. I.S.T.
- Place of Birth: Belgaum

## Procedure

### First Step: Find Out the Local Mean Time

Note the place of birth, next refer Krishnamurti Ephemeris Volume I, pages ix to xi and note the Latitude and Longitude of the place of birth. Indian Standard Time is always 5 hours 30 mts. in advance of Greenwich Mean Time (1° = 4 mts.). Therefore Indian Standard Time is fixed for 82° 30' East Longitude.

Place of Birth — Belgaum — Latitude 15° 52', Longitude 74° 34' E.

Difference between I.S.T. and G.M.T. is 5 hours 30 mts.

    7-37 a.m. I.S.T. means (7-37) - (5-30) = 2-07 a.m. G.M.T.
    Longitude E; 1° = 4 minutes
    74° 34' × 4 = 298 mts. 16 secs. = 4 hours 58 minutes 16 secs.
    Hence L.M.T. = 2-07 + 4-58-16 = 7 hours 5 minutes 16 secs.

TIME OF BIRTH 7-37 a.m. I.S.T. = 7-5-16 a.m. L.M.T.

A more simple method will be as follows:

Place of Birth: Belgaum. Longitude 74° 34' E. Indian Standard Time is fixed for 82° 30' E Longitude. Now note the difference between the Longitude 82° 30' E and 74° 34' E, place of birth i.e., (82° 30') - (74° 34') = 7° 56'.

    1° = 4 mts.
    7° 56' × 4 = 31 mts. 44 secs.

(Local Mean Time will be greater than Indian Standard Time if the Longitude of the place of birth is greater than 82° 30' E and if the Longitude of the place is less than 82° 30' E, then L.M.T. is less than the I.S.T.)

    (7-37) - (0-31-44) = 7h-5m-16s a.m. L.M.T.

### Second Step: Find Out the Sidereal Time

Take the Krishnamurti's Ephemeris Vol. I, and turn over to page 117 i.e., June 1930. As the time of birth is before 5-30 p.m. L.M.T., take the sidereal time given for the previous date 5-30 p.m. L.M.T. Suppose the birth is above 5-30 p.m. L.M.T., then take the sidereal time given for the same date). Sidereal time at 5-30 p.m. on 10-6-1930, as is given in Krishnamurti's Ephemeris is 10° 42' 28" (Correction for Longitude i.e., 7° 56' @ 213 sec. per hour is negligible - so omit.)

(Note: — For birth in India, one need not deduct the correction if the Longitude of place of birth is above 82° 30' E or add the correction if the Longitude of place of birth is less than 82° 30' E. The Longitude of India is between 70° E to 90° East. So even if the Longitudinal difference is 12° between locality of birth and 82° 30' E, the correction that we have to give will be a maximum of 8 seconds only i.e., (12° × 1 secs). As none can note the time of birth correct to a second and the movement of the Zodiac is an average of 1/30 of a degree for every 4 seconds, this calculation to correct can be conveniently omitted.)

    Add interval between previous 5-30 p.m. and time of birth in L.M.T.   =  13h-35m-16s
    Add correction for the time interval, 10 seconds per hour             =   0h-02m-16s
                                                                             -------------
    ∴ Sidereal time at the time of birth                                  =  24h-20m-00s
    Deduct 24 hours                                                       =  24h-00m-00s
                                                                             -------------
    Therefore sidereal time at the moment of birth                        =   0h-20m-00s

### Third Step: Find Out the Position of Cusps

Taking the table of houses (Raphael's table of houses for Northern Latitudes) and referring to the page where the cusps of houses are furnished for 16° N (which is near to 15° 52' N i.e., Latitude of Belgaum; place of birth) it will be seen that the sidereal time at time of birth i.e., 0h-20m-00s falls between the two timings found in the book. They are 0h-18m-21s and 0h-22m-02s.

[[J_KP reader_1_casting the horoscope.pdf#page=163|p. 163]]

The position of cusps given in the Raphael's table of houses is noted below.

| Sidereal Time | 10th cusp (Aries) | 11th cusp (Taurus) | 12th cusp (Gemini) | Ascendant (Cancer) | 2nd cusp (Leo) | 3rd cusp (Virgo) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0h-18m-21s | 5° | 8° | 11° | 10° 39' | 6° | 4° |
| 0h-22m-02s | 6° | 9° | 12° | 11° 28' | 7° | 5° |

The Ascendant moves from 10° 39' to 11° 28' i.e., 49'. In (0h-22m-02s) - (0h-18m-21s) = 3 mts. 41 secs. = 221 secs. The birth is at 0h-20m-00s i.e., 99 secs after 0h-18m-21s. This means that the ascendant has moved 49' in 221 secs and [?in] 99 secs. It moves 49/221 × 99 = 22'. Adding 22' to the position of ascendant at 0h-18m-21s, we get the position of ascendant for the required sidereal time i.e., 0h-20m-00s.

Therefore Ascendant is Cancer 11° 01'.

The other cusps moved by 1° = 60' during 221 seconds. In 99 secs they move 60/221 × 99 = 27'. Adding 27' to the position of cusps 10th, 11th, 12th, 2nd, and 3rd at 0h-18m-21s, we get the position of cusps (tabled below) for sidereal time 0h-20m-00s.

| Sidereal time | 10th cusp (Aries) | 11th cusp (Taurus) | 12th cusp (Gemini) | Ascendant (Cancer) | 2nd cusp (Leo) | 3rd cusp (Virgo) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0h-20m-00s | 5° 27' | 8° 27' | 11° 27' | 11° 01' | 6° 27' | 4° 27' |

Add 180° to the above cusps to find out the position of cusps for the other houses. Thus the position of all the twelve cusps are obtained.

### Fourth Step: Position of Planets

(b) The proportionate motion of the Planet for 14 hours 7 minutes and 0 seconds can be calculated. ### Sixth Step: Calculation of Dasa Balance at Birth

Moon at the time of birth was in 234° 40' in Jyeshta Star, which extends from 226° 40' to 240° (Scorpio). Therefore the portion to be covered by Moon is 5° 20' = 320'. 'Mercury' has 17 years of dasa. The Longitude of star is 800'.

    ∴ The balance of Dasa at birth is 320/800 × 17 = 6y-9m-18d.
