# Jinendramala and Bhuvana Dipakam - Extracts

[[J_KP reader_6_Horary Astrology.pdf#page=67|p. 67]]

One should approach an astrologer with such a devotion and affection similar to a devotee of God and the pet child of a mother. Unless one is sincere, even Veda is not useful to him. Whenever one approaches a manthravadi, or doctor or an astrologer, one should never go empty handed.

Scars: Sun, Mars, Mercury and Jupiter give marks on the right side, whereas Moon, Venus, Saturn and Nodes cause moles and marks on the left side.

Cancer and Scorpio indicate reptiles; Capricorn and Pisces show birds. Other signs denote two-legged or four-legged creations of God. Moon and Nodes indicate the reptiles and also those who do not have legs; Mercury indicates birds; others denote human beings and animals. Saturn indicates lame or fractured or amputated leg.

Aries and Sagittarius denote forest; Taurus—agricultural land; Gemini and Virgo—village; Cancer—canal; Leo—Hill and Mountain; Libra and Capricorn—river; Scorpio—well; Aquarius—water.

Aries—forest; Taurus—agricultural land; Gemini—village; Cancer—canal; Leo—hill; Virgo—water; Libra—village; Scorpio—well; Sagittarius—garden; Capricorn—dry canal; Aquarius—lake; and Pisces—Ocean.

Sun, Mars and Saturn—forest; Moon, Venus—water; Mercury, Jupiter—village; Nodes—bush; Sun and Jupiter—pooja room; Moon and Venus—water. Mars—earth, Mercury—resting place; Saturn—the area where religious functions are done, or kitchen; Nodes—the hole in the tree, or anywhere there is the hole.

Saturn and Nodes indicate one year; Sun denotes six months; Mercury two months; Jupiter one month; Venus 15 days; Mars one day and Moon [?1½] hours. This is to find out the time needed to materialise.

[[J_KP reader_6_Horary Astrology.pdf#page=68|p. 68]]

Sun—[?trees]; Mars—produce from dry land; Mercury and Venus—produce from wet land; Jupiter and Venus—sugarcane and same species; Saturn and Nodes—thorny trees.

Leo and Sagittarius—trees. Taurus, Cancer and Libra—creepers; Aries and Scorpio—produce from dry land; Pisces—sugarcane; Gemini and Virgo—plants without thorn, Capricorn and Aquarius—thorny trees.

Sun—trees in the forest; Moon—planting trees; Jupiter—Coconut trees; Saturn and Nodes—palm trees; Mercury and Venus—domestic garden; Mars—plants etc., Saturn indicates ginger, saffron and such bulbs which grow under the ground.

Sun indicates barks; Moon—bulbs; Mars—flowers; Mercury—leaf. Jupiter and Venus—fruit; Saturn—root; and Nodes—creepers.

Sun, Moon and Saturn indicate dry trees and others show fruit-bearing ones. The colour of the fruit will be similar to the planet. Sun indicates beans (wheat); Moon—white sesame. Mars—dhall; Mercury—green dhall; Jupiter—Bengalgram; Venus—all white ones and Saturn—sesame, Nodes—black dhall, horsegram, etc.

Sun indicates tiger. Moon—milking animals. Mars—goat; Mercury—rabbit; Jupiter—horse; Venus—cow; Saturn and Nodes—buffaloes.

If Sun and Jupiter signify the animal, then it will be pregnant. Moon and Venus indicate that they will give plenty of milk. Saturn and Nodes indicate that they have no calves. No more issue. Mars indicates that they are dry and also cunning.

Thus Horary Astrology has been described in this book and various other rules which are found in Sinendramala are similar to those already furnished in this book. Those which are not found in the other text-books had been published for the benefit of the readers.

## Horary Astrology

*By Shri Padmaprapusuri*

He prays to Lord Ganesh and describes the fundamentals like the names of 12 signs; planets; the exaltation, debilitation; etc.

According to him, morning is indicated by Jupiter and Mercury. Evening is indicated by Moon and Venus. The day, by Sun and Mars whereas Rahu and Saturn denote the time of sunset.

Jupiter and Venus denote two-legged ones, Sun and Mars represent four-legged ones. Saturn and Mercury denote birds; Moon and Rahu, the reptiles. Moon indicates large ones, Venus flat one; Sun and Mars square; Mercury and Jupiter round; Rahu and Saturn tall. (Hindu authors give different versions.)

Mars indicates red colour; Mercury green; Jupiter golden; Moon handsome; Sun brilliant red; Venus white; Saturn and Rahu, black.

Mercury indicates child; Mars young man; Moon and Venus adults; Sun, Jupiter, Saturn and Rahu indicate aged people.

Then, he describes what the twelve houses indicate.

He also gives the method, by which one has to measure out the shadow and calculate the time of query. This is not at all applicable to different Latitudes, since in many latitudes, shadow is so long, that it cannot be measured at all. And this method may be tried by people living close to the Equator. Application of this rule is limited. Only day time is to be preferred. Cloudy days are out of question. When time-piece is available, there is no use for this method. Then, he gives the method by which one is to find out whether the lagna is strong, and also the bhava to be judged, is strong. It is general, and it can never come and it will mostly fail. Then, he deals with eclipse planet. He claims that when lord of lagna aspects the lagna which is to be judged, then the promotion of the bhava of the success is to be predicted. This is also not correct. He gives his opinion that if lord of lagna or lord of 11 is situated in the 11th house, one will gain. Stellar Astrology disproves this rule. Again, his opinion about the houses 6, 8 and 12 is not correct.

[[J_KP reader_6_Horary Astrology.pdf#page=70|p. 70]]

In Stanza 87, he discusses whether one will have safe delivery or not. He says that if lord of 5 is deposited or is aspecting the 8th, then the delivery is not easy; if an evil planet is in 5, or aspects 5, one is to predict miscarriage. But, in practice, it was found that lord 5 in 5, deposited in the constellation of lord of 5 in 1, has caused cesarian operation. Constellation theory alone, will prove to be successful. He says that twin birth is to be predicted, if 8 planets are deposited in 4 houses, 2 in each. Such a combination will be very rare. (So, research cannot be successfully done in one's lifetime.) He says that the age of the intra-uterine life of the Foetus is, as many number of months as the number of houses, where Venus is situated from the lagna. Thus, he goes on dealing in his book.

My opinion is that the whole thing is general and is not at all helpful to exactly pin-point the nature of the event and the time of the event.

As regards disease, he says Vayu; Pitta; Slesma like that. All out of date. Anyhow, I have to say that it is not useful for modern times.
