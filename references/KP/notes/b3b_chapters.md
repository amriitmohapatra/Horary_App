## Corrections found while checking examples against an ephemeris

Positions were recomputed (Swiss Ephemeris Moshier, Krishnamurti ayanamsa) for every dated chart.

- **Fig. B3.58 (B3-42)**: printed "30-8-1932" is wrong. The chart, the Sun dasa balance 2y10m11d and "Jupiter dasa from 11-5-1970" all fit **30-6-1932** at noon (Asc Vi 4°41', MC Ge 4°33' computed).
- **Fig. B3.61 (B3-46)**: printed "27-10-1942, 7-32 p.m." cannot be right: in 1942 Saturn was in Taurus, but the chart has Saturn in Libra 5°03'. The positions, the Rahu balance 10y9m10d and "Mercury dasa from 6-9-69" fit **26-11-1923, afternoon** (about 2–4 p.m. IST).
- **Fig. B3.73 (B3-73)**: printed "26-2-66" (a Saturday) contradicts "today is Monday"; positions fit **28-2-1966** (a Monday).
- **Fig. B3.80 (B3-74)**: "8-11-1942" in the figure and "9-11-1942" in the text are the same birth: 4-26 a.m. on 9-11 (civil, a Monday) is before sunrise, so the Hindu weekday is Sunday (8-11). That is why Krishnamurti counts the day lord as Sun.
