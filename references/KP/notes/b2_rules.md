# B2 "Fundamental Principles of Astrology": rule catalogue (KP2-nnn)

Reader: agent b2. Source: `references/KP/vault/B2 Fundamental Principles of Astrology/`.
Citations are PDF pages ("B2 p. 300"). B2 has one figure, the cover, so no rule here can be
graded VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE. The grades are **CLEAR** (the transcription reads coherently) and
**UNCLEAR** (the print or transcription is doubtful; the reason is given). Status values follow the
brief: EXISTS / COMPUTABLE / GUIDANCE / HORARY / REJECTED-TRADITION.

IDs are grouped by theme, and the numbering has gaps between sections on purpose. ★ marks
Krishnamurti's own stellar doctrine; everything else is traditional (Hindu or Western)
material that he reports. "Connected with a house" in B2 means occupying it, owning it,
aspecting it or its cusp, conjoining its lord, or (in the KP passages) sitting in the star of
its occupant or lord. B2 never defines the term more precisely.

Format: `- **ID** · **Title.** Condition → meaning. · (B2 p. N) · grade · status · notes`

## 1. Foundations: houses, strength, derived houses

- **KP2-001** · **House = cusp to cusp.** A house (bhava) runs from its own cusp to the next cusp. The 1st bhava starts at the degree rising in the East and runs to the 2nd cusp; the 12th bhava is the part that has just risen. · (B2 p. 281) · CLEAR · EXISTS · This is the app's Placidus placement rule ("house h spans cusp h → cusp h+1").
- **KP2-002** · **Luminaries count as planets; sign lordship.** Sun owns Leo and Moon owns Cancer, one sign each. The other five planets own two signs each. Uranus and Neptune own no sign of their own. · (B2 p. 173, 479) · CLEAR · EXISTS · Sign-lord table already in the app.
- **KP2-003** · **Nakshatra padas contained in each sign.** Aries: Aswini, Bharani, Krithika 1. Taurus: Krithika 2-4, Rohini, Mrigasira 1-2. Gemini: Mrigasira 3-4, Arudra, Punarvasu 1-3. Cancer: Punarvasu 4, Pushya, Aslesha. Leo: Makam, Pooram, Uttaram 1. Virgo: Uttaram 2-4, Hastham, Chithra 1-2. Libra: Chithra 3-4, Swathi, Visakam 1-3. Scorpio: Visakam 4, Anuradha, Jyeshta. Sagittarius: Moolam, Pooradam, Uttaradam 1. Capricorn: Uttaradam 2-4, Sravanam, Dhanishta 1-2. Aquarius: Dhanishta 3-4, Sathabhisha, Poorvabhadra 1-3. Pisces: Poorvabhadra 4, Uttarabhadra, Revathi. · (B2 pp. 133, 147, 160, 172, 187, 205, 216, 227, 238, 253, 267) · CLEAR · EXISTS · Matches the app's 27×4 pada table. Aries's stars are not listed in the Aries chapter; they follow by arithmetic.
- **KP2-004** · **Nirayana Sun ingress dates.** Krishnamurti's fixed-zodiac dates: Aries 13/14 Apr, Sagittarius 15 Dec–14 Jan, Capricorn 14 Jan–12 Feb, Aquarius 13 Feb–12 Mar, Pisces 12 Mar–13 Apr ("throughout Kaliyuga"). · (B2 pp. 127, 228, 238, 254, 268) · CLEAR · EXISTS · Transit engine. "Throughout Kaliyuga" is loose: the dates drift with the ayanamsa.
- **KP2-005** · **Ayanamsa at the time of writing.** The vernal equinox stands at 6°44' Nirayana Pisces, in the 2nd pada of Uttarabhadra (Saturn star, Venus sub). That puts the ayanamsa at about 23°16'. · (B2 p. 71) · CLEAR · EXISTS · Useful only as a sanity check on the KP ayanamsa for the 1960s–70s. Also used as a mundane "Aquarian age" argument.
- **KP2-006** · **Dharma-artha-kama-moksha houses.** Houses 9, 10, 11, 12 = dharma, artha, kama, moksha (para-loka sadhana). Houses 1–8 = worldly pleasures and pains (iha-loka). Aquarius is the kama sign for para-loka sadhana, opposite Leo (worldly pleasure). · (B2 pp. 256–257, 327, 338) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE ·
- **KP2-008** · **Derived houses (bhavat bhavam).** Every house also signifies the matters of relatives, counted from the house that represents them. The 3rd counted from the 9th (the father's 3rd) is the father's short journeys, the 8th from the 9th is danger to the father, and so on. B2 gives a full derivative list for each house (examples: 1st = gains of the younger sibling, the mother's fame, the children's long journeys, end of open enmity, the father's speculation; 2nd = the younger sibling's loss, the mother's gains, the children's profession, the maternal uncle's long journey, danger to the partner, the father's disease…). · (B2 pp. 283, 288–289, 292, 296, 299, 302, 306, 311, 315, 325, 337, 353) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · A generic (house − base + 1) mod 12 relabelling. The app can show "for relative R, house k of the native = house j of R". Relatives as B2 assigns them: 3 = younger sibling, 4 = mother, 5 = children, 6 = maternal uncle / servants, 7 = partner, 9 = father, 11 = elder sibling / friend, 12 = secret enemy.
- **KP2-009** · **The 12th from any house negates it.** The 12th counted from a house is detrimental to that house's matters. Examples: 2 = 12th from 3 (span of life), so it is a maraka; 7 = 12th from 8; 3 = change of residence (12th from 4); 5 = hospitalisation of pets (12th from 6); 11 = discharge from hospital (12th from 12). · (B2 pp. 287, 290, 299, 305, 337) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The logic behind the marakas (KP2-084/085). B6 uses the same "12th from" idea for denial.
- **KP2-010** · **A house and its 7th act alike (reflex).** Sanketanidhi sl. 106: a bhava and its 7th "have similar aims"; the good or bad effects of the 11th are the same as those of planets in the 5th, and vice versa. Hence the 11th also bears on children. · (B2 p. 329) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE · Supports the 2-5-11 child group (KP2-109).
- **KP2-011** · **Angular / succedent / cadent strength.** Planets in 1, 4, 7, 10 (kendra) have full power to express their nature and lordship. Planets in 2, 5, 8, 11 (panaphara) have moderate power; planets in 3, 6, 9, 12 (apoklima) the least, so the native "lacks opportunities at needed times". The three groups correspond to movable, fixed and common signs. · (B2 p. 316) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · A placement lookup. Raphael's remark (p. 338) that evil planets in the 12th are "far less evil than when angular" follows from this.
- **KP2-012** · **Upachaya houses.** 3, 6, 10, 11 are upachaya (improving) houses. Even malefics placed there prove auspicious and bring profit (Varahamihira). The 6th is called evil in one sloka and good for malefics in another, and needs interpretation. · (B2 pp. 301–302, 330) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · B2 p. 469 overrides it for nodes: Rahu in the star of the lords of 6/8/12 gives bad results "even though the house may be… upachaya" (KP2-054).
- **KP2-013** · **A planet's power to give its own house's results depends on where it sits relative to its own sign.** Pisces is a "friendly" sign to Sun, Moon and Mars, yet only the Moon benefits there (Pisces is 9th from Cancer, a trine to its own sign). Mars is weak in Pisces at giving Aries matters (12th from Aries), and the Sun is weak at giving Leo matters (8th from Leo). · (B2 p. 268) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Count the planet's sign from each sign it owns. 6th, 8th and 12th from its own sign = weak delivery; trines = good. Krishnamurti says "it appeals to me", so this is his own view.
- **KP2-014** · **Functional enmity comes from 6/8 lordship.** For natives of Venus's signs, Jupiter owns the 6th or 8th. For natives of Jupiter's signs, Venus owns the 6th or 8th. The same holds for Mars towards Mercury-sign natives and Mercury towards Mars-sign natives. That, not myth, is why these pairs are called enemies. · (B2 p. 409) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Check: Taurus lagna → Jupiter owns 8 and 11; Libra lagna → Jupiter owns 3 and 6; Sagittarius lagna → Venus owns 6 and 11; Pisces lagna → Venus owns 3 and 8. Correct.
- **KP2-015** · **No house is evil in itself.** Benefics occupying a house improve its matters; malefics in a house destroy its affairs. 6, 8, 12 are dusthanas, and Hindu sages hold their lordship productive of evil, "but no house is unfortunate or evil unless afflicted". The 8th is "evil" only as the 2nd from the 7th (the other party's gain). Benefics in the 8th mean gain to the partner (Jataka Tatva). · (B2 pp. 310, 338) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE ·

## 2. How a planet gives results (★ the stellar doctrine in B2)

- **KP2-030** · **A sign shows its pure nature only when empty.** The descriptions of a sign apply undeviated only when no planet occupies it. They come through more strongly if the sign's lord sits in it. Benefics (Jupiter, Venus) in the lagna mitigate the bad side; malefics (Saturn, Mars) bring the latent bad qualities into action. · (B2 pp. 126, 146–147, 173) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE · The display condition for all the sign texts (KP2-161…172): show them for the lagna sign, and say whether it is occupied.
- **KP2-031** · ★ **A planet gives the results of its star lord (Saturn example).** "Saturn ruling the constellations Pushya, Anuradha and Uttarabhadra will modify the results of planets in any of the three stars so much that what Saturn has to offer to the native by its lordship and occupation is given by the planet in its star(s)." · (B2 p. 441) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The only general statement of the star-lord principle in B2. The app's significator levels A/C already encode it. B3-06 p. 56 states it generally ("a planet offers the result of the lord of the constellation more predominantly than those indicated by the sign or house"). Agreement.
- **KP2-032** · ★ **For the 12th house, the occupant's star matters more than occupation or lordship.** "It is not the occupation or lordship of the 12th house that matters most. What is more important is the constellation in which a planet was posited in the 12th house and what houses are owned by the constellation lord." Example: a planet in the 12th in the star of the lords of 2 and 9 means long journeys, separation from the family, and living far from home. · (B2 p. 339) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Written about the 12th, but the wording is generic. Same as KP2-031.
- **KP2-033** · **Planets in the 11th always do good.** "Planets in 11 will invariably do good"; the 11th "alone is the most auspicious" for fulfilling desires. Saturn in 11 is very good; Saturn in 12 is "very very bad" (the Indrajit story). · (B2 p. 439) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE · Compare KP2-047 (Saturn in 11 and the timing of sub periods).
- **KP2-034** · **Mercury is "convertible".** Mercury does not act independently. It expresses the nature of the planet it is conjoined with or aspected by: benefic when connected with benefics, malefic when connected with evil planets. · (B2 p. 394) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Natural benefic/malefic status of Mercury should be derived from its conjunctions and aspects.
- **KP2-035** · **Nodes are evil by nature but turn benefic by association.** Rahu and Kethu are not independent planets and are always opposite each other. They are "by nature evil… yet by association they are capable of doing good; by association with benefics they turn benefic". · (B2 p. 456) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Detailed agent rules: KP2-050…060.
- **KP2-036** · **Kalapurusha.** Sun = soul, Moon = mind, Mercury = speech, Mars = strength, Jupiter = knowledge and happiness, Venus = desire, Saturn = misery. Each part is strong or weak as its planet is strong or weak. The exception is Saturn: a weak Saturn means more misery, a strong Saturn less. The effects are felt in the planets' own periods. Planetary cabinet: Sun king, Moon queen, Jupiter and Venus ministers, Mercury prince, Mars commander-in-chief, Saturn servant. · (B2 p. 356) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE ·
- **KP2-037** · **Opposite indications run in separate periods; they do not cancel.** Rahu in 11 (many children) and Kethu in 5 (loss of children) in the same chart are not a contradiction. Rahu protects the children in its dasa and sub periods, and Kethu causes abortion or ill-health in its own. "Any planet indicating any result can offer such during its period and sub period." · (B2 p. 468) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Display rule: attach each indication to its planet's periods. Never net them out.
- **KP2-038** · **Afflictions bite only in the afflicting planet's periods.** When a house is afflicted, its contrary results come "only when the native runs the period or sub period of the planet afflicting the house… and on the days, nakshatras and lagnas ruled by it, and not otherwise". The house's matters improve in the period of a planet beneficially connected to it. · (B2 p. 338) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Compute with the dasa engine: list the afflicting planets of a house and flag their periods.
- **KP2-039** · **Period results arrive on the period lord's day, lagna and Moon-star.** A Sun period gives its results on a Sunday, when Leo rises, or on days when the Moon transits Karthigai, Uttaraphalguni or Uttarashada (Sun's stars). Venus: Fridays and the Moon in Bharani, Pooram, Pooradam. Saturn: Saturdays and the Moon in Pushya, Anuradha, Uttarabhadra. · (B2 pp. 338, 364, 427, 451) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · A fine-timing filter on top of dasa-bhukti-anthra. Moon transits of the period lord's stars come from the transit engine. B3-05 has a related zone-transit timing rule.
- **KP2-040** · **Aspects deliver in joint periods and in progression or transit.** The results of an aspect between two planets come when the native runs the joint periods of those two planets (dasa, bhukti, anthra; "Ududasa system"). They also come when the two planets form the aspect again by progression or by transit; the effect in progression is "much greater" than in transit. · (B2 pp. 381, 401, 427, 429) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Joint periods can be computed now. Progressions are not in the app (secondary progressions would be new).
- **KP2-041** · **A mole is congenital; a scar forms in a planet's period.** Evil planets in the 2nd cause a mole or scar on the parts the 2nd rules. A mole is present from infancy; a scar forms when the native runs the period of the planet afflicting the 2nd. Malefics in the 2nd cause trouble with the family in their periods and sub periods. · (B2 p. 286) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE · Generalises to every house (p. 291: "malefics in any house affect that part of the body"). See KP2-065.
- **KP2-042** · **Aspects to the ascendant degree show health.** Good aspects to the cuspal degree of the ascendant portray good health. Bad aspects threaten ill health during the period of the planet throwing the bad aspect. · (B2 p. 281) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Aspects to the cusp degree, not to the bhava (KP2-195).
- **KP2-043** · **Jupiter as benefic or as afflicted lord of evil houses.** A Jupiter that is benefic by lordship gives respect and reputation in its period. A Jupiter that is afflicted, owns evil houses, and sits in evil signs and stars gives extremism: extravagance, debts, failed speculation, standing surety, gluttony. · (B2 pp. 412–413) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · An example of natural nature × functional lordship × star.
- **KP2-044** · **One house can be good for the native and bad for a relative.** The native may stay healthy yet visit hospital for an ailing relative and worry about them. · (B2 p. 283) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE · Display caveat for KP2-008.
- **KP2-045** · **The Saturn–Venus paradox.** A Saturn that is benefic by lordship should not be strong (own sign, exalted, vargottama) if it is to give good results during a Venus sub period. Kalidasa: a strong raja-yoga Saturn in the Venus period "will make one beg alms in the street even if born a King". · (B2 p. 441) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The classical Sani–Sukra dasa exchange rule. Computable: flag Saturn-dasa/Venus-bhukti and the reverse when Saturn is a strong yogakaraka. Vargottama needs the navamsa, which the app has.
- **KP2-046** · **"Saturnians" act out Saturn's role.** People born on Saturdays, with Capricorn or Aquarius lagna, or in Pushya, Anuradha or Uttarabhadra play the role of Saturn for good or evil in the native's Saturn period. Do not hire a Saturnian as private secretary if Saturn is evil (Pisces lagna). Analogous groups: Sun-, Venus- and Mars-people, used under Libra, Sagittarius and Capricorn (KP2-138). · (B2 pp. 274, 443) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE · A natal-matching heuristic: compare another person's weekday, lagna and star with a planet.
- **KP2-047** · **A benefic Saturn delivers in its own sub, not before.** With Saturn in the 11th, beneficial: "under the dasa-bhukti system the beneficial Saturn will give the favourable results in its sub period, and Saturn will not allow other planets to offer such results in their sub periods which precede Saturn." · (B2 p. 334) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · A timing rule: the promised event waits for Saturn's bhukti within the running dasa.
- **KP2-048** · **Jupiter dasa expands; Saturn dasa consolidates.** Under any lagna, a businessman expands and opens branches in a Jupiter dasa. In a Saturn dasa, or the dasa of planets in Capricorn, he consolidates and concentrates. · (B2 p. 245) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE ·
- **KP2-049** · **How promotion comes, by planet.** If Mercury gives the promotion, the superior is transferred and the native fills the vacancy. If Saturn gives it, the senior "mostly will pass away". If Jupiter gives it, the organisation expands or a post is created (p. 415). A favourable Mars–Jupiter aspect is needed to become GM, MD, bank agent, director, judge or religious head, and the native holds such a post in the two planets' conjoined period (p. 416). With a favourable Mercury–Jupiter aspect the native enters service in their joint periods (or one's sub period within the other's sub-sub) and is promoted in the later dasa of either (p. 402). Sun and Mercury close together → promotion in Mercury dasa, Sun bhukti (p. 400). · (B2 pp. 400, 402, 415–416) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE (timing) + GUIDANCE (style) ·

## 3. Nodes (★ agent doctrine)

- **KP2-050** · ★ **Nodes act as agents.** Rahu and Kethu own no sign, so they represent the lord of the sign they occupy. But a node "will act as a strong agent to the planet with which it is conjoined. If not conjoined with any planet, it gives the results of the planet which aspects it. Only when neither conjoined nor aspected does it represent the lord of the sign." Order: conjunct planet → aspecting planet → sign lord. · (B2 pp. 461–462) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The app has no node-agent rule (its vector puts the dispositor first). B3-10 p. 145 inserts the **star lord** between the aspecting planet and the sign lord, and B2 p. 472 adds star and sub (KP2-052). So B2 p. 461 is the oldest and least complete version.
- **KP2-051** · ★ **Nodes are stronger than their partners.** "Rahu and Kethu are ever stronger than other planets whether they are conjoined with or aspected by a planet." With Venus and Rahu together in Pisces, Libra or Taurus, Rahu gives Venus's results "to a greater extent than Venus itself", even if Venus is exalted, vargottama or in its own sign. If Venus is a significator, Rahu becomes the stronger significator of that matter. · (B2 pp. 470–471) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Significator ordering: a node conjunct a significator ranks above it. Agrees with B3's rule that nodes are stronger significators.
- **KP2-052** · ★ **Nodes are agents of sign lord, star lord and sub lord.** "Rahu and Kethu are to be taken as the agents of the lord of the sign, the lord of the star and the lord of the sub. They are capable of producing the results caused by the combination." Example: Rahu at Taurus 26°36' is in a Venus sign, the Mars star Mrigasira and the Jupiter sub. The native got a manager's job in an automobile industry in Rahu dasa – Jupiter bhukti – Venus anthra – Mars sookshma. · (B2 p. 472) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The printed longitude "26° - 36°" means 26°36' Taurus = 56°36'. Arithmetic check: Mrigasira starts at 53°20'; the Mars sub runs to 54°06'40", Rahu to 56°06'40", Jupiter to 57°53'20". So 56°36' is in the Jupiter sub. Correct. Example b2-E02. Note that the timing chain runs through the sub lord, the sign lord and the star lord in that order.
- **KP2-053** · ★ **When a node is benefic.** A node is benefic if it is conjoined with or aspected by the lords of beneficial houses. Krishnamurti adds ("according to me") that it is also benefic if it occupies the constellation **and** sub of planets owning favourable houses. Example: Capricorn lagna, Venus benefic as 10th lord, so Rahu is benefic if conjoined/aspected by Venus, or in Bharani, Pooram or Pooradam, or in a Venus sub ("which will extend to 2°13'20" in each constellation"). The same applies to Kethu. · (B2 p. 469) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Venus sub width 20/120 × 13°20' = 2°13'20". Correct.
- **KP2-054** · ★ **A node in the star of the lords of 6, 8 or 12 gives bad results.** "Rahu in the constellation of the lords of 6, 8 and 12 will provide undesirable results, even though the house (solar mansion and bhava) may be considered a beneficial one [upachaya]." · (B2 p. 469) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE ·
- **KP2-055** · ★ **A node's aspect = the aspect of its star lord and its sign lord.** When Venus receives an aspect from Rahu or Kethu: find the star and sign the node is in, and treat the lords of that star and that sign as if each aspected Venus. Both results come, especially in the periods of those star and sign lords within a Venus dasa or Venus bhukti. · (B2 p. 435) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Written under Venus, general in form.
- **KP2-056** · ★ **A node gives the matters of both signs of its dispositor.** A node in a sign gives the results of that house and sign, and also of the other sign ruled by the same lord (every planet except Sun and Moon rules two). · (B2 p. 471) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Equivalent to adding the dispositor's ownerships to the node's vector (the app already does this: `[dispositor placement, node placement, dispositor ownerships…]`). The text supports the existing vector.
- **KP2-057** · ★ **A node as indicator of profession.** If the lord of 2, 6 or 10 is conjoined with or aspects Rahu or Kethu, that node indicates the profession. The native enters service in the node's sub period, "if the lord of the constellation is also a significator". · (B2 p. 471) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · "Lord of the constellation" = the node's star lord, which must itself signify 2/6/10. Profession group 2-6-10 as in KP2-110.
- **KP2-058** · ★ **A node as indicator of marriage.** If the lord of 2, 7 or 11, or a planet in 2, 7 or 11, aspects Rahu or Kethu, the marriage is celebrated in Rahu's sub period, provided the node's star lord is also a significator. · (B2 p. 471) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Marriage group 2-7-11 (KP2-104). B4 uses the same group throughout (B4 p. 179).
- **KP2-059** · ★ **A node as giver of children.** Rahu or Kethu in 2, 5 or 11 with good aspects gives children in its periods and sub periods "in any of the following stars": Arudra (Mercury and Rahu periods), Swathi (Venus and Rahu), Aswini (Kethu and Mars), Makam (Sun and Kethu), Moolam (Jupiter and Kethu). · (B2 p. 471) · UNCLEAR · COMPUTABLE · B2 does not say what must be in the star: presumably the Moon at the child's birth, or the child's birth star (compare KP2-220, p. 63). The pattern is regular: each star is a node star inside a sign whose lord is the other period planet (Arudra = Rahu star in Gemini, a Mercury sign; Swathi = Rahu star in Libra, a Venus sign; Aswini = Kethu star in Aries, a Mars sign; Makam = Kethu star in Leo, Sun's sign; Moolam = Kethu star in Sagittarius, a Jupiter sign). Ask the user before using.
- **KP2-060** · **Node rules quoted from Uttara Kalamrita (Kh. VI sl. 14ff).** (a) A node in 5 or 9 conjoined/aspected by the lord of 2 or 7 causes death in its periods, even though the lords of 5/9 give rajayoga. (b) A node in 2 or 7 conjoined/aspected by the lords of 5 or 9 gives no death; it increases wealth, health and longevity. A node in 2 or 7 connected with the lords of 2/7 causes death. (c) A node in a dual sign (Ge, Vi, Sg, Pi) conjoined with the lord of 1, 4, 5, 7, 9 or 10 brings wealth and influence in its periods. With the lords of 6, 8 or 12 it brings little benefit and possible loss of an elder relative. (d) A node in 6, 8 or 12 connected with any of those three lords brings suffering in its dasa; with the lords of 2 or 7 it affects longevity. With kendra/kona lords it gives good results in that lord's sub period, but disease, accident, suicide or drowning in the sub period of the lord of 6/8/12. (e) A node in 1, 3, 4, 7, 9 or 10 with rajayoga planets conjoined or aspecting gives happiness, wealth, children and conveyance in its periods. (f) Nodes in evil houses with kendra/kona lords "cannot produce beneficial results" (others disagree; Kalidasa leaves it open). · (B2 pp. 461–463) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Krishnamurti uses (a)/(b) to infer the agent principle KP2-050. The rest is traditional. Kalidasa's ranking: Rahu good in 4, 7, 9, 10, 11; Kethu good in 3 (p. 464).

## 4. Health and disease

- **KP2-061** · **Acute, fatal and chronic illness: 6, 8, 12.** The 6th and its planets give ordinary, acute illness, its duration and recovery. The 8th gives the fatal nature of a disease and anxiety to life. The 12th gives chronic disease that needs confinement to bed. Discharge from hospital = 11th (12th from 12th, 6th from 6th), which also means recovery and victory over enemies. · (B2 pp. 299, 337, 351) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Health house group 6 / 8 / 12, with 11 for recovery. Agrees with B3/B6 (1-5-11 for cure appears only in later books).
- **KP2-062** · ★ **Health breaks down only in the periods of 6th-house significators.** "The health will break down only when a person runs the periods or sub periods of planets occupying the sixth house, or the stars of these occupants, or during the periods of planets in conjunction with the lord of the sixth house." · (B2 p. 300) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Significators of the 6th: occupants (app level A/B), planets in the occupants' stars (the app's "tenants"), and planets conjoined with the 6th lord (new: a conjunction-with-lord level). Compute the flagged periods from the dasa engine.
- **KP2-063** · ★ **How to judge a house's significators (the 10th as model).** The 10th is judged from: the strength of the 10th; its occupants and the planets in their constellations; the lord of the 10th and the planets in his constellations; the planets conjoined with the 10th lord; and the planets aspecting the 10th. · (B2 p. 317) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The fullest significator list in B2. It equals the app's PL list (occupants, tenants, owner, tenants of owner) **plus** conjunctions with the lord and aspects to the house. Krishnamurti's later strict order (B3/B6: planets in the star of occupants > occupants > planets in the star of the lord > lord) is not stated here.
- **KP2-064** · ★ **Disease significators (Scorpio lagna).** For Scorpio-born natives the 6th sign Aries signifies disease. The ailment is caused by the planets occupying Aries, the planets in the constellation of those occupants, the planets in the constellation of the lord of 6, and Mars, the lord of 6. · (B2 p. 220) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The same chain as KP2-062/063, written per lagna. Note the order: occupants, their star-tenants, the lord's star-tenants, the lord.

- **KP2-271** · **Jupiter and Venus as literal enemies.** Hindus call Jupiter (Deva guru) and Venus (Asura guru) enemies. Krishnamurti: their conjunction brings prosperity; Venus is exalted in Jupiter's Pisces; the "enmity" only means opposite ways of life (para-loka against iha-loka), plus the 6/8 lordship each gives the other's lagnas (KP2-014). · (B2 pp. 268, 408–409) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION ·
- **KP2-272** · **Ownership and exaltation of the nodes.** Kalidasa: Rahu exalted in Taurus, debilitated in Scorpio (Kethu the reverse); Rahu owns Aquarius, Kethu Scorpio; moolatrikona Rahu Gemini, Kethu Virgo; enemies Leo and Cancer; friends Libra and Capricorn; neutral Ar, Sg, Pi. Sanketanidhi: Rahu exalted in Gemini, owns Virgo; Kethu exalted in Sagittarius, owns Pisces. Others: Rahu exalted in Scorpio, Kethu in Aquarius. "There is only confusion and contradiction… Better leave it." "For prediction, one can offer correct results without taking into consideration their ownership, exaltation etc." · (B2 pp. 461, 463–464) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION · The app correctly gives the nodes no ownership. Kalidasa's line "Rahu and Kethu are very strong when exalted" (p. 464) falls with this.
- **KP2-273** · **Saturn = Brahma.** Some say Saturn represents Brahma. "I disagree… the conception of Saturn representing Lord Siva-Rudra is more appealing." · (B2 p. 440) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION ·
- **KP2-274** · **"Karaka bhava nasaya" for Saturn.** The oft-quoted rule that a karaka in its own house destroys that house "does not apply to Saturn": Saturn in the 8th gives long life. · (B2 p. 444) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION · Partial: the rule is rejected only for Saturn in the 8th.
- **KP2-275** · **Western node doctrine.** The West holds Rahu benefic and Kethu malefic, and does not use the nodes for prediction. Krishnamurti treats both as agents (KP2-050). · (B2 p. 459) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION ·
- **KP2-276** · **Transits judged only from the natal Sun (West) or the natal Moon (Hindu gochara).** "Both the methods will be found to be incomplete and therefore the percentage of failures is bound to be large." Add the ascendant and Fortuna. · (B2 pp. 83–84) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION · B5 develops the KP transit method.
- **KP2-277** · **Jataka Chandrika: lords of the 8th that do not turn malefic.** Venus (for Libra) and the Sun (for Capricorn) are said not to become malefic by owning the 8th. Krishnamurti restricts this to longevity only: as 8th lord, Venus still throws obstacles in all other matters, and the Sun's periods still bring difficulties. · (B2 pp. 215, 245) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION · Partial rejection.
- **KP2-278** · **Uranus = Rahu, Kethu or Indra.** "A few beginners… mistake Uranus as either Rahu or Kethu and a few call it 'Indra' without any authority." · (B2 p. 479) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION ·
- **KP2-279** · **Mercury loses its power when combust (within 17°).** Reported as disputed. Krishnamurti cites great men with Mercury within 3° of the Sun who rose in Mercury–Sun periods, which implies he does not accept the loss. · (B2 p. 400) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION · Soft rejection, by counter-example only.
