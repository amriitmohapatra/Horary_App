## A. KP method as this book applies it

### A1. Houses, ownership, occupancy

- **KP4-001** · **House = arc from cusp to next cusp.** "Second house means the arc commencing from second cusp and ending by the third cusp"; do not take half before and half after the cusp as tradition does. · (B4 p. 121; p. 192) · CLEAR · EXISTS · Placidus cusps, Lagna = start of house 1, as the app already does.
- **KP4-002** · **Occupancy is decided strictly by cusp longitude.** Mercury Vi 10°31' with XI cusp Vi 11°32' falls in the 10th; Mars Li 2°45' with XII cusp Li 10°32' falls in the 11th. · (B4 p. 151) · VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE (Fig. B4.35) · EXISTS · Compare KP4-003.
- **KP4-003** · **Planet just before a cusp counted in the next house (one instance).** In the 1939 chart, Rahu at Li 13°09' is called the occupant of the 11th although the XI cusp is Li 15°38'42" (2°29' later). The 2nd-house occupant table also lists Sun Ge 0°38' only as a tenant of the 2nd occupant's star. · (B4 p. 112, table p. 114) · UNCLEAR · COMPUTABLE (if an orb is adopted) · Contradicts KP4-002. The text gives no orb. It reads like an unstated "planet within a few degrees before the cusp joins the next bhava" convention. Do not implement until other Readers state an orb.
- **KP4-004** · **House lord = lord of the sign on the cusp.** This holds even when most of the house lies in the next sign: the 7th runs from Vi 20°25' to Li 25°43', yet "the lord of the seventh Bhava is that planet which owns the sign where the 7th cusp falls" (Mercury). · (B4 p. 148) · CLEAR · EXISTS · Matches PL's "owner".

### A2. Significators and their strength

- **KP4-005** · **Significator order (5 levels).** (1) planets in the star of the occupants of the house (strongest); (2) the occupants; (3) planets in the star of the lord of the house; (4) the lord; (5) planets conjoined with or aspecting those significators, and planets aspecting the house. · (B4 p. 114; repeated p. 122, p. 131 (a)–(f), p. 192, p. 205, p. 214) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The app has A–D unordered. Required change: rank them 1 > 2 > 3 > 4 (= PL tenants-of-occupants > occupants > tenants-of-owner > owner). p. 131 splits level 5 into "(e) conjoined" and "(f) aspected by them". Same order in B6.
- **KP4-006** · **Principle behind the order.** "Occupants are stronger than owners. Constellations are stronger than signs" (p. 122); "Occupant of the constellation of a planet is stronger than the occupant. Occupant of a house is stronger than the lord" (p. 192). · (B4 p. 122, p. 192) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE · Wording for the KP tab's significator panel.
- **KP4-007** · **A significator with no tenants in its stars is strong in its own right.** "No planet is in Mars star. Hence Mars is a significator" (strongest significator of the 2nd), and similar statements. When a planet's stars are empty it carries the house itself; when they are occupied, the tenants take precedence. · (B4 p. 149, p. 154, p. 179, p. 221) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Ranking refinement within level 3/4.
- **KP4-008** · **Every matter has its house group.** Marriage 2-7-11 (p. 74, p. 113); children 2-5-11 (p. 214); promotion 2-6-10-11 (p. 179); reunion 2-7-11 (p. 205) or 1-5-7-11 (p. 196); separation 1-6-10-12 (p. 150); return from abroad 3-9-11 (p. 211); higher-education success 9-11 (p. 212); kidnapping 3-6-7-9 / 1-3-9-12 (p. 202); second conception 2-5-7-11 (p. 246); delivery 2-5-11 (p. 238). · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Each is detailed in its own section below. B6 uses the same groups.
- **KP4-009** · **Elimination by the significators' sub lords (1-6-10).** Of the significators of 2-7-11, keep those in the sub of a planet connected with 2, 7, 11. Reject those in the sub of planets in, or lords of, 1, 6, 10, "as these are the 12th house to the houses 2, 7 and 11". p. 122 words it: "if the sub is lord of 1, 6, 10 and is the significator of 12 houses [sic], marriage cannot take place". · (B4 p. 122, p. 154) · CLEAR (p. 154) / UNCLEAR (p. 122 wording) · COMPUTABLE · The 1-6-10 denial group agrees with B3 p. 444 (sub lord of the 7th signifying 1, 6, 10 or 12 → no marriage with that party). Contrast KP4-122 (4-6-10).
- **KP4-010** · **Elimination by the sub lords of the house lords.** "To select the strongest significators, reject the planets that are not in the sub of lords of 2, 5 and 11." · (B4 p. 239) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Children case; the same idea as KP4-009, phrased positively.
- **KP4-011** · **Elimination by stars of 6-8-12 lords.** Significators that "do not occupy the star or sub of planets owning houses 6, 8 or 12" are the strongest. · (B4 p. 129) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · From a contributor's (Western-flavoured) chapter.
- **KP4-012** · **Star lord gives the matter, sub gives the net result.** "A planet offers the results of the lord of the constellation in which it is posited. The affairs pertaining to the house over which the lord of the constellation holds lordship will be experienced by the native predominantly. The sub will indicate the net result." · (B4 p. 206) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Core KP; also B3 "Role of Sub".
- **KP4-013** · **Marriage comes from the star lord, separation from the sub.** One DBA can both marry and separate: "Marriage comes because of the lord of the constellation. Separation comes due to the sub occupied by the planet." · (B4 p. 199) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP4-014** · **Same star, different sub: opposite outcomes.** Sun and Moon both sit in Magha (Ketu = agent of the 11th lord). If a planet in the star of an 11th-house significator is in the sub of a planet connected with 6, 10 or 12 → separation or bereavement. If the sub is a significator of 2, 7 or 11 → wedding. Here Sun in Saturn sub (signifies 2) gave the marriage and Moon in Venus sub (L12 and L7) gave the wife's death. · (B4 p. 184) · VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE (Fig. B4.42) · COMPUTABLE · Chapter subtitle: "Few planets may be in the same star: note how the sub indicates the truth".
- **KP4-015** · **A planet promising good and bad gives both.** "If a planet has to offer a desirable as well as an undesirable result, it will offer both. One result does not cancel the other." Rahu in the 2nd (marriage) sits in the star of the 10th lord, and 10 = 12th from 11 (breaking the tie). · (B4 p. 184) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE
- **KP4-016** · **A karaka (chief governor) counts mainly when it is a significator.** Venus governs marriage, "more especially if it happens to be a significator of the houses to be judged". Venus promising marriage in a chart does so "not ... because it is the Chief Governor ... but because it is in the constellation of Saturn, occupant of second house". Jupiter is the karaka for children and is added to the 2-5-11 significators. · (B4 p. 113, p. 120, p. 214) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE

### A3. Nodes

- **KP4-017** · **What a node represents (natal/horary).** "The nodes ... reflect the results of the planets with which they are in conjunction or aspect. When not so conjoined or aspected, they offer the effects of the planet ruling the sign they are in." p. 125: conjoined planet first, then sign lord ("Kethu is Sani's agent and Rahu deputises for Moon"). Rahu in Virgo "represents Mercury by occupation" (p. 128). · (B4 p. 112, p. 125, p. 128) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The app has no node agent rule. Order: conjunction > aspect > sign lord. Contrast KP4-136, which for the 7th sub lord ends with the **star lord** instead of the sign lord.
- **KP4-018** · **A node is stronger than the lord whose sign it occupies.** "Rahu, the node, is stronger than Mars." When a significator's sign holds a node, prefer the node and the planets in the node's stars. Example: Moon is in the 7th but Rahu is in Cancer, so Rahu and its tenants are preferred. · (B4 p. 152, p. 179–180, p. 219, p. 244 "Nodes are powerful than planets") · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP4-019** · **A node "governs" its sign lord and its star lord.** Rahu in Saturn's sign and Jupiter's star governs Saturn and Jupiter; Ketu in Leo in Venus's star governs Sun and Venus. "Therefore reject Saturn, Jupiter, Sun and Venus and select Rahu and Kethu." · (B4 p. 239, p. 243–244) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Ceylon contributors (ch 68–69). Stronger than KP4-018: the governed planets are dropped.
- **KP4-020** · **A node in a sign of a two-sign planet stands for that planet in both signs.** "if either of the nodes were to occupy either of the signs belonging to a planet, then the node will give the results of the lord of both the signs". It is used to put Rahu in place of Jupiter among the ruling planets. · (B4 p. 212) · UNCLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The example says "Rahu in Meena", but Rahu in that chart (Fig. B4.50) is in Leo; the reasoning as printed does not fit the chart.
- **KP4-021** · **A node in the 2nd represents the 2nd lord.** Rahu in the 2nd bhava (Sagittarius) "is to represent Guru who is lord of 2 and 5". · (B4 p. 183–184) · VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE (Fig. B4.42) · COMPUTABLE · Application of KP4-017 (sign lord).

### A4. Ruling planets (RP)

- **KP4-022** · **Ruling planets at the moment of judgment.** The lord of the day, the lord of the Moon's sign, the lord of the Moon's star (and the Moon's sub), the lord of the rising sign, and the lords of the rising star and sub. Of the significators, the ones that are also RP are selected; nodes count when they represent an RP. · (B4 p. 115, p. 123, p. 129, p. 133, p. 150, p. 152, p. 155, p. 177–178, p. 193, p. 208, p. 212, p. 244) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The app has none. p. 177: the RP Lagna is the sign **actually rising** at the moment of analysis (Aquarius), not the horary-number Lagna (Cancer). In horary (p. 244) the RP are listed with sign, star and sub for the Ascendant, the Moon and the day lord's own position.
- **KP4-023** · **RP at query = RP at birth.** The RP at the moment the chart is examined agree with the RP at birth; this is used to confirm the chart. · (B4 p. 111, p. 135 (a), p. 182–183) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Check for birth-time rectification (B3 has the procedure).
- **KP4-024** · **RP at fulfilment = RP at query.** "The ruling planets at the time of query are the ruling planets at the time of fructification of the expected result"; the dasa lords then running "will also belong to these planets". · (B4 p. 133, p. 135 (b), p. 208) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP4-025** · **The partner's birth RP = the native's marriage significators.** "When a few planets are the significators for marriage, they not only portray the time of marriage; they also describe the partner, her birth, her ruling planets". Example: the bride's Moon sign lord, Moon star lord, Lagna lord and weekday were Mercury, Moon, Jupiter and Mars = the groom's significators. See also "porutham but no proptham" (KP4-124). · (B4 p. 135, p. 84) · VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE (Fig. B4.29) · COMPUTABLE (two charts) · B3 p. 443 states the same as a certainty: the partner's birth RP = the DBA lords at marriage, or planets in their stars. **The KP replacement for porutham: show it on the Matching tab.**

### A5. Dasa logic

- **KP4-026** · **Event in the conjoined period of significators.** The event comes in the dasa–bhukti–anthra (and sookshma) of planets that are all significators of the house group. · (B4 p. 115, p. 131, p. 140, p. 143, p. 154 …) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The app has the 5-level dasa; it still needs the matter-wise significator filter.
- **KP4-027** · **Dasa lord as Prime Minister.** "No Bhukti lord can offer a result if Dasa lord does not permit." If A's dasa promises marriage and B (the bhukti lord) promises divorce, the result is only misunderstanding, not separation. "Planets form a Cabinet. The Prime Minister is the Dasanatha. The Bhuktinathas are Cabinet Ministers. The Anthranathas are Deputy Ministers." · (B4 p. 199) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP4-028** · **Bhukti lord as turncoat.** A bhukti lord that can give both benefit and harm "will confer benefit in the dasa of a planet which is beneficially connected; it will cause harm during the dasa of another planet which has to bring about evil". Rahu gave marriage in Sun's dasa and took the wife in Moon's dasa. · (B4 p. 184) · VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE (Fig. B4.42) · COMPUTABLE
- **KP4-029** · **Within a mixed dasa, each bhukti lord's sub decides.** Note the sub lord of the dasa lord. If the dasa signifies both good and bad, the bhuktis whose lords sit in favourable subs give good results and those in unfavourable subs give bad ones. · (B4 p. 198) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP4-030** · **Unoccupied house with an unoccupied owner's star.** "If a particular house is unoccupied and the constellations of the owner of the house is also unoccupied, then those planets deposited in the sub of that planet, will offer the results." · (B4 p. 199) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · A level beyond the four PL levels (tenants of the owner's **sub**). B4 p. 179 also lists sub tenants for the 7th and 11th (Mercury and Venus in Saturn's sub).
- **KP4-031** · **Aspect-timing rule.** An aspect's good or bad effect is felt during the conjoined periods of the two planets. If malefic B aspects dasa lord A, success waits until A-dasa B-bhukti (or A-bhukti B-anthra) is over; then A gives the result in the sub-period of a favourable significator. So Jupiter (L7, promising marriage) could not marry the native until the Jupiter–Saturn sub-period was over. · (B4 p. 131) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP4-032** · **Day of the event: weekday of the stronger lord.** "Whenever one wants to find out the day of an event in any Bhukti or anthra, one is to note whether the lord of the Bhukti and Anthra are strong or the lord of the constellation. The Lord of the constellation, if stronger, will give the results only on its day." Weekdays are also predicted from the RP (Thursday, Saturday/Mars day, Monday). · (B4 p. 223; p. 126, p. 155, p. 208, p. 245) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP4-033** · **Dasa-sandhi rule (replacement).** (1) If the next dasa lord occupies the star of the current dasa lord, there is no major change at the junction (Mars → Rahu in Chitra: stays in the military; Jupiter → Saturn in Punarvasu: bank career continues). (2) If the next lord owns a good house, occupies a good house and a benefic's star, unafflicted → "like day-dawn", prosperity from the start. (3) Most major events come mid-dasa, not at the sandhi. · (B4 p. 263–265) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP4-034** · **Dasa sandhi as a marriage defect: rejected.** Astrologers who reject a match because both partners' dasas change together are wrong: destiny is the same for both, and the couple in Fig. B4.71 always share dasa and sandhi yet are an "enviable couple". · (B4 p. 262–266) · VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE (Fig. B4.71) · REJECTED-TRADITION
- **KP4-035** · **Eka dasa (both running the same planet's dasa): rejected.** The same planet rules different houses, occupies different houses and stars, and has different company in each chart, so its results differ, often oppositely (her jewels pledged = his bank balance rising). The good matches by the 9th/18th/27th star rule themselves produce eka dasa. · (B4 p. 266–268) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION
- **KP4-036** · **"Match a childless boy with a fertile girl": rejected.** "If the boy is denied of a child, how can the bride have?" · (B4 p. 263) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION

### A6. Saturn: delay versus denial

- **KP4-037** · **Saturn delays, does not deny, when it signifies the matter.** "Whenever Sani happens to be a significator of a particular matter, it will not deny but will cause delay." If it is connected with 2, 7, 11 by occupation, ownership or the star of an occupant → delay at every stage (fixing, celebration). "But if Sani has no connection with houses 2, and 11 but only throws its aspect, this portends denial and even disappointment." · (B4 p. 112) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP4-038** · **Saturn in the 5th delays marriage "till the very end".** From the 5th, Saturn aspects the 7th (3rd aspect), the 11th (7th aspect) and the 2nd (10th aspect). · (B4 p. 113, p. 126, p. 130) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · p. 112 says "if Sani occupies the 11th house where from it will aspect the 7th, 11th and 2nd". That is impossible from the 11th, so it is a typo for the 5th (p. 113 and p. 126 say 5th). Aspects here are sign-based Hindu aspects.
- **KP4-039** · **Saturn in 1, 3, 5, 7 or 10 from the Lagna or the Moon delays marriage.** This applies when Saturn does not own beneficial houses. · (B4 p. 76, p. 130) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Traditional but endorsed in KP examples. A benefic Saturn (L1 and L2 for Capricorn) delays but does not deny (p. 126).

### A7. Retrograde planets

- **KP4-040** · **No result while the key significator is retrograde.** Mercury, the selected significator, was retrograde until 8-10-1969: "she will never have the child before 8-10-69" (the doctors said on or before 6 October; she delivered on 24-10-1969). "Retrograding planet will not manifest result", so its signs are excluded from the Moon-transit search. · (B4 p. 238–240, p. 244) · VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE (Fig. B4.63, Fig. B4.64) · COMPUTABLE · The app has stations. The 7th lord Saturn retrograde delays the marriage (p. 139). Retrograde Mercury in the disease house → temporary; relief after it turns direct (p. 249–250). B6 p. 201: a retrograde 5th sub lord, or one in a retrograde planet's star, denies children.

### A8. Transits, progression, day and hour

- **KP4-041** · **KP transit method.** Relate each transiting planet to its own natal place, to the other natal planets and to the natal cusps by actual longitude, and note the star and sub of every transit. At the event the DBA lords transit the signs, stars and subs of one another or of the significators. · (B4 p. 116–117, p. 126, p. 141, p. 143–144, p. 180, p. 200, p. 240, p. 247) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The transit engine exists; "KP-style transit judgement" is new.
- **KP4-042** · **Sign-only Gochara and Guru-bala: rejected.** Traditional transit counts the sign from the natal Moon (Moon at Leo 29° and a planet at Virgo 2° = "2nd"). Guru-bala (Jupiter in 2, 3, 7, 9 or 11 from the Moon sign, as printed; the usual list is 2, 5, 7, 9, 11) as the condition for marriage "is wholly unreliable". Ashtama Sani (Saturn 8th from the Moon) as a bar to marriage is also rejected. · (B4 p. 116, p. 137) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION · The app shows Sade Sati. If it shows Guru-bala or Ashtama Sani for marriage, add KSK's caution. B5 (Transits) treats Ashtama Sani at length.
- **KP4-043** · **Sun-transit date method.** Take the dasa and bhukti lords. Find when the Sun transits a sign of the DL or BL **and** a star of the DL or BL (or of the significators). Then read which antara and sookshma fall then, and take the sub of the DL inside that star. Example: Sun in Revati (Mercury) in Pisces (Jupiter), in Jupiter's sub Pi 26°06'40"–27°53'20" → 8–10 April → married 9-4-1962. Tamil practice: no weddings while the Sun is in Sagittarius, Cancer or Virgo. · (B4 p. 134–135) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Similar uses: the Sun as 2nd lord to transit Venus's sign, Moon's star, Saturn/Ketu sub (p. 137); Sun in Jupiter's star, Saturn's sub (p. 152); Sun crossing natal Rahu (p. 155); Sun in Rohini pada 4 for conception (p. 221).
- **KP4-044** · **Fast-planet transit pins the day.** Mercury (antara lord) transiting the dasa lord's star (Ardra, Rahu), in its own sign (Gemini), in the bhukti lord's sub (Saturn) = Ge 10°26'40"–12°33'20" → 10-6-1966, the day of the first child's birth. · (B4 p. 219) · VERIFIED (prediction came true) · COMPUTABLE
- **KP4-045** · **Moon transit for events within a week.** "As this is a minor event that is expected to manifest within a week, consider the transit of Moon": the Moon in the sign, star, sub and sub-sub of the selected significators, e.g. Ta 6°27'03" (Venus sign, Sun star, Mercury sub, Jupiter sub-sub) → 2 a.m. 24-11-1969, birth exactly then. · (B4 p. 244–245) · VERIFIED (outcome reported) · COMPUTABLE · Needs a sub-sub lord table (the app has a sub-sub lord per point).
- **KP4-046** · **Progression supports timing.** Use secondary progression (one day = one year), read with the current DBA and the stars and subs of the progressed planets. Progressed Moon, Sun, Mercury and Venus are significant; slow planets only general. · (B4 p. 117–118) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Low priority.

### A9. Karakas in karaka houses (replacement doctrine)

- **KP4-047** · **"Karako bhava nasaya": rejected as a blanket rule.** Venus in the 7th, Sun in the 9th, Moon in the 4th, Mars in the 3rd, Jupiter in the 5th and Saturn in the 8th do not by themselves harm those matters. Counter-charts: Fig. B4.69 and Fig. B4.70. Of the 24 short-life charts in Uttara Kalamrita only one has Saturn in the 8th. · (B4 p. 255–260) · VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE (Figs. B4.69–70) · REJECTED-TRADITION
- **KP4-048** · **KP replacement: the karaka's star lord decides.** A karaka in its karaka house harms that matter only if it is in the star of a planet that is evil by lordship. With a benefic star lord, "expect only good results". The harm comes in DBA combinations involving the karaka (A-dasa B-bhukti Venus-antara and the like), not throughout life. · (B4 p. 260–262) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP4-049** · **Tenants of the karaka's stars deliver its result.** "What results the Karaka planets in Karaka signs indicate, will be offered by those planets which occupy the constellation of the Karaka planets." E.g. Venus in the 7th in an evil planet's star plus a planet in Bharani, P.Phalguni or P.Ashadha → that planet's period spoils married life. Jupiter in the 5th (Punarvasu, Visakha, P.Bhadra tenants) harms children. Mars in the 3rd (Mrigasira, Chitra, Dhanishta tenants) harms brothers. · (B4 p. 261–262) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP4-050** · **Bhava karakas listed (Jataka Parijata).** 1 Sun; 2 Jupiter; 3 Mars; 4 Moon, Mercury; 5 Jupiter; 6 Saturn, Mars; 7 Venus; 8 Saturn; 9 Sun, Jupiter; 10 Jupiter, Sun, Mercury, Saturn; 11 Jupiter; 12 Saturn. Phaladeepika: Sun-9, Moon-4, Mars-3, Jupiter-5, Venus-7, Saturn-8 cause distress. Jataka Parijata: Venus-7, Mercury-4, Jupiter-5 evil but Saturn-8 good. · (B4 p. 257–258) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE · Reference data; Hindu 4th = mother ("follow only Hindu").
- **KP4-051** · **Exaltation and raja yoga do not guarantee good results.** "the planets which are exalted need not at all give advantageous results ... Everything depends upon the sub". Rama's chart had many exalted planets and Sita was kidnapped. · (B4 p. 202) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION

### A10. Character from the Ascendant's sub lord

- **KP4-052** · **Twins differ only in the cusps, so read character from the Ascendant's sub lord.** "As long as all the planets remain in the same position ... the only factor which moves fast ... is only the cusps", so "the sub lord is taken". Aries-rising natives with the Lagna in a Moon or Venus sub are modest; with Mars's sub, militant. · (B4 p. 108, p. 251, p. 269) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Used for the partner's temperament (KP4-139) and a child's character (KP4-162).

## B. Matching (porutham) and Mars dosha: Krishnamurti versus the app

**Summary for the Matching tab.** Krishnamurti reports each traditional rule faithfully and then
says: (1) examine each chart separately first (six points); (2) dasa porutham is "useless and
meaningless" and fit only for people whose birth time is unknown; (3) where a porutham is
kept, he re-reads its meaning (rajju, rasi 6/8) or adds his own condition (25th star);
(4) Mars dosha by position alone is wrong, and any planet can cause discord through its sub
lord. The app already computes PL's kootas, so for each koota record (a) where his rule agrees
with PL, (b) where it differs, (c) his verdict.

### B1. Frame

- **KP4-060** · **Judge each chart alone first, on six points.** Before any porutham, check in each chart: (1) longevity, (2) health, (3) finance, (4) future prospects, (5) children, (6) harmony. If both promise long, healthy, happy life, the marriage is satisfactory "even if dasa porutham is lacking". If either chart denies longevity or harmony, "even if all the poruthams are excellent, how can one expect happy wedded life?" ("painting a collapsing wall"). · (B4 p. 19–21) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE · Header text for the Matching tab, with links to the individual-chart KP checks (KP4-120ff., KP4-220ff.).
- **KP4-061** · **Dasa porutham: Krishnamurti's verdict.** "According to me, it is useless and meaningless" (p. 21). "Only the Moon's position (i.e. the star and the rasi alone) is considered ... incomplete" (p. 24). "This method is useful only for those who do not know the correct time of birth but somehow remember his or her star" (p. 24). · (B4 p. 21, p. 24) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION · The quotable verdict for the tab.
- **KP4-062** · **Choosing the star from the name's first letter: useless.** Names are no longer given by the birth star; "Rama married Sita. Ravana carried away Sita." Horoscopes cast for the girl's puberty are also unreliable. · (B4 p. 19) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION
- **KP4-063** · **Use Drik positions with the KP ayanamsa.** Almanacs following different siddhantas give different stars and dasa balances. Use Drik, or Raphael's ephemeris minus the Krishnamurti ayanamsa; "any other ayanamsa, differing much, is useless and absurd". · (B4 p. 19, p. 23–24) · CLEAR · EXISTS · The app uses KP ayanamsa variants.
- **KP4-064** · **Points/marks method criticised.** Giving equal marks to all kootas and declaring "agreement" when more than five of ten agree ignores the relative weight of each. "If the special rules are applied, most of the horoscopes agree"; under one rule a pair agrees, under another it does not. · (B4 p. 48) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION · Yet p. 37 itself allows a missing yoni when more than five of the ten agree.
