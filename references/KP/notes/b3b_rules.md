## 1. General KP machinery (significators, nodes, ruling planets, cusps)

- **KP3-501** · **Significator strength order ("rule is ever universal").** For a house group, significators in descending strength: (1) planets in the stars of the *occupants* of the houses; (2) the occupants; (3) planets in the stars of the *lords* of the houses; (4) the lords; (5) planets conjoined with or aspected by the above. All of these give the matter; strength follows this order. · (B3 p. 418) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The app's A–D levels have the data but no ordering. B6-33 and B3-40 (b3a) give the same order without level 5. Applied in every worked example of this range (e.g. B3 pp. 381, 384, 389–391, 394–395, 396, 401). "Lord" = owner of the sign holding the cusp. Throughout, an empty star ("no planet in its stars") promotes the occupant/lord itself to strongest (B3 pp. 360, 389, 399).
- **KP3-502** · **Nodes outrank planets.** Of the planets and nodes in the same group of stars, the nodes are always stronger (p. 401). Of all occupants of a house, Rahu/Ketu are the strongest (p. 487). When planets are conjoined with a node, reject the planets and take the node (p. 390: "As Moon and Mercury are conjoined with Rahu, reject them and take Rahu"). · (B3 pp. 390, 401, 487) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · B6-21 and B6-32: among ruling planets a node representing the lagna lord is stronger than the lagna lord.
- **KP3-503** · **Node as agent.** A node gives the results of (in the order Krishnamurti uses): a planet conjoined with it, a planet aspecting it, the lord of the sign it occupies, and its star lord. Examples: "Rahu acts as an agent of Saturn; Sun is represented by Kethu" (conjunction, p. 381); Rahu transiting Pisces gives what Jupiter has to give (p. 391); Rahu aspected by Mars gains Mars's 6th-house results (p. 396); "Rahu, unaspected by any planet, represents lord of the sign … [and] Sun, since it is in the constellation Karthik" (p. 519). · (B3 pp. 381, 391, 396, 408, 410, 519) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Aspect here is Parashari/Western, not specified. Same doctrine in B6-21. B3 p. 487 (water-and-milk simile): a node conjoined with a planet gives that planet's results in the node's period, and the planet is weakened.
- **KP3-504** · **Vacant house.** Traditional: the owner and the planets aspecting the house give its results, modified by planets conjoined with or aspecting the owner. KP: planets in the star of the house lord give the matters of a vacant house. · (B3 p. 482) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Level 3 of KP3-501.
- **KP3-505** · **Lagna only, never the Moon sign.** Until 1965 Krishnamurti allowed the Moon sign when the lagna was afflicted. He dropped this after the twin research: always use the exact lagna, afflicted or not. · (B3 p. 445) · CLEAR · EXISTS · The app already judges from the lagna and cusps. Record as doctrine for the KP tab (no Chandra-lagna judgements).
- **KP3-506** · **Cusp sub lord decides, significators time.** All 12 cusps matter; "the sub lord of the cusp … offers correct solution of that house. The significators show the time of event." · (B3 pp. 445–446) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Cuspal sub-lord judgement is not in the app yet. Basis for KP3-558, 581, 599, 625, 635, 636–639, 651, 653, 659.
- **KP3-507** · **Conjunction = shared sign, star and sub lords.** "What results are promised by conjunction will be produced by being situated in a position where the sign lord, star lord and sub lord are the same planets." Example: Jataka Parijata's Sun+Mars+Mercury+Saturn conjunction (minister) is met by Sun in Saturn's sign, Mars's star, Mercury's sub; or Mercury in Aquarius, Mars star, Sun sub; or in Anuradha (Saturn star) in Mars sign with Sun/Mercury sub. The result comes in the period or sub-period of the occupant. The five-planet yoga (Moon, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn conjoined in a house, not a sign) is met by e.g. Venus in Jupiter sign, Mercury star, Moon sub aspected by Saturn by 3rd aspect; or Saturn in Mercury sign, Jupiter star, Moon sub aspected by Venus. · (B3 p. 404) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · A key KP translation rule: any classical conjunction yoga can be tested on one planet's sign/star/sub lords plus an aspect. Note the "3rd aspect" of Saturn is Parashari.
- **KP3-508** · **Recurring events: the moment selects the repetition.** A matter that repeats (child birth, jewels, transfer, residence change, journeys) has many significators. The significators that rule the moment of judgement (the ruling planets) show the *next* repetition. Of the RPs, the lagna lord is the strongest and its dasa or bhukti is taken first; then the star lord (or the node representing it), then the others for anthra/sookshma. · (B3 pp. 392–393) · CLEAR · HORARY · Used again for reinstatement (p. 385: "eliminate those which are not the ruling planets at the time of judgment"). B6-32/B6-33 do the same.
- **KP3-509** · **Ruling planets (definition).** At a moment: (1) lord of the weekday (the Hindu day runs sunrise to sunrise, see KP3-681); (2) lord of the star the Moon transits; (3) lord of the sign the Moon transits; (4) lord of the rising sign; (5) lord of the star rising (ascendant's star lord). A node in the sign of any RP (or conjoined with one) is added as that planet's agent. · (B3 pp. 381, 385, 393, 519) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The app has no RPs yet. B6-21 lists only items 1–4. B3 p. 385 tabulates all five. Ordering in B3 p. 517: the lagna lord at the query is stronger than the Moon's sign lord. **In number horary the RP "lagna" is the sign actually rising at the moment of judgement, not the number-derived horary lagna**: #150 has horary lagna Scorpio 6°06'40" but RPs "Lagna lord Sun, lagna constellation lord Venus" (Leo, P.Phalguni was rising at 7-30 a.m. at 28°38'N; recomputed Leo 18°) (p. 381); #145 has horary lagna Libra 29°26'40" but RP "Rising lagna Pisces, U.Bhadrapada" (recomputed Pisces 26°) (p. 385). The dasa used in horary is computed from the Moon at the moment of judgement (#145: Saturn balance 3y 0m 14d from Moon Pisces 14°32', p. 383).
- **KP3-510** · **Sensitive points from the ruling planets.** Make every sign-star-sub combination of the RPs that exists in the zodiac (e.g. RPs Venus, Jupiter, Sun → Sun sign/Venus star/Jupiter sub ≈ Leo 21°; Venus sign/Jupiter star/Sun sub ≈ Libra 29°; Jupiter sign/Venus star/Sun sub ≈ Sagittarius 16°). The matter fructifies when the strongest fruitful significator, or a luminary, transits one of these points. Choose the transiting planet as the query-lagna lord if it is linked to the matter (Sun, lagna lord, lord of 10, in sub of lord of 2 in 11). · (B3 pp. 381–382) · VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE · COMPUTABLE · Predicted 7-9-1970: Sun in Leo, Venus star, Jupiter sub (Leo 20°–21° is P.Phalguni/Venus star; Jupiter sub there ✓). A movable, fruitful sign gives quick results (p. 382).
- **KP3-511** · **Retrograde denial.** (a) Before timing, check that no RP or significator is retrograde or in the star of a retrograde planet (p. 381, "None is retrograde. None in the constellation of retrograde planet"). (b) Horary: if the sub lord of the 11th cusp is retrograde, the desire will not be fulfilled (p. 433). (c) If the sub lord of the 10th cusp is retrograde the result is negative despite favourable signs (p. 411, minister query). · (B3 pp. 381, 411, 433) · CLEAR · HORARY (b, c) / COMPUTABLE (a) · B6-33 extends (b): the 11th sub lord *or its star lord* retrograde → no promotion; and the sub lord must also signify 2/6/10/11.
- **KP3-512** · **Saturn delays.** A significator in Saturn's sign and aspected by Saturn is a "planet of delay" and is skipped when choosing the anthra (p. 390). A strong Saturn (in its own star) as significator never denies but delays (pension, p. 401). · (B3 pp. 390, 401–402) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE ·
- **KP3-513** · **Transit timing with the Sun (and others).** The date is fixed when the Sun transits the sign-star-sub of the dasa/bhukti/anthra significators (transfer 27-2-1969: Sun in Saturn sign, Rahu star, Ketu sub, p. 390); or the natal position of the anthra lord (retirement ≈ Sun over natal Mercury, p. 396); or a named star (Mars+Saturn results come when the Sun transits Anuradha or Dhanishta, p. 409, i.e. Saturn star in Mars sign / Mars star in Saturn sign). Other planets' transits through stars of significators confirm (pp. 385, 390, 402). · (B3 pp. 382, 390, 396, 402, 409) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The app has a transit engine; only the star/sub matching against significators is new.
- **KP3-514** · **Day of the event by the Moon's star.** The day is the one on which the Moon transits the star (or sign) of a significator: leave job on an Ashlesha or Hasta day; start business when the Moon transits the second half of Mrigasira (p. 360); Moon in Scorpio through Saturn's star on 11-8-70 (p. 385); order on the day Moon is in Anuradha (Saturn) (p. 402). · (B3 pp. 360, 385, 402) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE ·

## 2. How a dasa lord gives results: Krishnamurti's own rules (B3-65)

Apply to dasa lord "D"; the same logic is used for bhukti and anthra lords throughout.

- **KP3-515** · **(a) Nature of D = the manner.** The planet's nature shows *how* the result comes: gains in Jupiter dasa are lawful and legitimate; in Saturn dasa by foul means (under the table, forgery). · (B3 pp. 458–459) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE · Same idea at p. 480 (Jupiter lord of 2 in 11 lawful gains; Saturn lord of 2 in 11 fair or foul).
- **KP3-516** · **(b) Houses owned by D = the source.** The houses whose cusps fall in D's signs (1, 2 or 3 houses) show the source and the cause of the result (who brings it). · (B3 p. 459) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Ownership = cusp-sign rule (as the app's level D).
- **KP3-517** · **(c) Star lord's houses = what D predominantly gives.** "The dasa-lord 'D' will predominantly offer those results indicated by the houses owned by the lord of the constellation in which the dasa lord was posited at birth." Leo lagna, Taurus 10th: planets in Krittika (Sun, lagna lord) → health, victory or defeat by the sub; in Rohini (Moon, lord of 12) → changes, loss, repayment, investment, pilgrimage, hospital or prison; in Mrigasira (Mars, lord 4 and 9) → higher/professional studies; in Ashlesha (Mercury, lord 2 and 11) even in the 12th → profit, marriage, child, friends. Mercury exalted for Leo lagna but in Hasta (Moon's star) gives Rohini-type (12th) results: a share broker lost everything in Mercury dasa (Mercury Virgo 10°36'). · (B3 p. 459) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · B3 p. 482 repeats it: "As per K.P., the nature depends mainly on the lord of the constellation". The app's level A gives houses *occupied* by the star lord; this rule needs the houses *owned* by the star lord (level C). Both levels already exist; the rule fixes their use.
- **KP3-518** · **(d) Sub lord of D decides fulfilment.** D in the sub of a benefic → no disappointment, the native realises the result; in the sub of a malefic → in its periods it harms, denies, disappoints, causes anxiety. · (B3 p. 459) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · "Benefic/malefic" here is ambiguous: natural, or by the houses signified (as in (f)-(g), where benefic/malefic is judged by the star lord's houses). The worked examples use the houses signified by the sub lord (e.g. pp. 381, 384). Treat the sub lord's significations as decisive.
- **KP3-519** · **(e) Dasa and bhukti lords both benefic + good mutual aspect → enjoyment.** · (B3 p. 460) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · "Aspects" = Western aspects (trine, sextile etc.); orbs not given here (B3 p. 495 gives annual-chart orbs).
- **KP3-520** · **(f) Benefic D with an evil aspect to another benefic.** If D is benefic (in the star of a lord of good houses) and forms an evil aspect with another benefic, then in the latter's bhukti in D's dasa everything looks favourable but nothing is enjoyed: delay, denial, disappointment. · (B3 p. 460) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE ·
- **KP3-521** · **(g) Two malefics in good aspect are dangerous.** D malefic (in the star of an evil planet) and bhukti lord malefic in good aspect: like two enemies combining to attack the native. · (B3 p. 460) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE ·
- **KP3-522** · **(h) Two malefics in adverse aspect → favourable.** D malefic receiving an adverse aspect from another malefic: the result in their joint period is favourable to the native. · (B3 p. 460) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Counter-intuitive; record as printed.
- **KP3-523** · **(i) Ignore kendra/kona/shashtashtaka counting.** "Do not be carried away by kendra, kona, shasta ashtama and so on." Rules (a)–(h) hold, provided the cusps are also included. · (B3 p. 460) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Consistent with KP3-590 (6/8 rule refuted on a chart).
- **KP3-524** · **(j) Aspects to cusps.** A cusp receiving a good aspect improves the matters of that house; the cusp indicates only that house, while a planet connected with the house adds more through its lordship, star, sub and house. · (B3 p. 460) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · This is how twin charts are separated (p. 461).

## 3. Doubts answered (B3-68)

- **KP3-525** · **Kendra is counted by sign.** Kendras are the lagna *sign* and the 4th, 7th and 10th signs from it, regardless of degree or of the bhava (cusp) positions; from Moon or another planet likewise by sign. For judging a planet as kendra lord, count only from the lagna. · (B3 pp. 470–471) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Examples: Gemini 29° lagna, Moon Sagittarius 2° = 7th kendra; Cancer 2° at Lucknow, meridian Pisces 24°, yet Aries is the 10th kendra.
- **KP3-526** · **Kendradhipatya dosha affects only health and longevity.** Natural benefics (Jupiter, Venus, waxing Moon, unafflicted Mercury) owning kendras are said to turn unfavourable, worse if in 2 or 7 (marakas); natural malefics owning kendras turn good. Krishnamurti: the dosha "refers only to one's health and longevity"; it does not spoil the matters of the houses owned. Evidence: Gemini-born IAS officer prospered in Jupiter dasa (lord 7, 10); Virgo-born with Jupiter in 2 or 7, and Aquarius/Leo/Scorpio-born with Venus in 2 or 7, outlived those dasas; Aquarius-born got his first car in Venus dasa (lord 4) at 52; Scorpio-born married in Venus dasa (lord 7) a bride born in Venus's star P.Ashadha. · (B3 pp. 471–473) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION (the general dosha) / GUIDANCE (the limited form) · Contrasts with B3 p. 468 #20/#29 (Phaladeepika, rejected).
- **KP3-527** · **Lagna lord and 10th lord protect.** The lagna lord always protects health and position unless it occupies 6, 8 or 12. A lord of 8 that also owns the lagna is not malefic (Jataka Chandrika); so a kendra lord that also owns the lagna is free of the dosha. The 10th lord makes even 3/6/8/11 lords beneficial (rajayoga) and, if it owns another kendra, reduces that house's evil (Jupiter lord 7 and 10 for Gemini). Conclusion: only lords of 4 and 7 may be evil, and only for health and longevity. · (B3 pp. 472–474) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE ·
- **KP3-528** · **No one's chart changes another's fate.** A child's auspicious birth, a lucky wife, or a fertile bride cannot modify a native's results; each chart shows the same event from its own side (father's overseas trip is visible in his, his wife's and the child's charts). Contradictory readings (husband denied children, wife promised) mean wrong reading, or "Jara Putra". · (B3 pp. 475–476) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE ·
- **KP3-529** · **Source, nature and outcome of a period.** Houses owned = source (through which relatives/affairs); house occupied = gain or loss; nature of the planet and its karakatva = manner; per KP the nature of the result depends mainly on the star lord. Simile: planet = lamp, house occupied = coloured slide. Lord of 3 in 11: gain through brothers, Saturn-brother economical, Mars-brother lavish; lord of 5 in 11: gains through speculation, cinema, children. · (B3 pp. 477, 480, 482) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE ·
- **KP3-530** · **Occupant is stronger than owner** in giving a house's results; the owner is next; both are modified by aspects to the house and by planets conjoined with or aspecting the owner. Exceptions: the occupant is weaker (a) if it is in an enemy sign or debilitated; (b) if the owner is exalted and the occupant is not; (c) if the owner is in its own sign conjoined with it and the occupant is neither exalted nor vargottama. The occupant is stronger when the owner is not exalted or vargottama, when the owner is elsewhere, and whenever the occupant is exalted. Examples: Moon+Jupiter in Cancer → Jupiter gives Cancer's results more than Moon; Mars+Moon in Cancer → Moon stronger; mutual exaltation case Moon-Taurus/Jupiter-Cancer/Venus-Pisces. · (B3 pp. 478–479) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · B3-39 (b3a): "occupant of a constellation is stronger than the lord of the constellation. Constellation is stronger than the sign."
- **KP3-531** · **Planet 6, 8 or 12 from a house it owns → contrary results for that house.** A planet in a sign 6th, 8th or 12th from its own sign gives results opposite to that house's indications (the 6th from the 6th is the 11th, the 8th from 8th the 3rd, the 12th from 12th the 11th). Lagna lord in 6 → ill health, in 8 → short life, in 12 → spends everything, leaves home. Scorpio lagna, Saturn (lord 3 and 4) in Virgo (11th): 3rd-house matters flourish (11 is 9th from 3), 4th-house matters suffer (11 is 8th from 4): mother ill though she sends money; Saturn lord 6 and 7 in 11 → wins a lawsuit, partner has a child (11 = 5th from 7). Virgo lagna, Saturn in Aries (8th) squares its 5th house → losses through children and games; as lord 6 in 8 (trine) it eases borrowing. Effects show only in the planet's own periods. · (B3 pp. 478, 480–484) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Uses sign counting from the owned sign. Example chart b3b-E15.
- **KP3-532** · **A planet owning a good and a bad house gives both, through the bhuktis.** The good results are not cancelled by the bad ones ("two entries in the ledger"). In its dasa, beneficial bhuktis bring the good house's results and evil bhuktis the bad house's. REJECTED: splitting the dasa into a first half (house nearer the lagna) and second half; refuted by a Leo-born whose Jupiter (lord 5, 8) dasa was blank in its first half (except Mercury bhukti) and excellent in its second (Venus, Moon, Rahu bhuktis). · (B3 p. 484) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE (both-results) / REJECTED-TRADITION (half split) ·
- **KP3-533** · **Malefics in 4, 6, 8, 12 from any house destroy its indications**; planets in other positions improve them. · (B3 p. 484) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Stated as a general rule; position counted from the house (sign or bhava not specified).
- **KP3-534** · **Rajayoga is conditional.** Before predicting a rajayoga check: (1) birth circumstances (scale of result); (2) whether the yoga planets' dasas fall in the lifetime; (3) planets in 6, 8, 12 from the yoga planet (they destroy the yoga in their bhuktis and give nothing in their own dasas; vipareeta rajayoga exempt); (4) the yoga planet conjoined with a node or with lords of 6, 8, 12; (5) a node in a sign owned by the yoga planet; (6) longevity to reach the joint periods; (7) the yoga planet in the star or sub of lords of 6, 8, 12. · (B3 pp. 485–488) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · App has PL's 1001 yogas; this is a KP filter on top of them.
- **KP3-535** · **A two-planet yoga works only in their joint periods.** Example: Aries lagna, Moon+Mars in Aquarius (Chandra-Mangala yoga). If Moon is at Aquarius 1°, Mars dasa is at most 3½ years at birth and Moon dasa comes only after 96¼ years; if Moon is at Aquarius 30°, Jupiter dasa runs first and Moon dasa begins at 69. Either way the native does not become a rich engineer through this yoga. · (B3 p. 488) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Direct test: do the dasa/bhukti periods of both yoga planets fall within, say, 0–80 years?
- **KP3-536** · **Nodes and rajayoga.** Rahu/Ketu conjoined with a yoga planet dilute it (water in milk); a node in a sign owned by the yoga planet takes over: the results come mostly in the node's period and in periods of planets in the node's star, not satisfactorily in the planet's own. Lords of 6, 8, 12 conjoined with yoga planets rob the results. · (B3 pp. 487–488) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE ·
- **KP3-537** · **Swa-bhukti forecasting.** Subdivide the first (own) bhukti of a dasa into its nine antaras. Whatever happened in antara X of the swa-bhukti repeats, on a larger scale, in bhukti X of the same dasa (promotion at the end of Saturn-Saturn-Mercury → promotion at the end of Saturn-Mercury; anxiety about a relative in Saturn-Saturn-Ketu → same relative in Saturn-Ketu; wedding in the Venus antara → happy married life in Venus bhukti; land bought in Mars antara → house built in Mars bhukti; eczema in Rahu antara → same in Rahu bhukti; child in Jupiter antara → child fortunate in Jupiter bhukti). Rejected: the claim that a good swa-bhukti makes the other eight bhuktis bad. · (B3 pp. 489–491) · CLEAR (dates recomputed and match) · COMPUTABLE · Mechanical mapping antara(dasa, own bhukti, X) → bhukti(dasa, X). Example dasa table b3b-E16 (Saturn dasa from 10-3-1956, 365.25-day years). Same applies within a bhukti: its good antaras forecast the corresponding bhuktis (p. 492).
- **KP3-538** · **Saturn transits are not evil by themselves.** Saturn dasa-Saturn bhukti with Saturn transiting the 8th, ardhashtama (4th) or sade sati is not to be judged evil without the other factors. · (B3 p. 492) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE · App computes Sade Sati; attach this caveat.
- **KP3-539** · **Transit and progression only confirm the dasa.** During a long dasa the transits and progressed aspects show separate results but never contradict the dasa reading. · (B3 pp. 491, 494, 500) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE ·
- **KP3-540** · **Astrologer's chart: Mercury.** Good astrologers have a strong, favourable Mercury; Moon-natives without Mercury's aspect only reproduce memorised slokas (Moon = mirror, Mercury = lens). · (B3 p. 492) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE ·

## 4. Phaladeepika dasa rules quoted and rejected (B3-66, B3-67)

Krishnamurti introduces them as "general. But K.P. does not advocate these, as they fail" (B3 p. 461).
Recorded so the app never shows them as KP doctrine.

- **KP3-541** · **Dasa of strong house lords 1–12.** Lagna lord: prosperity, health, fame; 2nd: marriage, daughters, speech, wealth; 3rd: help from brothers, courage; 4th: relatives, land, house, vehicles; 5th: children, royal favour; 6th: victory over enemies, health; 7th: clothes, ornaments, marriage, journeys; 8th (powerful): prestige, debts cleared; 9th: fortune, family happiness, piety; 10th: completion of good acts, honourable livelihood; 11th: wealth, friends; 12th: spending on virtuous acts. · (B3 pp. 461–463) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION · Chapter title mentions retrogression, own sign, exaltation and friend's camp, but the text gives only these 12 items.
- **KP3-542** · **Dasa of weak or 6-8-12-placed house lords 1–12.** Lagna lord in 6/8/12: recluse, affliction, change of place for the worse; lord 2: harsh speech, eye disease, waste; lord 3: harm to brothers; lord 4: mother ill, sale of property; lord 5: danger to child, stomach disease; lord 6: robbers, imprisonment; lord 7: away from wife, disease of private parts; lord 8: anguish, poverty; lord 9: wrath of deity, harm to father; lord 10: fruitless work, lives abroad; lord 11: bad news, ear trouble; lord 12: decline "like the waning Moon". · (B3 pp. 463–464) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION ·
- **KP3-543** · **Strength-state proportions and bhukti rules.** Vargottama planet does good; eclipsed or debilitated mixed; lords of 6, 8, 12 bad (§3.1). In a malefic's dasa, bhuktis of lords of the 3rd, 5th, 7th stars from the birth star and of lords of 1 and 8 bring robbers and enemies (§3.2). The 4th dasa of Saturn, 6th of Jupiter, 5th of Mars or Rahu, a planet at 30° of a sign, and lords of 6/8/12 are harmful (§3.3). Mars exalted in Capricorn as 10th/11th, Venus exalted in Pisces as 10th/11th/12th: prosperity (§3.4, 3.5). Debilitated or 6-8-12 benefics: inauspicious, can be warded off by shanti; malefics there: inescapable (§3.6). Bhukti of an enemy of the dasa or lagna lord: enemies, change of dwelling (§3.7). Bhukti results = bhava of the bhukti lord counted from the dasa lord (§3.8). Auspicious results in exaltation/moolatrikona/own/friend/enemy/debilitation = full, ¾, ½, ¼, very little, nil; inauspicious results reversed; eclipsed = debilitated (§3.9). Arohini/Avarohi/Madhya/Athama periods (§3.34). · (B3 pp. 464–466, 470) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION · The brief asked for "results of dasas by planet state (retrograde, own sign, exaltation, friend's camp, inimical, debilitation, eclipse, 6-8-12)": in B3 these exist *only* as this rejected Phaladeepika list. Krishnamurti's own replacement is KP3-515…524 (results by star lord and sub lord, not by sign dignity). Retrograde is never given a dasa rule here. B6-22 and B6-23 cover retrograde and eclipsed planets in horary.

## 17. Rectification of birth time (B3-74)

- **KP3-672** · **Vighati 4/9 rule (traditional).** Convert the interval from sunrise to birth into vighatis (1 vighati = 24 s; 1 hour = 150 vighatis). Multiply by 4 and divide by 9: the remainder r (0 counts as 9) is the birth star counted from Aswini, Magha or Mula (r-th of each group). If the actual birth star does not match, move the time by single vighatis until it does; matching times recur every 9 vighatis (3 min 36 s), e.g. 278, 287, 296, 305, 314, 323, 332, 341 for remainder 5. · (B3 pp. 507–508) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Krishnamurti uses it only to fine-tune the vighati *after* the KP method fixes the sub-sub (KP3-679). B6-21 gives it as "traditional" with the note that a 1-hour window yields 16 candidate times.
- **KP3-673** · **Mandi rule (traditional).** Lagna in 1, 5, 7 or 9 from Mandi (alternatives: Moon or lagna, rasi or navamsa). Criticised: gives the sign only (30°), or 3°20' by navamsa; contradicts the same authors' results of Mandi in each of the 12 houses. · (B3 pp. 508–509) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION ·
- **KP3-674** · **Sex by vighatis (Uttara Kalamrita).** Vighatis from sunrise divided by 225: remainder 0–15 male, 15–45 female, 45–90 male, 90–150 female, 150–225 male. For a waxing Moon at birth, the epoch ascendant = natal Moon's place, and the epoch day is when the Moon (about 9 months earlier) transits the natal ascendant degree; the epoch Moon then gives the corrected birth ascendant. · (B3 pp. 513–515) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION ·

- **KP3-678** · **Procedure (step by step).** (1) Note the RPs at the moment the question is answered (KP3-509, with node agents). (2) Among the candidate ascendant positions (the doubtful window on the birth day), keep the sign whose lord is an RP; within it, the star whose lord is an RP; within it, the sub whose lord is an RP; then the sub-sub whose lord is an RP. (3) Planets that are not RPs eliminate positions (e.g. Taurus/Gemini doubt with RPs Mercury, Jupiter, Moon, Mars: Venus and Saturn are absent, so not Venus sign/Mars star/Saturn sub; Mercury sign, Mars star, Mercury sub agree).
