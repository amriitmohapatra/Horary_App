Reader: agent b3a. Scope: vault notes `B3-00` … `B3-40` (PDF pp. 1–360). Chapters B3-41 onward
belong to agent b3b. Rule IDs in `b3a_rules.md` are `KP3-001` … `KP3-219`; worked charts are
in `b3a_examples.json` (`b3a-E01` … `b3a-E47`).

Verdict legend (from the brief): **EXISTS** · **NEW-COMPUTABLE** · **NEW-GUIDANCE** · **HORARY** ·
**NOT-RELEVANT**. "REJECTED-TRADITION" marks the parts where Krishnamurti quotes traditional
Hindu/Western rules only to dismiss them (catalogued as rule status in `b3a_rules.md`).

> Country", p. 331) holds only one line ("Moon Dasa balance 7 years 4 months 20 days"). That

> transcribed text does not state a 9th-house job-change rule. Cite by page, not by the
> vault chapter title.
