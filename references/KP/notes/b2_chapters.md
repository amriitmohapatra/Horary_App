# B2 "Fundamental Principles of Astrology", all 50 chapters: relevance verdicts (b2)

Reader: agent b2. Scope: vault notes `B2-00` … `B2-49` (PDF pp. 1–501). Rule IDs in
`b2_rules.md` are `KP2-001` … `KP2-279`. The few worked cases are in `b2_examples.json`
(`b2-E01` … `b2-E15`).

Verdict legend (from the brief): **EXISTS** · **NEW-COMPUTABLE** · **NEW-GUIDANCE** ·
**HORARY** · **NOT-RELEVANT**. "REJECTED-TRADITION" marks the places where Krishnamurti
quotes a traditional or Western rule only to dismiss it. These are recorded as a rule status
in `b2_rules.md`.

**What kind of book this is.** B2 is the meaning dictionary of the KP Readers. About 70 % of
it describes what each sign (B2-14…26), each house (B2-27…38) and each planet (B2-39…49)
signifies. It lists traits, body parts, diseases, professions, places, products, countries,
lucky days and numbers, and colours. Most of this is traditional or Western material, written
in sign-ascendant terms ("people born in Leo…"). Krishnamurti's own stellar doctrine shows up
in short passages, flagged below with ★. Those passages are worth more than their length.
The main ones: a planet gives the results of its star lord (B2 p. 339, 441). Health breaks
down only in the periods of the 6th-house significators (p. 300). Nodes act as agents of
their conjunct or aspecting planet and of their sign, star and sub lords (pp. 461–472).
Badhaka and maraka rules apply per lagna (pp. 220, 232, 244, 259, 331). The matter-wise house
groups are 2-7-11 for marriage, 2-5-11 for children, 2-6-10 for profession, and 6 / 8 / 12 for
health. B2 has almost no worked charts: there is one figure (the cover) and no chart tables.
Its examples are anecdotes with partial positions.

**Page caveat.** Cite by PDF page, as the vault does. The printed page numbers on the
contents pages (p. 3–4: "Aries 117 … Fortuna 488") belong to an older edition and run
about 9–13 pages lower.

## What is NOT in B2 (for whoever builds the meaning tables)

- **No nakshatra-by-nakshatra chapter.** Star significations appear only in passing: the
  "Objects indicated by…" lists under Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius and Pisces, the
  disease-by-star notes, the lucky-star days, and the Rahu/Kethu-in-star tables. They are
  collected in KP2-197. The full star table is in **B3-05** (36 sign×star segments) and B3-08
  (each sub).
- **No cuspal sub-lord doctrine.** The word "sub" is used about 10 times: Venus's sub span
  2°13'20" (p. 469), the Rahu example (p. 472), "in the sub of a benefic" (p. 475), and Saturn
  "in certain sub in Libra" (p. 242). Cuspal sub-lord judgement comes from B3/B4/B6.
- **No ruling planets, no rectification, no retrograde doctrine.** The only retrograde
  mentions are retrograde Mercury rising, which gives a joker or critic (pp. 150, 153), "Sun
  never retrogrades" (p. 356) and the node's retrograde motion of about 3'/day (p. 459).
  Combustion appears once, as the disputed 17° Mercury orb (p. 400).
- **Aspects are Western**, measured in degrees: conjunction, parallel, vigintile 18°,
  semi-sextile 30°, decile 36°, sextile 60°, quintile, square and trine are named; the
  semi-square 45° and semi-demi-square 22.5° are evil. Orbs are given only for Sun–Venus
  (p. 427). B2 never uses Parashari full-sight aspects.
