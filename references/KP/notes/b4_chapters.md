## What this book is

Krishnamurti's fourth Reader has three parts, written mainly as worked consultations:

1. **pp. 13–74, traditional matching.** The ten poruthams, nadi, gender, gothra, caste and
   Mars dosha. Krishnamurti reports each rule and then qualifies or rejects it. His overall verdict
   (p. 21) is that dasa porutham is "useless and meaningless" except when the birth time is
   unknown (p. 24). Each chart must first be judged on its own, on six points (pp. 19–21).
2. **pp. 74–213, marriage by KP.** Houses 2-7-11. The sub lord of the 7th cusp decides promise,
   plurality and the nature of the partner. Significators are taken in the order
   star-of-occupant > occupant > star-of-owner > owner. Timing comes from dasa–bhukti–anthra,
   ruling planets and transits. Separation, divorce, reunion, second marriage, love and the
   partner's description follow the same method. About 25 worked charts, many of them
   predictions made before the event.
3. **pp. 213–284, children and miscellany.** Houses 2-5-11 (and 7 for a second child). The 5th
   and 11th cusp sub lords decide promise. Ceylon horary cases cover delivery dates. Then come
   short chapters rejecting *karako bhava nasaya*, dasa sandhi and eka dasa, a 12-sign
   character table and gems.
