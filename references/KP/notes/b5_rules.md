# B5 — Transits (Gocharapala Nirnayam): rule catalogue

Source: K. S. Krishnamurti, *Stellar Astrological Reader No. V* (vault
`references/KP/vault/B5 Transits/`). Cite as "B5 p. N" (source-PDF page).
PDF pp. 37–186 reprint B3 pp. 16–165 (B3-04…B3-13) and are catalogued in the
B3 notes, not here.

Grades: **VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE** (checked against the page image, and where a
star/sub claim is made, recomputed from the printed longitudes with the
app's 249-sub rule) · **CLEAR** · **UNCLEAR** (reason given).
Status: **EXISTS** · **COMPUTABLE** · **GUIDANCE** · **HORARY** ·
**REJECTED-TRADITION** (rules KSK quotes only to reject).

Vocabulary used below (as B5 uses it):
- *significator of house h* = a planet that, by the order of KP5-020, carries
  house h (tenant of an occupant, occupant, tenant of the cusp-sign lord,
  cusp-sign lord, then planets conjoined/aspected). *Signifies* in B5 always
  means this house list, not natural karakatva.
- *star lord / sub lord* of a longitude = the app's 249-sub table.
- *DBA* = dasa, bhukti, anthra (B5 spells "Bhukthi", "Anthra",
  "Shookshma"); *sookshma* = 4th level.
- *zone* = a stretch of the zodiac with one (sign lord, star lord, sub lord).

## 1. Where transit sits in KP (KSK versus tradition)

- **KP5-001** · **Transit is the "second hand" of the dasa clock.** Dasa ~ the
  day, bhukti ~ the hour, anthra ~ the minute, transit ~ the second: transit
  only *times* what the running DBA already promises. "Transit and dasa must
  agree"; when KP is followed "both agree hundred per cent". · (B5 p. 26) ·
  CLEAR · GUIDANCE · Restated B5 p. 258 ("The cusps indicate whether it is
  promised or not. The significators point the time of event. Both dasa,
  bhukthi and gochara must agree.") and B4 p. 141 ("dasa bhukti effects must
  agree with the results of the transits"); B3 p. 492 (transits "will only
  confirm" the dasa reading). The app should never show a
  transit verdict without the DBA context.
- **KP5-002** · **Rejected: the sages' "dasa overrides transit".** Tradition:
  unless the dasa is good a favourable transit cannot do good, and unless the
  dasa is bad an adverse transit cannot harm; magazines add "good dasa + good
  transit = fulfilment, both bad = misery". KSK: "Here I differ … their
  method of judgement is wrong." · (B5 p. 21, p. 26) · CLEAR ·
  REJECTED-TRADITION
- **KP5-003** · **Rejected: Western Sun-sign transits.** Judging transits
  against the natal Sun alone cannot explain opposite results for people born
  on the same date (two candidates for one seat, lender/borrower, winner/loser
  at cards). · (B5 p. 9, p. 206–207) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION
- **KP5-004** · **Rejected: Moon-sign (rasi/janma-nakshatra) Gochara.** Same-
  star natives meet opposite results (Bharani plaintiff, defendant and judge;
  Pushya inspector arrests Pushya criminal; Anuradha driver runs over Anuradha
  pedestrian …); marriages on any given day occur to all 27 stars and 12
  Sun-months. Sign-counting also ignores longitude: a planet 1° from the Moon
  but across a sign line is called "2nd", one 29° away "conjoined". ·
  (B5 p. 10–12, p. 205–206, p. 280) · CLEAR · REJECTED-TRADITION
- **KP5-005** · **Lagna first, then Moon, Sun and all planets.** Transit must
  be judged against the exact Lagna, all 12 cusps and all natal planets
  ("we have to take the ascendant primarily and then only the Moon, the Sun
  and the other planets"). Lagna = health, longevity, success/failure;
  Moon = the mind's reaction (a gain can still leave the native unhappy). ·
  (B5 p. 15, p. 18, p. 21, p. 207) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE
- **KP5-006** · **What the luminaries stand for depends on Lagna.** A transit
  affecting the natal Moon (or Sun) affects the matters of the house the
  luminary *owns* from Lagna, not the native as such. Aries Lagna: Moon = 4th
  lord (mother, car, house repairs), Sun = 5th lord (children); Taurus: Moon =
  3rd (short journeys, brother); Leo: Moon = 12th (loss); Virgo: Moon = 11th
  (gain); Capricorn: Moon = 7th (wife). Virgo vs Libra Lagna two hours apart:
  Sun = 12th lord (loss, separation, hospital) vs 11th lord (profit, reunion).
  · (B5 p. 18–20, p. 206–207) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE (list the houses each
  luminary owns from the Lagna cusp signs; show beside any Moon-sign/Sun-sign
  transit item)
- **KP5-007** · **Fortuna (Pars Fortunae).** Fortuna = Lagna + Moon − Sun
  ("the position of Thithi added to Lagna"); the most sensitive point for
  transits; "Fortuna has no influence on character but is said to benefit the
  sign in which it is found"; it changes every minute and separates people
  born minutes apart. · (B5 p. 15–16) · VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE (B5.7 prints
  Fortuna Virgo 1°15′; recomputed Asc 206°15′ + Moon 292°28′ − Sun 346°58′ =
  151°45′ = Virgo 1°45′ — the printed minutes look wrong, see notes in
  examples b5-E04) · COMPUTABLE (day/night formula not distinguished in B5)
  · Cross-book: see vault Topics/Fortuna.
- **KP5-008** · **Twins: same dasa, opposite result.** Twins 1 month 6 days
  apart in dasa terms, both Rahu–Jupiter: one appointed, one rejected → the
  difference lies in the sub (and anthra / transit). · (B5 p. 22) · CLEAR ·
  GUIDANCE

## 2. Significators — as restated in B5 (prerequisite of every transit rule)

- **KP5-020** · **Order of significators of a house.** (1) planets in the
  constellation of the occupants; (2) the occupants; (3) planets in the
  constellation of the owner (lord of the cusp sign); (4) the owner; (5) "very
  weak but yet connected": planets conjoined with or aspected by the
  significators already found. · (B5 p. 189; repeated p. 248 as (a)–(e)) ·
  VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE (worked through for the Alibag chart B5.3/B5.4; every
  star placement recomputed and correct) · EXISTS (A–D listing) /
  COMPUTABLE (the ordering and level 5) · Same order B3 (Cusps and Subs) and
  B6. "Aspect" for level 5 is not defined in B5.
- **KP5-021** · **Deputy Secretary analogy.** A planet in a constellation is
  the Deputy Secretary executing what the constellation lord (the Secretary)
  indicates by occupation and ownership; if the deputy is absent (no planet in
  its stars) the Secretary acts himself. Hence "tenants are stronger than the
  owners" and planets in a planet's stars are stronger than that planet for
  its houses. · (B5 p. 189, p. 244–245, p. 293) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP5-022** · **A planet gives its own house of occupation fully only when
  no planet tenants its stars.** "Only when there is no planet in its star,
  the matters signified by the Bhava occupied by it will be given" (to a
  greater extent by the tenant otherwise). Same for ownership: Sun owning the
  11th cusp with nobody in its stars "has to indicate the 11th house matters
  also". · (B5 p. 245, p. 301–302) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Strength
  qualifier, not an exclusion — the B5 p. 190 table still lists both.
- **KP5-023** · **Lordship is by cusp sign only.** "Lord of a bhava is the
  lord of the sign where the cusp falls … it does not matter in which sign
  major part of the Bhava is." A house runs from its cusp to the next cusp
  ("exact degree and minute"). · (B5 p. 188, p. 207, p. 242) · CLEAR · EXISTS
- **KP5-024** · **Planet → houses ready reckoner.** Tabulate, for each
  planet, the houses it signifies (via KP5-020) and, for each house, its
  significators; "these workings are not needed any longer" once tabulated.
  Example (Alibag 23-12-1924): Sun 2,7,5; Moon 4,1,5,6,9; Mars 4,8,7,5,10;
  Mercury 11,4,5,3,12; Jupiter 7,5,6,9; Venus 4,11,7,8; Saturn 1,4,8,7; Rahu
  3,12,5,1,4; Kethu 8,10,5,7,4. · (B5 p. 189–190) · VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE ·
  EXISTS (planet significators A–D) · The order inside each list in the
  book is not the strength order.
- **KP5-025** · **Nature vs source of a planet's results.** *Nature* (what
  happens) = the houses it signifies through its star lord (and its own
  occupation/ownership when nobody tenants its stars); *source* (how / through
  whom) = the houses it owns and occupies. Worked "ready chart" (5-11-1926):
  Sun results 1,9,11 via sources 1,11; Moon 1,9,10 via 1,10; Mars 1 via 2,7;
  Mercury 1,12,2 via 9,12; Jupiter 7,6,4 via 3,6; Venus 9 via 1,8; Saturn 1,5
  via 1,4,5; Rahu 9,12,2 via 9,12; Kethu 1,8,6,4 via 3,6. · (B5 p. 301–304) ·
  VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE (B5.15 star/sub claims recomputed: Mars Ve/Ra, Saturn
  Sa/Sa, Moon Ra/Sa, Sun Ra/Mo — all correct) · COMPUTABLE
- **KP5-026** · **Stellar principle (discovery case).** A planet placed in the
  12th but in the star of a planet in the 11th gives gains in its dasa;
  planets in the star of planets in the 12th give loss, whatever their own
  house. (1893 chart: Kethu, Venus in 12 in stars of 11th-house planets made
  money; Sun, Moon, Mars lost it.) · (B5 p. 222–223) · CLEAR (figure B5.6
  has no degrees; the table on p. 223 gives the star lords) · COMPUTABLE
- **KP5-027** · **A in the star of B: A is stronger than B** for B's matters.
  · (B5 p. 293, p. 244–245) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE

### Nodes

- **KP5-030** · **Node = agent of its sign lord, of planets conjoined, and of
  planets aspecting it; a node is stronger than the planets it represents.**
  "Node is ever stronger than the lord of the sign, the planet or planets with
  which it is conjoined." Kethu in Taurus represents Venus and gives Venus's
  houses (2, 9 there) and also Venus's occupation (12); Kethu in Gemini
  represents Mercury (sign lord) and Jupiter (aspecting). · (B5 p. 245–247,
  p. 278, p. 259) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Agrees B3 p. 401 ("the nodes are
  always stronger than the planets"). Aspect type not defined in B5.
- **KP5-031** · **Node conjoined with a planet: node's sub-period + that
  planet's sub-sub-period give the planet's results.** Rahu conj Mercury →
  "Rahu in its subperiod and Mercury sub sub period will offer such results
  which Mercury is to give". Planets in the node's stars then carry the
  node's (and represented planets') houses: Rahu in Mars's sign, Mars in 12 →
  planets in Rahu's stars give 12th results too. · (B5 p. 246) · CLEAR ·
  COMPUTABLE
- **KP5-032** · **Weekday and hour for a node's period.** In Rahu/Kethu
  periods the result comes in the node's star or sub, through (in this order)
  the planet conjoined with the node, the lord of the node's constellation,
  the lord of its sign; the weekday is ruled by the same planet in the same
  order (Rahu in Aquarius conj Jupiter in Dhanishta → Thursday; no
  conjunction → Mars → Tuesday; if Saturn is stronger than Mars → Saturday).
  "Whenever Rahu rules the dasa or Bhukthi, the matter will materialise
  during Rahu-Kalam" (Tamil Nadu). · (B5 p. 197) · CLEAR (Rahu-Kalam table
  not given in B5) · COMPUTABLE (needs a Rahu-Kalam definition — check
  whether the app's Panchanga has it)

## 3. KP transit judgement — the core doctrine

- **KP5-040** · **Every star is a "room" holding its lord's houses.** The 27
  stars fall into 9 groups of three (lord Kethu: stars 1,10,19 = 0°–13°20′,
  120°–133°20′, 240°–253°20′; Venus 2,11,20; Sun 3,12,21; Moon 4,13,22; Mars
  5,14,23; Rahu 6,15,24; Jupiter 7,16,25; Saturn 8,17,26; Mercury 9,18,27).
  Write into each star the houses its lord signifies; any planet passing
  through that star "offers" those houses. "27 rooms with nine different
  varieties of provisions … the salesman in room 1, 10 or 19 can give only
  rice." · (B5 p. 190–194, p. 207–209) · VERIFIED-ON-FIGURE (B5.5 ready
  reckoner) · COMPUTABLE (star → star lord → that planet's signified houses;
  a direct projection of the existing significator table) · Star 9 printed
  "106°43′" on p. 194 (text) — read 106°40′.
- **KP5-041** · **Star lord = what; transiting planet = source; sub lord =
  whether.** "The source is indicated by the planet transiting and what will
  happen is shown by the lord of the constellation, whether it will
  materialise or not, whether it is favourable or not, is indicated by the
  sublord according to the houses signified by the sublord." The source is
  read from the transiting planet's own occupation, ownership and nature
  (3rd-house significator transiting → money by M.O./cheque, brother,
  editing, brokerage; 4th → rent, yield, mother; 5th → music, cinema,
  speculation, children). · (B5 p. 191, p. 194–195, p. 208–211, p. 295,
  p. 299, p. 304, p. 307–308, p. 344) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · The single
  most repeated rule of the book. **Conflict:** B5 p. 336 (Libra section,
  ch. 34) says the reverse ("the transiting planet and the house it transits
  indicate which house matters … the lord of the star indicates the source").
  Treat p. 336 as a slip; every other passage (≥8) states the rule above.
- **KP5-042** · **Sign and house transited do not matter.** "Let a planet
  transit in any sign, or in any house. As per KP you never bother about it.
  What you should find out is the transit of the significators in the
  constellation and sub of the significators of a particular matter. This
  rule is applicable to one and all." · (B5 p. 365) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE ·
  Contrast ch. 34, where KSK still uses "lord of X transiting in the
  constellation of lord of Y and sub of lord of Z" by sign lordship for
  general (Lagna-only) predictions (KP5-090).
- **KP5-043** · **Entering the star is not the event; the sub fixes it.** A
  significator entering the star of a planet that signifies the matter does
  not deliver on the day of entry; it must reach the sub of a significator of
  the matter. Example: Jupiter (6th significator) in Poorvaphalguni (Venus
  star; Venus and Mercury signify 11) — no promotion until Jupiter touches the
  Mercury sub in Venus star, 144°00′–145°53′20″. · (B5 p. 192) · CLEAR
  (boundary arithmetic checked: Ve 133°20′–135°33′20″, Su –136°13′20″, Mo
  –137°20′, Ma –138°06′40″, Ra –140°06′40″, Ju –141°53′20″, Sa –144°00′,
  Me –145°53′20″ ✔) · COMPUTABLE
- **KP5-044** · **Sub lord signifying the opposite group reverses the
  result for that sub's duration.** 7th-house significator transiting the sub
  of an 11th significator → love affair pleasant; the same planet in the sub
  of a 6th significator → the girl goes away, separation lasts until it
  reaches a sub ruled by an 11th significator. · (B5 p. 192) · CLEAR ·
  COMPUTABLE
- **KP5-045** · **Each sub change = the next stage of the same matter.** A
  planet crossing the nine subs of one star walks the matter through stages.
  Marriage (Jupiter through a Mercury star whose lord is 2nd/11th lord):
  Mercury sub = advertisement, horoscopes received; Kethu sub = girl unwell 4
  days; Venus sub (3, 10) = boys come to see her; Sun sub = gold bought; Moon
  sub = negotiation nearer; Mars sub = marriage fixed; Rahu sub = purchases;
  Jupiter sub (Jupiter in 11) = wedding; Saturn sub = groom leaves alone to
  find accommodation. Service: interview → discouraging news (sub lord adverse
  to self-acquisition) → Mars sub, appointed → Rahu sub, idle waiting →
  Jupiter sub, joins duty. · (B5 p. 211, p. 220) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
  (sub-ingress list with each sub lord's signified houses)
- **KP5-046** · **Duration follows the speed of the transiting planet.**
  Saturn (slow) in the sub → long separation; Sun in the same star and sub →
  short; Kethu or Jupiter → longer. Jupiter stays ≈12 days in a Revati/
  Ashlesha-sized sub; Saturn weeks to months (1964: Saturn sub 5-5 to 27-7). ·
  (B5 p. 192, p. 218, p. 315) · CLEAR · EXISTS (speed) / COMPUTABLE
- **KP5-047** · **Transit over a natal planet's position.** "Whenever a
  planet transits the position occupied by Sun … 9th house matters will be
  enjoyed" — i.e. the natal planet's star/sub zone carries that natal planet's
  results; the transiting planet supplies the source. Transit conjunction of
  two planets (Mars over natal Saturn, Saturn over natal Mars): matter = the
  houses signified by the planet whose zone is entered, source = the
  transiting planet's houses; read relatives by derived houses (mother =
  4th, children = 5th, wife = 7th, father = 9th, elder brother = 11th,
  younger brother = 3rd) and "bhadhaka" lordship. · (B5 p. 301, p. 304–306)
  · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Western "Mars–Saturn conjunction is evil" applies
  only if both natal Mars and Saturn sit in the star *and* sub of occupants of
  6, 8, 12 — "which is rare" (B5 p. 306–307).
- **KP5-048** · **Several transits operate together; none cancels another.**
  A beneficial transit of a favourable planet in a favourable star and sub
  does not cancel what another planet threatens — both are experienced (gain
  by speculation and the medical bill on the same day). · (B5 p. 312–313,
  p. 332; Western rule 3 p. 24 agrees) · CLEAR · GUIDANCE
- **KP5-049** · **Derived houses for relatives in a transit reading.** One
  significator, different results to different relatives: a 2nd-house
  significator's star day gives the native money but is the 8th from the 7th
  (partner suffers), the 10th from the 5th (children promoted), the 11th from
  the 4th (mother profits), the 12th from the 3rd (younger brother loses). ·
  (B5 p. 210) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE (house h from house k = (h − k) mod 12
  + 1)
- **KP5-050** · **Retrograde passage repeats the result of the same sub.**
  When Saturn retrogrades back through subs it had crossed, "the results will
  be more or less the repetition" of the earlier passage through each sub
  (1964: Rahu sub 21-8–18-9 repeats 23-3–12-4; Moon sub 18-9–9-10 repeats
  13-3–23-3; Sun sub 9-10–25-11 repeats 8-3–13-3). · (B5 p. 318–319,
  p. 325, p. 335) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP5-051** · **Retrograde modifies strength.** Retrograde in an evil sub
  lord's portion → malefic results aggravated; retrograde in a benefic sub →
  gains assured but "much less remunerative for the same extent of pains"
  (₹70 profit per pump-set when direct, ₹10–15 when retrograde; pass but no
  rank; selected but not the best). Direct motion in a beneficial sub gives
  satisfactory gains. "The evil results during retrograde motion will be very
  severe. The severest time is the period when Saturn is stationary." ·
  (B5 p. 321, p. 334) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE (stations EXIST in the transit
  engine) · Research hint (not a rule): natives born with a planet retrograde
  may advance when the same planet is retrograde again in transit (B5 p. 290).

## 4. Timing an event: DBA ↔ transit (computable procedure)

- **KP5-060** · **Sensitive points of a conjoined period A–B–C.** For A dasa,
  B bhukti, C anthra, the matter materialises when the part of the zodiac
  "governed by these three" is transited "by them, or luminaries and Lagna".
  The governed parts are the zones whose sign lord, star lord and sub lord
  are A, B, C in any order. Example Venus–Moon–Mercury: Venus sign/Moon star/
  Mercury sub; Moon sign/Mercury star/Venus sub; Mercury sign/Moon star/Venus
  sub. Sun–Venus–Mercury: Sun sign/Venus star/Mercury sub; Venus sign/Sun
  star/Mercury sub; Mercury sign/Sun star/Venus sub. Mercury–Rahu–Jupiter:
  Mercury sign/Rahu star/Jupiter sub; Jupiter sign/Mercury star/Rahu sub. A
  repeated lord collapses the set (Venus–Mars–Venus → Mars sign/Venus star/
  Venus sub 13°20′–15°33′20″ and Venus sign/Mars star/Venus sub
  182°40′–184°53′20″). · (B5 p. 195–197) · CLEAR (boundary values checked:
  13°20′–15°33′20″ ✔, 15°33′20″–16°13′20″ ✔, 184°53′20″–185°33′20″ ✔,
  209°26′40″–210° ✔, 300°–301°53′20″ ✔, 215°26′40″–217°20′ ✔,
  237°53′20″–240° ✔, 177°53′20″–180° ✔; "[?182°40′]", "[?138°06′40″]" and
  "[?256°13′20″]" are the transcriber's doubts but agree with the sub
  table) · COMPUTABLE · Not every permutation exists (a sign contains only
  2¼ stars), so a period may have 0–6 zones; B5 lists only the ones that
  exist.
- **KP5-061** · **The two-level form (star = dasa, sub = bhukti).** KSK's
  own history: before subs he timed Mars–Kethu by Sun in Mars sign/Kethu star
  (13–26 April), Sun–Kethu by Sun in Leo/Magha (second fortnight of August),
  Jupiter–Kethu by Sun in Sagittarius/Moola (second fortnight of December);
  this failed for combinations with no such star in the sign (Mars sign has no
  Moon, Rahu, Mars stars; Venus sign no Saturn, Mercury, Kethu, Venus stars).
  Fix: "instead of taking Sign, take the Star to represent dasa, and the sub
  to indicate Bhukthi" → every dasa–bhukti pair has 3 zones 120° apart. ·
  (B5 p. 224) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE · Other books keep the Sun form for the
  month: B3 p. 31 (Mars–Kethu: Sun or Moon in Aswini, 14–27 April); B3
  p. 209 (retirement: Sun in Jupiter sign = dasa, Uttarabhadra = bhukti
  Saturn's star, Kethu sub = anthra, ~21-3-1980); B4 p. 134 ("whether Sun
  transits in the sign of either Dasanatha or Bhukthinatha and in the star of
  Dasanatha or Bhukthinatha", then the sub of the dasa lord).
- **KP5-062** · **Worked list for Jupiter–Mercury–Sun.** Sensitive points:
  (a) Jupiter sign, Mercury star, Sun sub 351°33′20″–352°13′20″; (b) Mercury
  sign, Jupiter star, Sun sub 88°46′40″–89°26′40″; (c) Jupiter star, Mercury
  sub (any sign); (d) Mercury star, Jupiter sub (any sign). In the anthra's
  "1 month and 10.8 days" Mercury and Sun "can be in any of the above 4
  sensitive points. It will be there. It must be there." Lagna marks the
  minute (of taking charge). · (B5 p. 220–221) · CLEAR (both ranges
  recomputed ✔; "1 month 10.8 days" = 16×17×6/120² × **360** days = 40.8 d;
  the following "44.8 days" is a misprint for 40.8; with 365.25-day years the

  held as the *sub* lord in (a)/(b) and (c)/(d) drop the anthra lord entirely
  — narrower than KP5-060's "any order". Implement both and label which
  form matched.
- **KP5-063** · **Which bodies must transit the points.** The ascendant (for
  the minute), the luminaries, and "the planets ruling the dasa, bhukthi and
  anthra"; Sun "is to indicate the month" (B5 p. 365); Moon the day (star day
  of the dasa/bhukti lord, then the sub); Lagna the minute. Jupiter and
  Saturn passing a favourable sub give the slow, long windows (B5 p. 218).
  "Whenever one tries to find results by transit, it is necessary to note
  primarily the transit of the Dasanatha, Bhuktinatha, etc. and then, of all
  other planets." · (B5 p. 196–197, p. 218, p. 221, p. 365; B3 p. 211) ·
  CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP5-064** · **Lagna is computed for where the native is.** "One should
  not always calculate the ascendant for the place of birth unless the native
  is permanent in his birth place." · (B5 p. 196) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
  (transit chart already EXISTS; needs a location input)
- **KP5-065** · **Moon in the sign, star and sub of the DBA lords.** "If the
  Dasa lord and Bhukthi lord are strong in the sign, then the matter
  signified by them can be had when Moon transits in the sign, star and sub
  ruled by them … whether it is chandra ashtama or not." Example relief from
  service 30-9-1965 15:30 (Mars–Mercury–Mars): Moon in Scorpio (Mars) in
  Jyeshta (Mercury); day Thursday (Jupiter = Mercury's star lord); Lagna
  Aquarius 1st degree = Mars star, Mercury sub "(a child born with Moon there

  zone recomputed: Aquarius 0°–1°53′20″ = Mars star, Mercury sub ✔) ·
  COMPUTABLE
- **KP5-066** · **Day selection with the Moon: star days of dasa and bhukti
  lords, then the sub.** Take the 3 stars of the dasa lord and 3 of the
  bhukti lord (6 star days); gain only if both lords are advantageous. Inside
  the star day choose the Moon's sub ruled by the bhukti lord / a favourable
  significator; then the sub-sub of significators of the matter; then the
  Lagna. · (B5 p. 235, p. 250–251) · CLEAR · COMPUTABLE
- **KP5-067** · **KSK's hand method for the Moon's subs (approximation).**
  Moon's time in a star (22–26 h, irregular) ÷ 120 = minutes per dasa-year;
  multiply by each lord's years in Vimshottari order starting from the star
  lord (24 h → 12 min per year; Venus sub 240 min, Sun 72 …). Worked:
  Bharani 27-4-68 19:15 → 28-4-68 22:15 (27 h, 13.5 min/yr): Ve 19:15–23:45,

### Synthesis — transit timing procedure the app can run (built only from the rules above)

Inputs: natal chart (KP cusps, 249-sub positions), the matter's favourable
house group H⁺ and opposing group H⁻ (§6), significator lists, the running
DBA (+ sookshma), a date range, the native's current location.

1. **Promise / period gate (KP5-001, KP5-066, §6).** Keep only DBA spans
   whose lords are significators of H⁺ and, where B5 says so, not of H⁻
   ("not connected with 8 and 12" for gains, etc.). B5 states that unless the
   DBA lords signify the matter, transits do nothing.
2. **Zones (KP5-060/061/062).** For the running D–B–A(–S): Z₃ = zones whose
   (sign, star, sub) lords are a permutation of (D, B, A); Z₂ = zones with
   (star, sub) ∈ {(D,B), (B,D), (D,A), (B,A), …} per KP5-061/062; Zₛ = zones
   whose star lord *and* sub lord are both significators of H⁺ (KP5-042,
   KP5-080) and whose sub lord does not signify H⁻ (KP5-044).
3. **Window per body (KP5-063).** Find the intervals when (in this order of
   priority) the dasa lord, bhukti lord, anthra lord, the Sun (month), the
   Moon (day) are inside Z₃ ∪ Z₂ (or Zₛ for "all planets" confirmation as in
   ch. 36). Jupiter/Saturn/nodes inside Zₛ give the long-range background.
   Retrograde re-entries are separate windows that repeat the result
   (KP5-050); a station inside a zone intensifies it (KP5-051).
4. **Day and minute.** Intersect: Moon in a star of D or B and in a sub of a
   H⁺ significator (KP5-065/066); then the local Lagna inside Z₃/Zₛ
   (KP5-064/068). Weekday ruled by a DBA lord, its star lord, or (nodes) per
   KP5-032 — confirmation only.
5. **Report** each qualifying window with which form matched (Z₃, Z₂, Zₛ),
   the sub lord's houses (stage of the matter, KP5-045) and the transiting
   planet's houses (source, KP5-041).
