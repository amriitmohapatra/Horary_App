How to use Kala Chakra — a friendly guide

For someone who has never used anything more than Excel. No installation. No internet needed.

Before you begin

  1. Ask someone to copy the file “Kala Chakra v1.5.html” (or the Odia edition, “Kala Chakra v1.5 (Odia).html”) onto your computer, tablet or phone — by pen-drive, WhatsApp or email. It is one single file.
  2. Double-click / double-tap it. It opens in your internet browser (Safari, Chrome, Edge — any). You do not need internet: everything happens inside the file, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.
  3. Each time it opens, a different quote of Gurudev greets you at the top.
  4. Wait two or three seconds until the small text beside the big button says “engine ready.”
The pictures below are drawings of the app, made so the arrows and numbers stay clear. Your screen will look the same, with the same words in the same places. In the Odia edition, results additionally render in Odia numerals, full Odia sign names, and Odia planet short forms in the lord chains (legend inside the Odia guide).

Step 1 — Enter the question and the moment

  1. 1Horary question — type the question exactly as it was asked (e.g. “Will I get the job?”). Below it, the querist's name.
  2. 2Date and Time — the moment the question was asked. Click the little boxes and pick, or type.
  3. 3The small “ss” box — seconds. On iPhone/iPad the clock picker has no seconds, so type them here (e.g. 42). On a computer you may ignore it if you typed seconds in the time box.
  4. 4Place — start typing (e.g. “Lap…”) and choose from the list: Lapanga, Puri (Karar Ashram), Singapore, Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, New Delhi are built in, and the latitude, longitude and UTC boxes fill themselves. For a new place, type its name and numbers once, then press “save this place for future use” — the app remembers it forever on that device.
  5. 5Ephemeris mode — leave it on “Solar Fire compatible” (it reproduces the old software exactly). “Modern” is slightly more astronomically accurate; ask Amriit before changing.
  6. 6Press the big brown Cast & Calculate button.
  7. 78Only for old charts: if you have a Solar Fire report, paste it in the right-hand box (or drop the file) and the app will use it instead of casting. Normally leave this empty.

Step 2 — Read the chart and the positions

  1. 1Your question and details appear at the top of the results.
  2. 2The East-Indian chart: every sign box shows its cusps and planets with degree and lords — star lord in green, sub lord in crimson, house number after the dash.
  3. 3The centre shows name, date, time, place and the exact latitude/longitude used.
  4. 4Buttons: Download Excel workbook (the finished chart as an Excel file) and Print / save as PDF.
  5. 5House cusps table — all 12 cusps with five lords each (Star/Sub/Sub²/Sub³/Sub⁴).
  6. 6Chart points table — the nine planets: position, five lords, house, cusp-number vector, and the final power line.
  7. 7In each power line, the orange pill marks the strongest base(s).

Step 3 — The dasha periods

  1. 1“Running now” — the five-level chain at the chart moment (Mahadasha → … → Prana).
  2. 2The pratyantar table of the current period — the running row is highlighted.
  3. 3Upcoming pratyantar changes — every change for the next 8 months.
  4. 4Upcoming prana periods — the next 12 fine periods with date and time.
At the very end of the page, the Deha dasha section goes one level finer still (sixth tier), covering the same span as the prana table.

Step 4 — Notes, and checking a real event

  1. 1Write your notes about what actually happened (e.g. “the letter arrived this day”).
  2. 2Enter the event's date and time (with the ss box for seconds if needed).
  3. 3Press “Show periods ±2 days” — the app lists every prana period from two days before to two days after the event; the one running at your event's moment is highlighted, and its name is written above the table.
  4. 4The same window appears once more at the very end of the page in Deha (sixth-tier) detail.
Everything you see can be printed: press Print / save as PDF and keep a paper copy with your notes.

If something looks wrong

  1. “engine ready.” never appears → close the file and open it again; wait five seconds.
  2. A red message under the button → it tells you exactly what is missing (usually date, time, or place numbers).
  3. The chart looks different from the old Solar Fire computer → check the place: different computers had slightly different coordinates saved. Use the app's built-in places for consistency.
